The ERR-BIN error catalogue lists system error codes generated by bin management transactions and services in Ledgerline. These codes indicate data validation failures or missing records encountered during inventory bin operations. As of 26 October 2026, the catalogue defines errors encountered across bin-related service operations.

### ERR-BIN-0001: BIN MASTER NOT FOUND
* **Message**: `BIN MASTER NOT FOUND` [^r1][^r2].
* **Cause**: The requested bin record does not exist in the master files during processing [^r1][^r2].
* **Remedy**: Verify the bin identifier and ensure the bin master record exists prior to invoking bin operations [^r1][^r2].
* **Raised by**: Raised by [[services-inv-bin|bin services]] including `bin_block` [^r1] and `bin_get` [^r2]. Detailed guidance is provided on [[err-bin-0001|ERR-BIN-0001]].
