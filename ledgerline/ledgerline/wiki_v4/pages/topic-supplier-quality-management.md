Supplier quality management governs how Northbridge Cycles handles defective vendor deliveries across its facilities. Formal quality notices provide the documentary basis for issuing debit notes, claiming rework costs, and processing vendor returns. Purchasing management also enforces order suspensions against suppliers demonstrating persistent defects across delivery batches.

## Quality notices and commercial remedies

Quality notices serve as the formal foundation for debit notes, rework claims, and vendor returns across both Manchester and Leeds [^r1].

## Vendor performance and order suspension

The purchasing manager suspends further purchase orders for a commodity if a supplier demonstrates recurring faults across multiple delivery batches [^r2]. This commodity-level suspension remains in place until corrective and preventative actions are agreed and demonstrated [^r2].
