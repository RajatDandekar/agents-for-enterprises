Battery handling and dangerous goods logistics govern the transport and receipt of lithium battery packs returned under warranty. Returned battery packs must travel strictly through certified transport channels to prevent safety hazards and regulatory breaches. Receiving staff must follow strict containment and logging protocols if non-compliant shipments arrive at Northbridge facilities.

## Transit and carrier requirements

Returned battery packs from bike shops under warranty must be shipped exclusively via approved dangerous-goods carriers and never through ordinary postal or parcel networks [^r1]. 

## Non-compliant consignments and incident protocol

If a returned battery pack arrives via standard parcel transit, the safety officer must be notified immediately and an incident logged before opening the consignment [^r2].
