Purchasing parameters in Ledgerline govern automated replenishment batch runs and material call-off timing. Accurate maintenance of vendor lead times and prompt goods receipt recording are essential to avoid late call-offs and operational expediting costs.

## Parameter maintenance and replenishment

Failing to adjust default vendor delivery lead times in Ledgerline during supplier transit disruptions causes replenishment batch runs to place call-offs too late, forcing buyers to chase consignments and arrange costly last-minute transport [^r1]. Delayed booking in of arriving pallets by warehouse staff impacts the accuracy of available stock counts used during replenishment planning [^r2].
