Stefan Berg is the production planner at Northbridge Cycles and the process owner for core production procedures [^r1][^r2]. He authors and maintains standard operating procedures governing work order releases, backflushing, and engineering change management [^r1][^r2]. Berg regularly coordinates cross-site production schedules, resolves assembly line interruptions, and represents planning across site committees [^r5][^r7][^r8].

## Responsibilities and standard operating procedures

Stefan Berg is the author and owner of [[sop-prd-001|SOP-PRD-001]] (*Work Order Release and Backflush*) [^r1]. He also serves as the owner of [[sop-prd-002|SOP-PRD-002]] (*Engineering Change (ECN)*) [^r2]. In these roles, he manages the formal administrative workflows for releasing shop orders, handling component backflushes, and implementing engineering revisions across production lines [^r1][^r2].

## Production coordination and incident management

On 10 July 2019, Berg reported repeated material shortages on the Deansgate assembly line where physical bins were empty despite Ledgerline showing stock in main stores [^r8]. To resolve the Deansgate assembly disruptions, he asked [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] to review open transfer orders with the stores supervisor and arrange an urgent physical count of missing lines [^r9].

When a drive unit fault on the assembly end-of-line test rig disrupted communication with the line controller on 4 April 2021, finished bicycles began backing up [^r10]. Berg directed assembly personnel to move finished frames into temporary holding bays until an external technician completed repairs [^r11].

On 25 April 2021, Berg identified anomalous overnight Material Requirements Planning (MRP) suggestions that conflicted with scheduled production plans [^r12]. He instructed the purchasing team to hold off on generating purchase orders, required manual cross-checks against paper stock sheets for critical components, and prohibited raising firm requisitions from the queue without prior consultation [^r12][^r13].

Ahead of the spring delivery schedule on 17 April 2025, sales management asked Berg to review outstanding retailer enquiries alongside the Leeds unit team to ensure sub-assembly runs matched incoming expectations [^r14]. His planning team was also instructed to track retailers requesting revised delivery locations or transit holds until full spring orders were packed [^r15].
