The regular weekly purchasing meeting was rescheduled to Thursday afternoons effective from late September 2025 [^r1]. The change aligns the Manchester buying office and Leeds unit support team around mid-week production schedules, supplier order books, and stock adjustments [^r2]. Today, team members must ensure system records, variance notes, and expediting lists are updated ahead of each Thursday session [^r4].

## Context and rationale

The meeting moved from its previous time slot to Thursday afternoons to give the Manchester buying office and Leeds unit support team sufficient time to review mid-week production schedules, updated supplier order books, and stock adjustments before convening [^r1][^r2].

## Meeting format and requirements

The weekly purchasing meeting follows a standard agenda reviewing pending purchase requisitions, supplier delivery performance, and supplier invoice queries recorded in the system [^r3]. All purchasing team members must update their variance notes and expediting lists on the system prior to the Thursday meeting [^r4].
