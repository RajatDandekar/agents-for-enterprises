The continuous improvement workshop held on 8 October 2025 reviewed operational inefficiencies across manufacturing and warehousing operations in Leeds and Manchester [^r1]. The session established five key operational action items targeting ERP recording delays, supplier labelling deficiencies, warranty stock isolation, work order closings, and drawing revision synchronisation [^r2][^r4][^r6][^r8][^r10]. As of 26 October 2026, these findings document cross-site workflow friction between shop-floor execution and stock accuracy in Ledgerline ERP [^r2][^r6][^r8][^r10].

## Workshop Attendance

Stefan Berg, Rhys Pryce, Emma Clarke, and Pieter Jansen attended the continuous improvement workshop on 8 October 2025 [^r1].

## Operational Findings and Actions

### Leeds Paint Booth Booking Delays

Delays in recording frame sub-assemblies into Ledgerline ERP after exiting the paint booth in Leeds led to inaccurate stock balances in Manchester, triggering duplicate replenishment requests [^r2]. Alan was assigned to investigate the physical placement of the shop-floor terminal in the Leeds paint booth and review shift handover routines for booking in completed frames [^r3].

### Inbound Goods Receiving at Manchester

Deliveries of electrical harnesses arriving in Manchester without internal purchase order references on outer packaging caused warehouse gangway obstructions and delayed incoming inspections of battery casings [^r4]. Brenda was tasked with drafting standard delivery note requirements to be issued to regular component suppliers and hauliers ahead of the manufacturing cycle [^r5].

### Warranty Returns and Rework Stock

Returned warranty motors and electronic display consoles were placed into a generic rework location in Ledgerline ERP, causing stock availability reports to overstate serviceable units [^r6]. Colin was assigned to review stock movement routines for returned warranty items to ensure quarantined stock is separated immediately from serviceable spares [^r7].

### Work Order Electronic Sign-Off

Assembly line operators delayed electronic sign-off on work orders until the end of the week, which delayed automated issuance of fasteners, clips, and motor mount brackets in Ledgerline ERP [^r8]. Diane was tasked with organising a refresher briefing for assembly line leaders focused on closing work orders at the end of each shift [^r9].

### Engineering Drawing Revisions and Component Listings

Delays in updating component listings in Ledgerline ERP following drawing revision updates resulted in the Leeds unit preparing mounting plates according to older drawings [^r10]. Graham was assigned to establish a formal sign-off step between engineering and stock control prior to releasing revised component listings for active production orders [^r11].
