Physical warehouse transfers govern the timely logging of stock movements between central storage and production locations at Northbridge Cycles. Failing to book these movements promptly distorts inventory visibility and disrupts automated purchasing runs. All stores supervisors are required to clear transfer ticket backlogs by the end of each shift to keep planning screens accurate.

## Stock Movement Booking and MRP Impact

Unbooked physical transfers between the main Manchester warehouse and sub-assembly lines lead directly to inventory count discrepancies on material requirements screens [^r1]. When physical stock moves to production without corresponding transaction entries in the system, automated routines detect an artificial deficit and trigger the generation of unnecessary purchase requisitions [^r1]. To prevent these erroneous purchase requisitions, stores supervisors must enter all physical transfer tickets into Ledgerline before the close of each working shift [^r1].
