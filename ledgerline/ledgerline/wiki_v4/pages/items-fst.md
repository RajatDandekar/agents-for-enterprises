The `FST-*` item family encompasses assembly hardware, including metric bolts, screws, and washers in zinc-plated, stainless steel, and black finishes across Northbridge Cycles' facilities. These hardware components are stocked at Manchester Stores (WH-MAN-STR) and Leeds Stores (WH-LDS-STR) to support manufacturing lines and scheduled maintenance. As of 26 October 2026, stock balances and active purchase orders reflect high-volume consumption across active production work orders [^r1][^r2][^r33][^r40].
