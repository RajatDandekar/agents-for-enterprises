ERR-BNK-0101 is an Accounts Payable validation error that prevents payments to vendors whose banking records have not been formally confirmed [^r1]. When triggered during automated processing, it halts the inclusion of affected vendor invoices in proposed disbursement batches [^r1]. Accounts payable staff must coordinate with procurement to confirm whether outstanding bank validation paperwork is pending [^r2].

## Meaning
The error indicates that a supplier's banking details are currently unverified in the system [^r1]. It operates as an automated safeguard to prevent unauthorized or unverified disbursements during batch processing [^r1].

## Cause
The error occurs when payment proposal generation job JOB-AP-030 encounters invoices for a vendor whose bank account details lack required system verification [^r1].

## How to fix
If an expected supplier invoice does not appear on screen [[screen-ap-pay-03|AP-PAY-03]], review the processing logs to determine whether ERR-BNK-0101 was triggered [^r2]. When this error is present, check with procurement to ascertain whether the supplier's banking paperwork is pending verification [^r2].

## Raised by
The error is raised by background job JOB-AP-030 during the generation of the payment proposal defined under [[sop-ap-003|SOP-AP-003]] [^r1].
