SOP-INV-004 governs the standard operating procedure for receiving, moving, storing, and issuing traction [[items-bat|battery packs]] across the Manchester works and the Leeds unit [^r1][^r2]. Owned by stores manager [[user-cshaw|Callum Shaw]] and approved by quality manager [[user-pjansen|Pieter Jansen]], it enforces strict fire-cell capacity limits, stacking thresholds, and quarantine controls [^r1][^r6][^r7][^r9]. All handling must comply with established safety parameters to prevent damage and thermal incidents across storage areas [^r6][^r10][^r14].

## Responsibilities

Goods inward staff accept initial custody of battery deliveries, examine outer cartons for transit damage, check pallet quantities against documentation, and enter receipts into Ledgerline ERP [^r3]. Inbound lithium-cell consignments must be booked exclusively into inspection warehouse WH-MAN-QC under [[sop-inv-001|SOP-INV-001]] and can transfer to the Manchester main stores fire-cell area only after Quality release [^r13].

Workshop technicians are required to draw battery packs strictly against issued build kits and return unassigned or surplus units to stores before shift completion [^r4]. The production supervisor directs cell quarantine measures and ensures any swollen, dropped, or suspect packs are immediately taken out of active assembly zones [^r5].

## Steps

### Receiving and movement

1. Offload cartons at goods inward and check outer cartons for visible transport defects [^r3].
2. Verify pallet counts against delivery paperwork and enter the receipt into Ledgerline ERP [^r3]. If an incoming carton lacks a legible date label, hold it on the inbound staging pallet and obtain a replacement print from the inventory clerk using the consignment paperwork [^r11].
3. Move cartons exclusively on pallets; stacking must never exceed four cartons in height [^r7].
4. Maintain clear gangways around Manchester charging racks at all times, keep floor markings freshly painted, and transfer delivery packaging to external skips at the end of each shift [^r16].

### Put-away and storage

1. Move approved battery packs into fire cells BAT-Z1 through BAT-Z4 in Manchester main stores [^r6][^r14].
2. Confirm the cell count board prior to placement to enforce the limit of no more than 1,500 packs per cell, in accordance with [[pol-bat-004|POL-BAT-004]] §2.2 [^r6][^r14].
3. Ensure stored packs are kept at a 30% to 50% state of charge [^r14].

### Issue and inventory reconciliation

1. Issue battery packs strictly on a first in, first out (FIFO) basis using the date displayed on the carton label [^r8].
2. Perform weekly stock counts alongside physical condition checks, signing off paper tally sheets and reconciling them against the system ledger before shift end [^r12].

### Quarantine and emergency response

1. Quarantine any dropped battery pack under [[sop-qua-002|SOP-QUA-002]], even when no external damage is visible [^r9]. Never return a dropped unit to the storage shelf [^r15].
2. Relocate damaged or suspect cartons immediately to the quarantine holding area, affix a physical red tag, place the pack into the red quarantine bin inside cell BAT-Z4, and book it out of available stock in Ledgerline ERP [^r10][^r15].
3. During emergency drill or response events, team leaders isolate charging racks at local isolator boards and close fire doors leading to the battery pack storage pen [^r17].
4. Nominated marshals position vermiculite granules, containment drums, and extraction kits from beside the testing bay without blocking exit routes toward the rear yard assembly point [^r18].

## Forms and screens

* Ledgerline ERP goods receipt screens for booking incoming stock and quarantine status adjustments [^r3][^r10].
* Physical carton date and identification labels [^r8][^r11].
* Physical red quarantine tags [^r10].
* Weekly battery tally sheets for paper sign-off and system reconciliation [^r12].
