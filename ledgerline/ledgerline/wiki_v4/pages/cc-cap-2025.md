Cost centre CAP-2025 (Capital projects 2025-26) covers Northbridge Cycles' capital project expenditures for the 2025-26 financial year[^r1]. It is assigned to operational unit NBC-MAN-01 under owner GWHITAKER[^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, the cost centre is closed, having ceased active postings on 2026-03-31[^r1].

## Cost centre record

CAP-2025 is recorded in [[data-cost-centres-and-budgets|Cost Centres and Budgets FY2026-27]] under unit NBC-MAN-01 with owner GWHITAKER[^r1]. The cost centre's official status is CLOSED following its closure on 2026-03-31 at the conclusion of the 2025-26 financial year[^r1].
