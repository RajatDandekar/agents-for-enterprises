The `SPK-*` item family encompasses bicycle spokes used across Northbridge Cycles wheel assemblies, categorized primarily into 13-gauge and 14-gauge variants in multiple lengths [^r1][^r8][^r11]. Stock balances are primarily held in the central Manchester store WH-MAN-STR, with small quantities of select lines maintained at the Leeds facility WH-LDS-STR [^r2][^r4]. As of 26 October 2026, stock-on-hand levels and open purchase commitments are recorded across the active part numbers [^r1][^r6][^r9].
