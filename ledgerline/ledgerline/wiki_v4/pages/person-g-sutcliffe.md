G. Sutcliffe appears in historical records as an approving authority for master data updates at Northbridge Cycles[^r1]. On 21 March 2019, Sutcliffe formally signed off on renumbering [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] in the supplier master[^r1]. This change remains relevant for tracing historical supplier codes back to their predecessor identifier[^r1].

## Actions

On 21 March 2019, G. Sutcliffe approved the supplier master change request that renumbered Voltway Cells from [[sup-vc-ein-0009|VC-EIN-0009]] to [[sup-vc-ein-0042|VC-EIN-0042]][^r1].
