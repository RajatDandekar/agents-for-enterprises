Role PUR-MGR is the Ledgerline purchasing role assigned to the Purchase Manager, held by [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor (DOKAFOR)]][^r1]. It carries a financial approval authority of £1,000,000 gross including VAT under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]][^r1][^r2][^r3]. The role governs rate contract administration, vendor onboarding pack approvals, and supplier unblocking authorizations across Northbridge Cycles[^r4][^r5][^r7].

## Approval limits and assignments

According to [[data-users-and-approval-limits|Users_and_approval_limits]], users holding the PUR-MGR role, such as [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]], possess an approval threshold of £1,000,000 gross[^r1]. This £1,000,000 gross limit is mirrored on the approval matrix sheet for the PUR-MGR role code[^r2]. Under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] §3, the Purchase Manager role's approval ceiling is set at £1,000,000 gross including VAT[^r3].

## Responsibilities and authority

Under [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]], the purchase manager role must review and approve supplier onboarding packs prior to vendor record creation in Ledgerline[^r4]. The role acts as the sole authority permitted to issue written requests to IT to unblock a supplier account[^r5]. Additionally, duplicate vendor registrations created in error must be reported to the purchase manager on the same day they occur[^r6].

Under [[sop-pur-004|SOP-PUR-004]], the Purchase Manager exercises overall commercial responsibility for negotiating agreements, confirming annual price settlements, authorising rate revisions, and determining whether open purchase orders must be amended or reissued following rate errors[^r8]. Rate contracts in Ledgerline ERP are entered strictly by the purchase manager's office under role PUR-MGR on the Rate contracts tab of the supplier master[^r7].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Approval limit revision (2024 to 2026)**: Today's approval limit for role PUR-MGR is £1,000,000 gross including VAT under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] v3.0 and the current user matrix[^r2][^r3]. Under superseded policy POL-APR-002 v2.0 (effective 2024-04-01), the approval limit was £750,000 gross including VAT (`superseded`)[^r10].
* **Intranet export (2016)**: Today's limit is £1,000,000 gross including VAT[^r2][^r3]. Under the historical 2016 intranet approval limits, the Purchase manager role had an approval limit of £600,000 net before VAT (`superseded`)[^r9].
