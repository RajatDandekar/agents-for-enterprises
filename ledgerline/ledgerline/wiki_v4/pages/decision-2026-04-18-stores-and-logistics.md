Operational decisions agreed at the stores and logistics meeting on 18 April 2026 govern immediate stock movements, carrier handovers, inter-site transfers, customer returns, and packaging counts [^r1]. They mandate same-day terminal postings and physical controls across Manchester and the Leeds satellite unit to prevent false line shortages, stock discrepancies, and dispatch delays [^r2][^r3][^r4]. These decisions remain in force across warehouse and dispatch operations as of 26 October 2026 [^r1][^r2].

## Decisions

### Job order material issues
Stores hands must post issue records on the terminal against the correct job orders prior to materials leaving holding pens [^r2]. This control prevents false line shortages and unnecessary purchasing over-ordering [^r2].

### Dispatch staging and carrier collections
Manchester dispatch staff must strap and label pallets earlier in the afternoon [^r3]. In addition, depot management of the primary carrier will be contacted to enforce adherence to scheduled collection windows [^r3].

### Inter-site transfer processing
Staff at Manchester and the Leeds satellite unit must process goods-out and goods-in transfer routines in Ledgerline on the same day the van arrives, rather than deferring posting until the end of the week [^r4].

### Customer returns quarantine
Staff must immediately affix a clear quarantine label to every returned crate offloaded from local bike shops [^r5]. The sales office must provide return paperwork promptly following receipt [^r5].

### Packaging material counts
Dispatch packaging materials, including basic cardboard cartons and packing foam, must be included in the weekly perpetual inventory count rota [^r6].

## Meeting details
The regular stores and logistics meeting took place on 18 April 2026 [^r1]. Attendees were Emma Clarke, Stefan Berg, Isla Morgan, [[user-atanaka|Aiko Tanaka]], Joanna Fletcher, and Grace Whitaker [^r1].
