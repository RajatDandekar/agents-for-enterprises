Brightcell Energy is a prospective supplier based in Lyon, France, offering 36 V 500 Wh battery packs for [[items-bat|BAT-* items]][^r3][^r4]. The company is not currently set up in the Ledgerline ERP supplier master, meaning Northbridge Cycles cannot commence trading with them today[^r1][^r2]. Qualification is currently blocked because their mandatory UN 38.3 compliance test report is undergoing laboratory renewal and is not expected until December 2026[^r5][^r8].

## Record

Brightcell Energy approached [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]] regarding battery packs, seeking setup as a new supplier to Northbridge Cycles[^r1][^r3]. The firm is located at 12 rue des Tanneurs, 69007 Lyon, France, and their Key Account Manager is Julien Marchand[^r3][^r6]. Brightcell Energy is not currently registered in the Ledgerline supplier master[^r1][^r8]. Trading cannot commence until standard evaluation is complete, compliance documents are vetted, and [[user-lmoreno|Luis Moreno]] sends the onboarding pack under [[sop-pur-001|SOP-PUR-001]][^r2].

## Certificates

Brightcell Energy does not currently meet Northbridge Cycles' compliance standard because their [[topic-un-38-3-certificates|UN 38.3 report]] is undergoing laboratory renewal[^r5][^r8][^r9]. The supplier cannot pass onboarding qualification until this vetting requirement is fulfilled under [[sop-pur-001|SOP-PUR-001]][^r2][^r8]. Brightcell Energy expects the renewed UN 38.3 test report to be ready in December 2026[^r5].

## Rate contracts

Brightcell Energy does not have an active rate contract with Northbridge Cycles[^r1]. Under quotation Q-BE-26-0917, Brightcell Energy offered 36 V 500 Wh battery packs at £104.00 per pack delivered (DAP) to Manchester, with a minimum order quantity of 500 packs and a lead time of six weeks from order[^r4][^r7].

## Notes

The qualification and possible switch to Brightcell Energy from incumbent suppliers such as [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] was evaluated following quotation Q-BE-26-0917[^r7]. However, compliance rules under [[sop-pur-001|SOP-PUR-001]] prevent onboarding or issuing purchase orders until valid UN 38.3 documentation is delivered[^r2][^r8].

## Versions and conflicts

- **future_dated**: Brightcell Energy cannot pass qualification or commence trading today (26 October 2026) because their UN 38.3 compliance report is undergoing renewal[^r2][^r8][^r9]. A new laboratory report is expected in December 2026, at which point qualification documents can be re-evaluated under [[sop-pur-001|SOP-PUR-001]][^r2][^r5].
