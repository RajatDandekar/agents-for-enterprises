POL-QUA-005 establishes Northbridge Cycles' quality standards for electric bicycle design and manufacture across the Manchester and Leeds facilities[^r1][^r4][^r9]. It defines defect segregation on the production line, incoming acceptance sampling levels, and quarantine management in the stock ledger and physical holding areas[^r2][^r5][^r9]. As of 26 October 2026, version 3.0 remains current and enforces quality thresholds linked to supplier management and ISO 9001:2015 certification valid through 31 May 2027[^r1][^r4][^r7].

## Clauses

### 1. Our commitment
In Manchester, if an operator discovers a stripped thread, an out-of-tolerance bracket, or an intermittent electrical fault, the unit is immediately removed from the line, physically labeled, and segregated in the rework area, halting station production until the cause is identified[^r2]. No bicycle may be booked out or manifested until every production check has been cleared in the system[^r3].

Operational controls agreed in quality meetings reinforce this commitment:
* Rear triangle alignment issues on frames from the Manchester plant fabrication area require manual setting prior to drive unit fitting, and fabrication jigs and clamping fixtures must be checked against reference gauges[^r13].
* Down tubes must be strictly deburred and an inspection mirror used on every frame prior to internal harness insertion to avoid insulation chafing on weld spatter[^r14].
* Tamper-evident lacquer must be applied immediately across motor drive unit bracket bolts and washers following torquing, with visual verification required prior to clipping protective covers into place[^r15].
* Secondary paper tape strapping is required on corrugated fork protectors at the Leeds depot to prevent transit scuffs[^r16].

### 2. Certification
Northbridge Cycles is certified to ISO 9001:2015 for the design and manufacture of electric bicycles under a certificate valid through 31 May 2027[^r4]. For external ISO 9001 surveillance audits, obsolete forms, loose drawings, and superseded work instructions must be cleared away into recycling bins, controlled copies of standard procedures must be readily accessible, and open non-conformance notes or pending calibration checks must be reviewed with the relevant section head without delay[^r19][^r20].

### 3. Incoming inspection
Under clause 3.1, incoming lithium-cell parts ([[items-bat|BAT-* items]]) must be inspected at acceptable quality level (AQL) 0.65, whereas all other incoming parts are inspected at AQL 1.5[^r5]. Every incoming sample battery pack must undergo an open-circuit voltage check using calibrated testing meters before acceptance[^r6]. Operational procedures for inspection workflows are detailed in [[sop-qua-001|SOP-QUA-001 Incoming Inspection]].

### 4. Supplier quality
Under clause 4.1, a supplier whose rejection rate exceeds 2% for two consecutive quarters is placed on watch in accordance with [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003 Supplier Policy]] §8.2[^r7]. When a supplier fails to meet quality standards, purchasing issues a corrective action request requiring evidence of root-cause investigation and containment measures before normal trading resumes[^r8].

### 5. Non-conformance
Defective items identified on the line must be marked with standard quarantine tape, isolated in the Manchester quarantine pen or Leeds holding area, and updated in the stock ledger to prevent allocation to live manufacturing orders[^r9]. The Ledgerline quarantine routine must be completed immediately as soon as a reject ticket is tied to a pallet, preventing stores from reissuing failed components[^r12]. Inspectors affix yellow status labels to suspect components once moved into the main quarantine hold, and paperwork for supplier returns cannot be cleared against delivery notes in the system until rejected parts are transferred into the secure lock-up[^r17][^r18]. The quality inspector and works manager determine whether non-conforming material is scrapped, reworked on site, returned to the supplier for credit, or accepted under concession, updating the stock ledger accordingly[^r10].

### 6. Continual improvement
Continual improvement reviews collate scrap trends, bike shop warranty returns, and supplier non-conformance logs to test and update work instructions, jigs, or inspection frequencies in the ERP before becoming standard practice[^r11].
