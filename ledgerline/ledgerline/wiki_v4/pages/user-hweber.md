## Responsibilities and approvals

Henrik Weber holds an approval threshold of £5,000,000 gross including VAT under purchasing matrix [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] v3.0, reviews the matrix annually every April, and coordinates sign-off recording through his office for commitments exceeding £5,000,000 that require Managing Director approval[^r5]. Under [[pol-abc-011|POL-ABC-011]] §3, he is responsible for granting advance approval for any hospitality exceeding £150 a head[^r4].

As Plant Head, Weber serves as the formal document approver across major company policies and standard operating procedures:
* Policies: [[pol-abc-011|POL-ABC-011]] v1.3[^r3], [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] v3.0[^r5], [[pol-hse-010|POL-HSE-010]] v5.0[^r6], [[pol-pur-001|POL-PUR-001]] v4.1[^r7], [[pol-qua-005|POL-QUA-005]] v3.0[^r8], and [[pol-ret-006|POL-RET-006]] v2.1[^r9].
* Procedures: [[sop-fin-001|SOP-FIN-001]] v1.1[^r10], [[sop-inv-002|SOP-INV-002]] v1.3[^r11], [[sop-it-001|SOP-IT-001]] v1.6[^r12], [[sop-it-002|SOP-IT-002]] v1.0[^r13], [[sop-prd-001|SOP-PRD-001]] v1.5[^r14], [[sop-prd-002|SOP-PRD-002]] v1.0[^r15], [[sop-pur-002|SOP-PUR-002]] v1.4[^r16], [[sop-pur-003|SOP-PUR-003]] v1.2[^r17], [[sop-pur-004|SOP-PUR-004]] v2.0[^r18], [[sop-pur-006|SOP-PUR-006]] v1.0[^r19], [[sop-qua-001|SOP-QUA-001]] v2.0[^r20], [[sop-qua-002|SOP-QUA-002]] v1.1[^r21], and [[sop-sal-001|SOP-SAL-001]] v2.2[^r22].

## Operational management

In August 2019, Weber was tasked with cloning new purchasing starter profiles from the existing buyer template into Ledgerline[^r39]. He planned a technical review visit to [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] in Eindhoven in January 2021 to inspect testing and unit calibration stations[^r40]. On 28 September 2021, he coordinated on-site visits from the Leeds team to Manchester regarding frame deliveries, transfers, and order queries[^r41].

Weber reviewed warranty claim logs with [[user-imorgan|Isla Morgan]] in December 2021 to set production checks on rear hub assemblies and wiring looms[^r42]. In February 2023, [[user-cshaw|Callum Shaw]] coordinated with him to ensure shift rework or heavy production schedules would not conflict with workshop battery fire drills[^r43]. On 6 October 2023, he received a purchasing headcount request from [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] proposing an additional buyer[^r44].

In September 2024, Weber issued operational directives for the Manchester site Christmas shutdown, scheduling skeleton maintenance for the assembly lines and paint plant while mandating timesheet submissions and carrier dispatches[^r45][^r46]. In October 2024, he was consulted regarding stores layout adjustments at Leeds to alleviate aisle congestion from Manchester stock transfers[^r47]. On 1 October 2026, he notified staff that cost centre [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] was open and that [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] would close on 15 October 2026[^r48].
