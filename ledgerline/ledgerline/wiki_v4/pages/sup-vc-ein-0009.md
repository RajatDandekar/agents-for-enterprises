VC-EIN-0009 is the legacy supplier master record for [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]]. It is blocked from active purchasing and must not be used on new procurement documents. Operating units should transact exclusively against active account [[sup-vc-ein-0042|VC-EIN-0042]].

## Record
In the supplier master, record VC-EIN-0009 is blocked and has been renamed to include "(old code, do not use)" [^r1].

## Certificates
No active certificates are maintained under this historic code.

## Rate contracts
No current rate contracts exist under VC-EIN-0009.

## Notes
The account was retired and blocked in accordance with [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]] §5 [^r1]. Attempting to raise purchase orders against this record triggers [[err-sup-0031|ERR-SUP-0031 Supplier is blocked]].
