As of 26 October 2026, he enforces compliance controls across battery suppliers, managing onboarding requirements and purchasing holds[^r33][^r46][^r48].

## Role and responsibilities

Luis Moreno holds the active Ledgerline role PUR-SR in operating unit NBC-MAN-01 with a gross approval limit of £500,000, assigned to default cost centre [[cc-prd-03|PRD-03]][^r17]. In the 2016 intranet directory, he held the role of Buyer at extension 2236[^r21].

He is the assigned buyer in [[data-item-master|ITEM_MASTER_2026-10-26]] for [[items-bat|BAT-* items]] including BAT-500, BAT-500-R, BAT-375, and BAT-750, [[items-chg|CHG-* items]] including charger CHG-4A-01, and [[items-fg|FG-* items]] including finished bike FG-ANC-M-BLK[^r1]. He co-authored the 2024 purchasing induction deck and serves as the department point of contact for suppliers and UN 38.3 certification queries[^r20].

He authors and owns [[sop-pur-005|SOP-PUR-005]] v1.1, which mandates executing monthly batch job JOB-PUR-022 on the first working day of each month with a 90-day window to track expiring battery documentation[^r18][^r19]. Following a battery incident review on 30 July 2026, he agreed to draft [[amd-sup-2026-02|AMD-SUP-2026-02]] to the Supplier Policy, establishing a 12-month window and 60-day margin[^r31].
