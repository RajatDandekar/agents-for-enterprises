Sofia Lindqvist is the HR Manager at Northbridge Cycles who approved version 6.1 of [[pol-hr-020|POL-HR-020]][^r1]. She is the designated business owner for integration interface INT-005, which manages outbound payroll data exports[^r2]. In her operational HR capacity, she coordinates user access onboarding with IT and issues company-wide employee guidance on leave balances and carry-forward entitlements[^r3][^r5].

## Responsibilities and governance

Lindqvist acts as the policy authority for employee working hours, absence, and leave entitlements, having approved [[pol-hr-020|POL-HR-020]] (Employee Handbook extract) version 6.1 on 1 January 2026[^r1]. Within the [[data-integration-inventory|Integration inventory]], she is listed as the business owner for interface INT-005, which handles the outbound payroll export[^r2].

## Operational activity

On 7 August 2019, Lindqvist coordinated Ledgerline ERP user access setup with [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber]] for incoming purchasing team personnel assigned to Manchester works and the Leeds unit[^r3]. In that request, she specified that the new starters would shadow senior buyers on operational routines, including checking supplier acknowledgements, handling price variances, and resolving partial warehouse deliveries[^r4]. 

On 15 December 2021, Lindqvist issued an annual leave reminder to all Manchester staff outlining year-end leave balance management and carry-forward rules[^r5].
