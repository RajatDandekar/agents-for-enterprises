Pieter Jansen is the Quality Manager at Northbridge Cycles, overseeing quality assurance procedures, incoming inspections, and plant quarantine protocols [^r21][^r22][^r23][^r24]. He is the author and document owner of [[sop-qua-001|SOP-QUA-001]] and [[sop-qua-002|SOP-QUA-002]], as well as the owner of technical interface INT-004 for inbound battery test rig results [^r22][^r23][^r25]. Jansen also manages supplier ISO-9001 compliance uploads in [[data-supplier-master|SUPPLIER_MASTER]] and leads non-conformance quarantine enforcement across the Manchester and Leeds sites [^r1][^r2][^r28][^r32].

## Quality procedures and ownership

Jansen is the author and document owner of [[sop-qua-001|SOP-QUA-001]] (Incoming Inspection v2.0, effective 2024-04-01) and [[sop-qua-002|SOP-QUA-002]] (Damaged Battery Pack Quarantine v1.1) [^r22][^r23]. In his capacity as Quality Manager, he approved version 1.0 of [[sop-inv-004|SOP-INV-004]] (Battery Pack Storage and Handling) and version 1.1 of [[sop-sal-002|SOP-SAL-002]] (Warranty Claims from Bike Shops) [^r21][^r24]. In the system integration inventory, he owns interface INT-004, which handles inbound results from the battery test rig [^r25].

## Quality oversight and operations

Jansen coordinates quarantine holds, incoming inspections, and defect rectifications across facilities [^r27][^r32][^r40]. In December 2019, he directed Stores to clear the assembly area quarantine bin to avoid suspect parts being picked by temporary agency staff on late shifts, emphasizing the hazards of leaving uninspected parts on pallets near walkways [^r34][^r35]. In July 2024, he resolved plant quarantine backlogs on bought-in electrical assemblies and frame brackets by requesting assistance from stores and assembly teams for visual checks and booking accepted items [^r40][^r41].

His operational duties include preparing sites for external audits, addressing carrier transit damages on bicycle frames, and managing equipment maintenance, such as arranging external technicians when the end-of-line test rig drive unit failed in April 2021 following a request from [[user-sberg|Stefan Berg]] [^r36][^r37][^r38]. Ahead of a June 2021 external ISO 9001 surveillance audit across Manchester offices, stores, and assembly lines, Jansen instructed supervisors to audit training sign-off sheets, track inspection stamps, and clear open non-conformance notes [^r38][^r39]. Furthermore, he issued a supplier quality review in January 2019 regarding recurring defects in frame finishes and motor wiring harnesses into Manchester and Leeds, instructing buyers to obtain corrective action plans and update vendor records in Ledgerline [^r33].

Jansen regularly attends and leads site review sessions, including the IT steering meeting on 11 March 2022, stores and logistics sessions on returns quarantine and line moves on 1 October 2024, the Leeds unit update meeting on 18 March 2025, and purchasing reviews on 24 January 2026 [^r26][^r27][^r28][^r29]. At the 23 May 2026 quality meeting, he evaluated assembly defects, wheel building calibration, transit bay bin counts, and battery housing latch fitment [^r30]. On 21 August 2026, he led discussions regarding frame alignment, wiring deburring, motor fasteners, packaging, and the requirement for immediate quarantine entries in Ledgerline [^r32].

## Battery testing and compliance

Jansen manages testing and safety procedures for incoming [[items-bat|BAT-* items]] [^r31][^r42][^r43]. As part of [[decision-2026-06-15-voltway-battery-receipt|receiving procedures]] for [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]], he draws AQL samples directly at the receiving bay prior to put-away into Manchester main stores [^r42]. 

On 28 July 2026, Jansen reported that a quarantined batch of 400 battery packs failed incoming abuse testing after the supplier switched cell makers since their 22-month-old [[topic-un-38-3-certificates|UN 38.3]] report [^r43]. Following this failure, he advised purchasing and plant management to reject UN 38.3 test reports older than roughly 12 months with a margin before expiry [^r44]. At the 30 July 2026 battery incident review, Jansen was tasked with keeping the failed lot quarantined until supplier collection [^r31].
