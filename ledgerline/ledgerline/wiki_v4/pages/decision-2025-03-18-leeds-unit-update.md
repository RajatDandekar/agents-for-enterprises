Operational review held on 18 March 2025 by [[user-pjansen|Pieter Jansen]], [[user-bprice|Bronwen Price]], and [[user-kmensah|Kwame Mensah]] covering Leeds unit workshop operations, stock control, despatch labelling, warranty returns, and housekeeping [^r1]. The meeting established immediate operational directives to prevent loading bay bottlenecks, inaccurate line stock-out displays, carrier collection refusals, and safety hazards [^r2][^r4][^r5][^r8]. As of 26 October 2026, these operational controls govern component transaction timing, inter-site transfer documentation, and electrical quarantine processing [^r3][^r4][^r7].

## Operational decisions and actions

### Manchester transfer documentation
Missing transfer documentation from Manchester previously resulted in bicycle frames sitting on the Leeds loading bay while goods inward staff telephoned Manchester to verify allocations [^r2]. The Manchester dispatch supervisor was assigned to review the packing checklist and ensure transfer documentation accompanies every vehicle [^r3].

### Stores kit issue timing
Delays in keying manual kit issue sheets into the system until late in the shift caused apparent assembly line stock-outs on screens when parts were physically present on the floor [^r4]. Floor staff were reminded that transactions must be entered against jobs as soon as components leave stores [^r4].

### Despatch labelling bottlenecks
Carrier collection drivers for finished electric bicycles were turned away when palletised bikes were not fully labelled with delivery notes due to frequent ribbon replacement bottlenecks on the warehouse terminal [^r5].

### Electrical warranty returns quarantine
Warranty returns of motors and battery packs from independent bike shops were backing up in the quarantine bay awaiting technical diagnostics, skewing inventory valuations [^r6]. The quality team was tasked with inspecting quarantined electrical components and posting disposition adjustments in the system against original return authorisations [^r7].

### Workshop housekeeping and battery bay safety
Fire exit routes near the battery charging bay had been partially obstructed by scrap wooden pallets and cardboard packaging following deliveries, requiring immediate clearance by the floor team [^r8].
