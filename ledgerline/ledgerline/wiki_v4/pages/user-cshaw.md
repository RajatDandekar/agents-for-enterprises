Callum Shaw (`CSHAW`) is the stores manager at Northbridge Cycles, overseeing warehouse receiving, storage, and inventory control operations across the Manchester works and Leeds unit [^r1][^r11]. He authors and owns key inventory standard operating procedures, including goods receipt, vendor returns, cycle and annual stock counts, and battery pack handling [^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4]. He also manages physical intake staffing, quarantine moves, carrier booking interfaces, and inter-site transfer confirmations [^r5][^r14][^r21][^r23].

## Responsibilities and procedures

Shaw owns and maintains four core inventory procedures: [[sop-inv-001|SOP-INV-001]] for goods receipt, [[sop-inv-002|SOP-INV-002]] for returns to vendor, [[sop-inv-003|SOP-INV-003]] for cycle and annual stock counts, and [[sop-inv-004|SOP-INV-004]] for battery pack storage and handling [^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4]. He is also the registered owner of interface INT-003 for outbound carrier booking with Moorland Logistics [^r5].

Within daily warehouse operations, Shaw supervises intake processing, damaged delivery handling, and quarantine allocations [^r14][^r15]. He requires delivery manifest endorsements for damaged packaging, photographs damaged shipments for insurance purposes, and coordinates transfer of suspect parts to the main quarantine hold [^r14][^r15]. Additionally, he issues the annual forklift refresher training notices for Manchester warehouse and stores staff, coordinates battery fire drill sequences with the site safety officer, and alerts external units to prevent false emergency alarms [^r16][^r19].

## Operational coordination

Shaw coordinates intake logistics and assembly material availability across sites [^r17][^r21]. In October 2023, he alerted purchasing to late deliveries that threatened assembly schedules in Manchester and Leeds, instructing buyers to update revised delivery dates in Ledgerline ERP and to escalate major supplier delays directly to him [^r17][^r18]. Following reports of Monday morning intake congestion and yard blockages at the Manchester receiving bay in March 2026, he was tasked with reviewing haulier delivery slots and scheduling extra intake desk staff to protect the Leeds transfer wagon schedule [^r20][^r21].

On internal logistics controls, [[user-kmensah|Kwame Mensah]] instructed Shaw in May 2026 to have stores verify returnable crate sleeves, destination slips, and routing stickers [^r22]. In September 2026, [[user-bprice|Bronwen Price]] instructed him to ensure stores teams across both Leeds and Manchester strictly confirm dispatches and receipts against open transfer advices for inter-site stock movements [^r23].
