Ledgerline ERP 7.1 is an enterprise update released on 8 May 2017 governing system administration, inventory management, and production control across all active sites [^r1]. It established mandatory rank validation on approval delegations, automated ownership transfer for consignment stock, and strict engineering change revisions on active assembly runs [^r3][^r6][^r8]. The upgrade also prompted site-wide operational alignments, including a revised supplier numbering standard across the Manchester plant and the Leeds unit [^r1][^r13].

## Changes

### System administration

Approval delegation functionality under [[screen-sys-dlg-03|SYS-DLG-03]] enforces that an appointed delegate must hold an operational role rank equal to or higher than the delegator, otherwise saving halts with error [[err-dlg-0210|ERR-DLG-0210]] [^r3]. Pre-existing delegation records remain active following the upgrade, but any subsequent revision, reinstatement, or new entry immediately enforces the rank restriction [^r4]. Background feeds and third-party utilities transmitting delegation tables must validate seniority levels prior to transfer, writing rejected entries to a local interface log and stopping the batch rather than failing silently [^r5].

### Inventory management

Consignment stock tracking under [[screen-inv-csg-17|INV-CSG-17]] introduces an explicit ownership indicator on stock maintenance and warehouse enquiry screens, retaining component visibility for production scheduling while excluding non-owned items from general company stock valuation [^r6]. Receiving incoming components under consignment terms increments on-hand quantities without generating an unvouched liability in the purchase ledger [^r7]. The system posts an ownership transfer entry only when stock moves to assembly lines at the point of consumption [^r7].

### Production control

Under engineering change notes [[screen-prd-ecn-06|PRD-ECN-06]], approving an ECN initiates an immediate revision verification across active assembly runs, marks revised status alongside drawing references, and locks component allocations against issuing superseded parts without supervisory sign-off [^r8]. Integrations for shop floor data collection and production scheduling must supply active ECN references in staging tables, or the interface halts and rejects batch files attempting to issue obsolete items against revised works orders [^r9].

### Known issues

Printing the works order summary report to network dot-matrix printers on wide continuous stationery can cause misaligned column headers [^r10]. Running an unfiltered debtor ledger lookup from sales order entry can introduce lookup window latency, which is resolved by typing at least the first letter of the customer surname or the initial character of the trading name [^r11]. During peak periods with concurrent entries across Leeds and Manchester, the free stock display on the inventory inquiry screen may lag behind recent receipts until the screen is closed and reopened [^r12].

## Effect at Northbridge

Operating split software versions between the Manchester plant and the Leeds unit is unsupported, requiring all departments across both sites to migrate simultaneously during scheduled maintenance [^r1]. Migration to Ledgerline 7.1 introduced a new supplier numbering scheme that required supplier master change requests, including renumbering [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] to VC-EIN-0042 and blocking the legacy code [[sup-vc-ein-0009|VC-EIN-0009]] [^r13][^r14].

## Versions and conflicts

Voltway Cells operates under active supplier code [[sup-vc-ein-0042|VC-EIN-0042]] following the Ledgerline 7.1 migration [^r13][^r14]. Under the prior numbering scheme, the supplier was registered as [[sup-vc-ein-0009|VC-EIN-0009]], which was superseded and blocked upon cutover [^r13][^r14] (`superseded`).
