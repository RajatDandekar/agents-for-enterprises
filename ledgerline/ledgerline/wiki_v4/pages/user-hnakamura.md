Hana Nakamura (user ID HNAKAMURA) is a purchase executive operating under role PUR-EXEC for the Leeds operating unit (NBC-LDS-01)[^r2][^r7][^r12]. She manages procurement execution and purchase order approvals for Leeds assembly and maintenance activities[^r10][^r13][^r15]. Additionally, she serves as the assigned buyer in the item master for packaging items such as PKG-LBL-UN[^r1].

## Responsibilities and procurement activity

Hana Nakamura acts as the designated buyer for [[items-pkg|packaging]] materials, including item PKG-LBL-UN in [[data-item-master|ITEM_MASTER_2026-10-26]][^r1]. Throughout 2026, her primary procurement activity covers the Leeds assembly cost centre [[cc-lds-prd-01|LDS-PRD-01]] and Leeds maintenance cost centre [[cc-mnt-02|MNT-02]][^r10][^r13][^r16]. Transactions are recorded in [[data-po-extract-2026|PO_extract_2026-01_to_2026-10]][^r2][^r7].

In January 2026, she raised Leeds-allocated orders including PO/2026/00006 for Bollin Tyre Co. Ltd (TYR-24-TRK-2.25) and PO/2026/00012 for Tame Fixings ([[items-fst|FST-WSH-M4-BK]]) against LDS-PRD-01, alongside creating and approving orders PO/2026/00014, PO/2026/00032, PO/2026/00050, and PO/2026/00056[^r2][^r10]. In February 2026, she raised purchase orders for Leeds operations such as PO/2026/00131 for Canalside Alloy Wheels on 2026-02-02[^r11].

During March 2026, Nakamura raised order PO/2026/00311 on 9 March 2026 for 40,500 units of [[items-rim|RIM-20-AL-DW]] at a rate of 35.83 (£1,741,338.00 gross) under cost centre LDS-PRD-01[^r12]. Between 2026-03-26 and 2026-03-30, she raised orders PO/2026/00387, PO/2026/00398, PO/2026/00401, PO/2026/00402, and PO/2026/00417 across MNT-02 and LDS-PRD-01, and cancelled PO/2026/00408[^r3][^r13].

In April 2026, she processed orders across LDS-PRD-01 and MNT-02, including PO/2026/00428 for 1,800 TYR-28-PCT-1.75 tyres, PO/2026/00429 for MRO-00791, PO/2026/00515 for 70,000 [[items-brk|BRK-PAD-01]] brake pads, PO/2026/00552 (£24,691.20 gross to TF-TAI-0460), and PO/2026/00565 (£780.80 gross to NES-NUR-1051)[^r4][^r14].

In May 2026, she executed multi-million pound frame orders PO/2026/00602 and PO/2026/00717 for Leeds assembly, alongside orders PO/2026/00636, PO/2026/00638 (which was rejected), PO/2026/00659, PO/2026/00660, PO/2026/00661, PO/2026/00663, PO/2026/00673, PO/2026/00679, PO/2026/00681, PO/2026/00683, and PO/2026/00688[^r5][^r15].

In June 2026, she handled packaging order PO/2026/00721 and [[items-sdl|saddle]] order PO/2026/00839, as well as Leeds maintenance orders PO/2026/00842, PO/2026/00845, and PO/2026/00848 for MRO items under reverse-charge VAT (VAT20-RC) between 2026-06-23 and 2026-06-25[^r16][^r17].

In July 2026, Nakamura created PO/2026/00882 on 2026-07-01 for 11,200 NOS of [[items-frm|FRM-TAM-S-GRN]] (£2,644,320.00 gross), which was approved by [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber (HWEBER)]][^r6]. On 2026-07-06, under role PUR-EXEC, she self-approved PO/2026/00902 for 1,200 NOS of [[items-bat|BAT-500]] valued at £164,160.00 gross[^r7]. On 2026-07-13, she raised order PO/2026/00939 for FRM-IRK-L-BLK with a gross value of £209,966.40, self-approving and releasing it on the same day[^r8]. Additional orders processed in July 2026 included PO/2026/00894, PO/2026/00912, PO/2026/00918, PO/2026/00949, PO/2026/00950, PO/2026/00955, PO/2026/00956, PO/2026/00960, PO/2026/00964, PO/2026/00966, PO/2026/00971, PO/2026/00976, PO/2026/00985, PO/2026/00986, PO/2026/00995, PO/2026/01001 (50 units of FST-SCR-M5X12-SS from TF-TEL-0287), PO/2026/01018 (BRK-ROT-160 from SBS-WIG-0969 for £532,008.00 gross), PO/2026/01019 (FST-BLT-M4X8-SS from PF-HUL-0325 for £2,862.00 gross), and PO/2026/01020 (released for 2 units of MRO-00958 from VES-BRI-0680 under MNT-02)[^r18][^r19][^r20][^r21].

In August 2026, she created and approved orders including PO/2026/01034 for MRO-00100 to MNT-02, alongside LDS-PRD-01 orders PO/2026/01045 for fasteners, PO/2026/01049, PO/2026/01062, PO/2026/01078 for [[items-lac|LAC-BLU]], PO/2026/01083 for bike frames, and PO/2026/01096[^r9][^r22].

In September 2026, she executed purchase orders for LDS-PRD-01 including PO/2026/01100 for 1,600 units of FRM-SPR-L-BLK from Wyre Frameworks (£228,153.60)[^r23].

In October 2026, she raised LDS-PRD-01 orders including PO/2026/01153, PO/2026/01156, PO/2026/01158, PO/2026/01164, and PO/2026/01166[^r24].
