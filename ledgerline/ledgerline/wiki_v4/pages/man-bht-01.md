Course BHT-01 covers Northbridge Cycles' safety standards for handling, storing, and shipping lithium battery packs, focusing on fire cell limits and damage quarantine protocols [^r1][^r3][^r5]. Dropped packs must never be restocked because latent cell damage can emerge days after an impact [^r2][^r6]. All handling staff must maintain valid certification by completing refresher training every 24 months [^r8].

## Overview

Quality department course BHT-01 version 3 was issued in November 2025 to govern the handling of [[items-bat|BAT-* items]] [^r1]. Most battery incidents trace back to a dropped pack, and lithium cells can manifest internal damage days after an incident occurs [^r2]. Handlers must complete refresher training every 24 months [^r8]. General governance and standard operational practices align with [[pol-bat-004|POL-BAT-004 Battery Safety and Handling Policy]] and [[sop-inv-004|SOP-INV-004 Battery Pack Storage and Handling]].

## Storage and fire cells

Battery storage is restricted to designated fire cells BAT-Z1, BAT-Z2, BAT-Z3, and BAT-Z4 located inside the Manchester main stores [^r3]. Each individual fire cell has a maximum capacity of 1,500 battery packs [^r4]. Stored packs must be maintained at a state of charge between 30% and 50% [^r4].

## Damaged pack handling and quarantine

Any dropped pack must never be returned to storage shelves [^r6]. Damaged units must immediately be placed inside the red quarantine bin located in fire cell BAT-Z4 [^r5]. Handlers must log a Non-Conformance Report on screen [[screen-qa-ncr-02|QA-NCR-02 Non-Conformance Report Entry]] during the same shift in which the damage occurred [^r6]. Quarantine processing follows [[sop-qua-002|SOP-QUA-002 Damaged Battery Pack Quarantine]].

## Shipping requirements

Shipping battery stock requires carrier service DGX [^r7]. Standalone battery packs must be shipped under UN code UN3480 [^r7]. Packs shipped inside or packaged alongside a bicycle must be shipped under code UN3481 [^r7].
