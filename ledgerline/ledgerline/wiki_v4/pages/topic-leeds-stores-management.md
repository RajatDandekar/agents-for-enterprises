Leeds stores space management covers layout controls and inventory staging rules implemented to resolve staging bottlenecks at the facility. In October 2024, overflow from fully occupied pallet racking led to inventory accumulating in the central aisle, disrupting daily logistics dispatch operations [^r1]. Relocating slower-moving items to secondary storage spaces ensures transit paths remain unobstructed for scheduled afternoon collections [^r2].

## Aisle Congestion and Storage Arrangements

In October 2024, the Leeds main stores faced severe aisle congestion caused by full pallet racking [^r1]. Finished bicycles and boxed conversion kits were left standing in the central aisle, obstructing afternoon carrier collections [^r1].

To mitigate carton damage and prevent dispatch delays during afternoon carrier pickups, slower-moving spare parts were designated for relocation into the secondary room near the trade counter [^r2].
