On 12 December 2025, [[user-kmensah|Kwame Mensah]], [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]], [[user-cshaw|Callum Shaw]], and [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]] met for a finance and purchasing catch-up covering open balances, goods received without invoices, and purchase ledger housekeeping.[^r1] The meeting established practical coordination procedures between purchasing buyers and accounts payable to clear unmatched receipts, eliminate suspense account delays on foreign orders, and prevent order placements with suppliers on credit stop.[^r1][^r2][^r6][^r9] These joint commitments remain in effect to prevent dispute holds during standard invoice matching and payment processing.[^r4]

## Decisions

### Accruals and unmatched receipts
* Purchasing agreed to review outstanding accrued deliveries against paper goods received notes and close completed orders that remain open on the system.[^r2]
* Finance agreed to send an updated list of unmatched receipts to the purchasing desk for checking.[^r3]

### Price adjustments and invoice matching
* Purchasing buyers must notify accounts payable in writing of any accepted price adjustments prior to invoice arrival rather than waiting for invoice registration, avoiding dispute holds on payment runs.[^r4]
* Finance agreed to provide purchasing with a summary of all supplier accounts currently held on query.[^r5]

### Overseas orders and pro-forma payments
* Purchasing agreed to pass all pro-forma payment receipts and pro-forma invoice copies to the ledger clerk upon order placement with overseas component suppliers.[^r7]
* Finance must cross-reference advance bank payments against open foreign purchase orders before month-end to resolve suspense account reconciliation issues.[^r6]

### Credit holds and creditor management
* Purchasing agreed to forward all supplier credit hold notifications to the joint finance mailbox rather than handling them at individual desks.[^r8]
* Finance agreed to distribute an aged creditor balance summary to buyers ahead of order cycles to avoid placing fresh orders with suppliers that have placed Northbridge on credit stop.[^r9]
