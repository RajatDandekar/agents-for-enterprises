Purchasing from continental European vendors requires raising all commitments within Ledgerline ERP before placing orders, with orders recorded in pounds while finance manages foreign currency payments [^r1][^r2]. Deliveries arrive via approved road freight carriers at the Manchester factory or the Leeds unit and require immediate inspection and same-day receipt logging [^r6][^r8][^r13]. Invoices from European suppliers must cite official purchase order numbers and match system pricing before accounts payable releases payment [^r11][^r12].

## Ordering and Currency

All purchase orders for continental vendors must be raised through Ledgerline ERP before making any commitment to the supplier [^r1]. Prior to generating purchase documentation via [[sop-pur-002|SOP-PUR-002]], staff must confirm that part descriptions, carriage terms, and agreed lead times match the master vendor file [^r3]. Where contracts require payment in foreign currency, finance handles exchange arrangements via the bank while the purchase order remains recorded in pounds in Ledgerline [^r2].

Any discrepancy between an order confirmation and the system purchase order must be resolved with the vendor immediately rather than waiting for invoice arrival [^r4]. In addition, repeat orders for high-turnover e-bike parts such as drive units and wiring looms must account for cross-channel transit schedules to prevent production stoppages [^r5].

## Freight, Delivery, and Goods Inward

European consignments arrive via approved road freight carriers at either the Manchester factory or the Leeds unit [^r13]. Receiving teams must inspect all shipments upon arrival before signing carrier documentation or recording receipts into Ledgerline ERP [^r6]. Packing notes from overseas suppliers must be checked against physical contents for missing items, incorrect specifications, or transit damage before offloading is completed [^r7].

Goods inward staff must enter deliveries against the open purchase order on the day of arrival to ensure stock balances update for manufacturing planning [^r8]. Delivery notes, carrier consignment notes, and international transport documents must be stamped, signed, and forwarded to the purchasing office without delay [^r9]. If goods arrive damaged or short, receiving staff must photograph the consignment, endorse the carrier paperwork accordingly, and notify the buyer to lodge a claim with the vendor [^r10].

## Invoicing and Payment Processing

European vendor invoices must quote Northbridge's official purchase order reference, or they will be held pending buyer clarification [^r11]. In accordance with standard matching procedures under [[sop-ap-001|SOP-AP-001]], price variances between a purchase order and a vendor invoice require written sign-off from the purchasing department before finance can release payment [^r12].
