POL-DRP-009 defines the mandatory retention schedules and disposal rules for physical and electronic business records across Northbridge Cycles[^r1]. Version 1.4 is owned by Finance and IT, was approved by Financial Controller [[user-gwhitaker|Grace Whitaker]], and took effect on 2025-05-01[^r1]. It establishes departmental retention periods ranging from 31 days for CCTV to 15 years for battery test logs, and mandates secure physical destruction consoles and manual ERP purge procedures[^r4][^r5][^r10][^r13].

## Clauses

### §1. Why we keep records
Signed paper documents, including goods received sheets and carrier delivery notes, must be filed in departmental binders promptly at the end of each shift[^r2]. Historical files must never be destroyed or purged without written confirmation from the department manager[^r3].

### §3. Disposal
At the Manchester and Leeds sites, confidential paper waste, old job cards, and superseded despatch notes must be placed into locked confidential destruction consoles on site[^r4]. Ledgerline ERP does not automatically delete expired transactions, requiring system administrators to run manual purge utilities during scheduled maintenance windows after full system backups[^r5].

### Table 1. Retention periods
* **Purchase orders, receipts, and supplier invoices:** Retained by Finance for 7 years after the end of the financial year[^r6].
* **Supplier certificates (e.g. UN 38.3, ISO):** Retained by Purchasing for 10 years after the supplier is blocked[^r7].
* **Ledgerline audit trail:** Retained by IT for 7 years (84 months)[^r8].
* **E-mails:** Retained by IT for 3 years[^r9].
* **CCTV recordings:** Retained by Facilities for 31 days[^r10].
* **HR personnel files:** Retained for 6 years after the employee leaves[^r11].
* **Job applicant records:** Retained by HR for 6 months after the decision[^r12].
* **Battery end-of-line test results:** Retained by Quality for 15 years[^r13].
