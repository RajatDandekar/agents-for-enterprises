R. Ainsworth was the Managing Director of Northbridge Cycles Ltd in 1998 [^r1]. In this executive role, Ainsworth executed the core contract that established the company's historical enterprise resource planning baseline [^r1]. This historical record documents the origin of Northbridge Cycles' deployment of Ledgerline ERP [^r1].

## Role and company history

R. Ainsworth served as Managing Director of Northbridge Cycles Ltd and signed the Software Licence Agreement for Ledgerline ERP version 5.2 on 14 August 1998 [^r1].
