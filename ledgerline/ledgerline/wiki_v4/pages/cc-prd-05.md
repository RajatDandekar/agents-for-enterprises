Cost centre PRD-05 covered regular assembly operations for Northbridge Cycles' City e-bike line under owner [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor (DOKAFOR)]] and business unit NBC-MAN-01 [^r1]. The cost centre was closed in Ledgerline on 15 October 2026 [^r1][^r10][^r12][^r13]. Any purchasing transactions saved against PRD-05 remain in Draft status with warning W-CC-114 and will not be transmitted to suppliers [^r14].

## Line closure and transition

Cost centre PRD-05 was closed on 15 October 2026 [^r1][^r10][^r12][^r13]. The closure required all open assembly orders tied to legacy frames to be settled or written off and work in progress cleared from the line side [^r10]. Physical inventory, obsolete jigs, and legacy frame fixtures from the closed PRD-05 line were transferred to the storage unit in Leeds [^r11].

Purchasing transactions entered against PRD-05 now remain in Draft status with warning W-CC-114 and are prevented from transmitting to vendors [^r14].
