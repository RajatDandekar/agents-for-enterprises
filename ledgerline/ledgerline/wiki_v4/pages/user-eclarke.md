Emma Clarke (user ID ECLARKE) is an active purchase executive at Northbridge Cycles based in the Manchester office, reporting to [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]][^r17][^r40]. She is assigned role [[role-pur-exec|PUR-EXEC]] under default cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] and holds an individual document approval threshold of £250,000 gross including VAT[^r17][^r18]. As of 26 October 2026, she is responsible for procuring core components and packaging materials across production cost centres, including the upcoming [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] spring launch run[^r1][^r38][^r48].

## Role and responsibilities

In Ledgerline, Emma Clarke operates as purchase executive for operating unit NBC-MAN-01 with a document approval limit of £250,000 gross[^r17][^r18]. Under [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]], any commitment exceeding £250,000 gross escalates to higher management, including [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] or [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber]][^r18][^r19][^r48]. When planning battery purchases for the launch run, she noted that ordering 2,000 [[items-bat|BAT-500]] units at £119.00 would exceed her £250,000 limit and mandate sign-off by Daniel Okafor[^r48].

She serves as buyer across component families such as frames, braking, accessories, and packaging items, including [[items-mnt|MNT-FRM-01]], [[items-bel|BEL-STD-01]], [[items-brk|BRK-PAD-01]], and [[items-box|BOX-BIKE-01]][^r1]. In her day-to-day work from the Manchester office, she monitors frame replenishment orders, drive assemblies, and parts requisitions originating from the Leeds workshop, arranging peer cover with [[user-kmensah|Kwame Mensah]] during leave periods[^r40][^r41].
