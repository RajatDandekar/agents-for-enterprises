This operational decision establishes that warehouse teams moving parts and sub-assemblies between the Leeds unit and the Manchester plant must strictly use the standard transfer routine against transfer advice documents rather than booking goods out as adjustments[^r1][^r6]. The rule ensures stock is physically confirmed before dispatch and receipted immediately upon delivery to prevent inventory from floating unresolved on the ledger[^r2][^r3][^r7]. It applies across all transfers between the two sites, specifically targeting short-notice movements of critical items such as motors and battery casings[^r5].

## Context and Decision

Warehouse teams transferring parts and sub-assemblies between the Leeds unit and the Manchester plant were booking stock movements as manual inventory adjustments, leading to inventory quantities floating between Leeds and Manchester on the stock ledger[^r1][^r6][^r7]. Moving stock between Leeds and Manchester must use the standard transfer routine against transfer advice documents and must never be booked out as manual inventory adjustments[^r1][^r6]. Stores teams at both Leeds and Manchester must adhere to this unified transfer routine, particularly during short-notice movements of motors and battery casings[^r5].

## Dispatch and Receipt Procedures

Whenever a van is loaded at the Leeds unit, the dispatch note must be confirmed against physical stock before the vehicle departs[^r2]. Upon arrival and unloading at the Manchester plant, Manchester stores staff must receipt inter-plant loads directly against open transfer advice immediately, avoiding discrepancies where stock is consumed on the assembly line before clearing the ledger[^r3]. Taking stock off shelves for assembly before system transfer records are cleared causes inventory quantities to float between the two sites on the stock ledger[^r7].

## Discrepancies and In-Transit Damage

If items arrive damaged or crates do not match paperwork during transit between Leeds and Manchester, stores staff must record the details directly on the carrier sheet and raise a correction promptly in the system[^r4][^r8].
