The RIM-* item family comprises standard and double-wall aluminium bicycle rims across wheel sizes from 20 to 29 inches[^r1][^r2][^r9]. Inventory is maintained primarily at WH-MAN-STR, with additional stock for select double-wall variants held at WH-LDS-STR[^r2][^r9]. Open procurement and recent purchase orders for the family are sourced from supplier CAW-CHA-0146 Canalside Alloy Wheels Co., Ltd.[^r10][^r11][^r12]
