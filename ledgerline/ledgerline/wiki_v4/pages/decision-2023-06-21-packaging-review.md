Meeting notes and operational decisions from the packaging review held on 21 June 2023, chaired by [[user-jfletcher|Joanna Fletcher]][^r1]. The meeting addressed outer carton damage, warehouse staging bottlenecks, export crating dimensions, transit security, and retailer requests to phase out single-use plastics[^r2][^r4][^r6][^r8]. These decisions established corrective actions for packing lines across Manchester and Leeds facilities[^r3][^r5][^r9].

## Attendees

The review was chaired or owned by [[user-jfletcher|Joanna Fletcher]] and attended by Grace Whitaker, [[user-atanaka|Aiko Tanaka]], Marta Kowalski, Pieter Jansen, and Stefan Berg[^r1].

## Operational Findings and Decisions

Crushed corners on outer transit cartons delivered to independent bike shops were traced to borderline flute specifications under damp conditions during autumn transit combined with double-stacking by freight carriers[^r2]. In response to box integrity problems, Manchester dispatch staff had instituted manual tape banding on bike cartons, which slowed throughput on the packing line and resulted in an untidy arrival condition[^r3]. In addition, bulk packaging deliveries to Manchester were blocking gangways near goods inwards after warehouse layout changes, disrupting assembly line replenishment[^r4].

Inventory visibility across sites caused operational delays, including stockouts of battery packaging sleeves at the Leeds unit caused by untracked split deliveries between Manchester and Leeds[^r5].

Transit and export issues identified during the review included:
* **Export crating dimensions**: On export consignments of crated e-bikes secured to timber bases, specific frame sizes projected beyond the pallet footprint, catching curtainsider lorry sides and tearing the outer barrier film[^r6].
* **Internal carton stability**: Internal cardboard handlebar fittings shifted during road transit whenever packing staff omitted secondary fixing wedges on the assembly line[^r7].
* **Accessory and cable packaging**: Loose charger cables pierced inner carton walls in torn accessory boxes, while retailers requested the complete removal of single-use plastic bags for user manuals, pedals, and cable ties[^r8].
* **Sustainable packaging substitution**: An action item was assigned to Clare to introduce unbleached paper envelopes and folding card dividers for bicycle accessory packs and charger cartons, replacing single-use plastic bags and foam offcuts[^r9].
