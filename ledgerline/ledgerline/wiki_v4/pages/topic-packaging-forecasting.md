Packaging forecasting covers the generation of forward call-off schedules for protective packaging across Northbridge Cycles' assembly operations. Northbridge's packaging supplier requires forward call-off forecasts for corrugated bike cartons and foam inserts for both the Manchester works and the Leeds unit due to cardboard mill visibility demands [^r1]. Because Ledgerline maintains only firm carton orders rather than forward packaging requirements, purchasing staff must manually convert expected assembly figures into carton schedules [^r2].

## Packaging Demand Planning

Corrugated bike cartons and foam inserts require forward planning to meet lead-time and visibility demands imposed by cardboard paper mills supplying Northbridge's packaging vendor [^r1]. The visibility requirements apply across operations at both the Manchester works and the Leeds unit [^r1].

Because Ledgerline records only firm orders for cartons and does not project forward packaging requirements, demand figures are not generated automatically by the system [^r2]. Consequently, purchasing staff are required to take projected bike assembly numbers and manually calculate and translate them into carton call-off schedules to satisfy supplier requirements [^r2].
