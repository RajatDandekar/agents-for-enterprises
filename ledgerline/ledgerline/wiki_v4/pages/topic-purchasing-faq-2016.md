This topic covers the legacy purchasing frequently asked questions page exported from the former intranet server NBC-INTRANET01 [^r9]. It documents Northbridge Cycles' baseline 2016 procedures for raising material orders, handling supplier price changes, managing inter-site transit between Manchester and Leeds, processing inspection rejections, and onboarding new vendors [^r1][^r3][^r5][^r6][^r8]. The page serves as a historical operational reference and was last updated on 3 November 2016 by [[user-dramsden|Derek Ramsden (DRAMSDEN)]] [^r9].

## Order Raising for Production Materials

To raise an order for production materials, staff must create a purchase requisition against the approved bill of materials, which departmental heads confirm electronically before purchasing converts it into a formal purchase order [^r1]. Staff must not place verbal purchase orders or send offline notes because goods inward cannot book in deliveries without an active purchase order record [^r2].

## Supplier Price Changes

When a supplier notifies Northbridge of cost revisions for motors, battery cells, wiring looms, or frame components, staff must verify written notice under the supply agreement, notify the purchasing manager, and update master files before placing further orders [^r3]. Invoices exceeding the agreed purchase order value are placed on hold automatically by accounts pending formal investigation [^r4].

## Inter-Site Deliveries Between Manchester and Leeds

Deliveries destined for Manchester or Leeds must be routed to the delivery address on official paperwork, and components mistakenly dropped at Manchester require an internal transfer note for scheduled inter-site shuttle transport [^r5].

## Inspection Failures and Rejections

When delivered components fail inspection, an inspector records a rejection notice, moves the items to quarantine, purchasing contacts the vendor for collection and replacement or credit, and accounts withholds invoice clearance while the rejection flag is active [^r6]. Accounts will not clear an invoice for payment while an active inspection rejection flag remains on the ledger [^r7].

## New Supplier Onboarding

To set up a new supplier for specialized bicycle parts, shop consumables, or workshop services, staff must complete the standard vendor assessment form for purchasing validation of bank details, trade references, and quality certifications [^r8].
