POL-HR-020 version 6.1 defines working hours, attendance logging, leave entitlements, and absence notification rules across Northbridge Cycles' Manchester plant and Leeds unit [^r1][^r2]. Effective from 1 January 2026, it sets the standard working week at 37.5 hours and specifies an annual entitlement of 25 days leave plus bank holidays [^r1][^r2][^r4]. The policy governs mandatory telephone notification protocols for sickness and sets strict deadlines for carrying over unused holiday days [^r4][^r7][^r8].

## Clauses

### 1. Working hours
The standard working week for all permanent and temporary staff across the Manchester plant and Leeds unit is 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday [^r2]. Staff record their attendance through electronic clocking terminals or standard departmental timesheets, agreeing start and finish times with their line managers [^r3].

### 2. Annual leave
Full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave plus bank holidays, and may carry over up to 5 unused days until 31 March [^r4]. Any annual leave remaining beyond the 5 carried-over days is forfeited without payment, and all carried days expire if untaken by 31 March [^r5]. In assembly and warehouse sections, cover must be agreed on the shop floor before annual leave requests are entered into the system, and staff must not make non-refundable travel bookings until supervisor approval is confirmed [^r6].

### 3. Sickness
Employees who are ill must telephone their line manager directly by 09:30 on the first day of absence, and provide a medical fit note after 7 calendar days [^r7]. Notification of sickness absence must be made by telephone rather than by text message, e-mail, or verbal message via a co-worker [^r8]. Upon resuming work after illness, employees must attend a return-to-work interview with their supervisor and sign an internal self-certification record [^r9].

### 4. Other leave
For scheduled civic or statutory leave, requests and supporting documentation must be submitted to the department supervisor as soon as known, whereas unexpected emergencies require calling the line manager before the working day starts [^r10].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Carry-over of unused annual leave**: Under version 6.1 effective 2026-01-01, full-time staff may carry forward up to 5 unused days into the next holiday year until 31 March without needing special approval, while untaken days beyond that cap are forfeited without payment [^r4][^r5]. Under the 15 December 2021 annual leave reminder, untaken annual leave could not generally be carried forward into the next holiday year unless exceptional prior written approval was secured before period end, and payment in lieu was prohibited [^r11][^r12].
