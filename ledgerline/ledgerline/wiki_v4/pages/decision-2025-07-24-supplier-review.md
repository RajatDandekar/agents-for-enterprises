Operational decisions agreed during the supplier review meeting held on 24 July 2025 by [[user-jfletcher|Joanna Fletcher]], [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]], and [[user-atanaka|Aiko Tanaka]][^r1]. The meeting established procedural controls across goods-in staging, batch quality certification, freight and material surcharges, Leeds warehouse packaging, and drawing distribution[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6]. These operational rules govern how dock receiving, engineering revisions, and Ledgerline invoice matching handle supplier discrepancies today[^r2][^r4][^r6].

## Context

On 24 July 2025, [[user-jfletcher|Joanna Fletcher]], [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]], and [[user-atanaka|Aiko Tanaka]] conducted a regular supplier review covering delivery documentation discrepancies, frame fitting quality variances, unnotified price surcharges, packaging standards, and engineering drawing releases[^r1].

## Decisions

### Goods-in Staging and Receipts
Because Ledgerline ERP does not permit discrepancies to remain open once receipts are booked, goods-in dock workers must hold shipments in the staging area until supplier advice notes are cross-checked against open purchase orders[^r2].

### Quality and Inspection Certificates
Suppliers must provide inspection certificates alongside delivery notes for every production batch rather than relying on Northbridge internal rectification of dimensional variances[^r3].

### Purchase Order Tolerances and Surcharges
Unannounced freight or raw material surcharges applied by suppliers halt the [[sop-ap-001|invoice matching]] process in Ledgerline ERP because values exceed recorded purchase order tolerances[^r4].

### Packaging Standards at Leeds Assembly
At the Leeds assembly unit, mixed part types consolidated into single unmarked cartons and damaged packaging created backlogs on unloading bays and discrepancies with the stock ledger[^r5].

### Engineering Drawing Releases
Engineering drawing revisions must be issued exclusively through the controlled document procedure to prevent suppliers from manufacturing obsolete revisions after informal e-mail distribution[^r6].
