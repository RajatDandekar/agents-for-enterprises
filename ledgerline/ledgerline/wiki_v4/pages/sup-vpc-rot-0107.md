Voltway Paints & Coatings is an active vendor based in Rotterdam, Netherlands, supplying paint and coatings under supplier code VPC-ROT-0107 [^r1][^r3]. Although sharing a partial name prefix with [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] in Eindhoven, it is legally and operationally an independent entity with distinct registration details [^r2][^r5]. All standard procurement transactions with this vendor are processed on N30 payment terms [^r1][^r3].

## Record

Voltway Paints & Coatings is listed in [[data-supplier-master|SUPPLIER_MASTER]] under supplier code VPC-ROT-0107 [^r1]. The supplier is marked with status ACTIVE in Rotterdam, Netherlands, and is registered under VAT number NL814205366B01 [^r1][^r3]. The primary contact person on file is Joost Bakker, reachable by email at orders@vpc-rot-0107.example [^r1]. Agreed standard commercial payment terms are N30 [^r1][^r3].

Under [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]], Voltway Paints & Coatings in Rotterdam is recognized as an independent supplier separate from Voltway Cells in Eindhoven because each entity holds a different VAT number and is registered in a different city [^r2][^r5].

## Certificates

No quality or compliance certificates are recorded for this supplier.

## Rate contracts

No rate contracts are currently recorded in the active catalogue for this supplier.

## Notes

Procurement staff handling supplier verification on [[screen-pur-sup-03|PUR-SUP-03]] or [[screen-pur-sup-01|PUR-SUP-01]] should ensure that orders are not misdirected to [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] or legacy supplier codes such as [[sup-vc-ein-0009|VC-EIN-0009]], as both entities maintain separate tax registrations and addresses [^r2].

## Versions and conflicts

Voltway Paints & Coatings has maintained code VPC-ROT-0107 with active N30 terms in the current supplier register [^r1][^r3], consistent with historic master data in superseded export `supplier_master_EXPORT_2024.csv` [^r4]. The legal distinction between Voltway Paints & Coatings and Voltway Cells was documented in superseded policy POL-SUP-003 v2.2 [^r6] before being carried forward into the current [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]] v2.3 [^r2].
