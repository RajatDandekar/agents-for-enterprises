POL-TEX-007 defines Northbridge Cycles' travel rules and expense reimbursement rates for staff, temporary workers, and contractors travelling on company business [^r1][^r2]. Version 2.4 remains in force until 1 January 2027 and establishes mileage bands, accommodation caps, subsistence rates, and claim procedures [^r1][^r4][^r6][^r7][^r18]. Claims must be submitted within 60 days via Ledgerline screen [[screen-ap-exp-09|AP-EXP-09]] with line manager approval and itemised receipts [^r11].

## Clauses

### 1. Scope

The policy covers all permanent employees, temporary workers, and contractors operating from the Manchester factory, the Leeds unit, or travelling on company business [^r2]. Staff must secure prior approval from their department head before arranging any journey [^r3]. Staff who volunteer to attend trade exhibition stands receive travel, accommodation, and subsistence coverage under this standard company policy [^r13].

### 2. Mileage

Business journeys made using a private car are reimbursed at 45p per mile for the initial 10,000 miles within a tax year, reducing to 25p per mile thereafter [^r4]. Claimants must record mileage claims on the standard travel expense form, specifying starting location, destination, business purpose, and distance [^r5]. Each claim requires the signature of the claimant's line manager prior to submission to payroll [^r5].

### 3. Hotels and meals

Hotel bookings are limited to £140 per night outside London and £190 per night within London, with breakfast included [^r6]. The subsistence allowance for meals during overnight trips is £35 per day and must be backed by itemised receipts [^r7].

### 4. Flights and rail

Flights with a scheduled duration of under six hours must be booked in economy class [^r8]. Rail journeys must be taken in standard class unless travel duration exceeds three hours, and any travel between the Manchester and Leeds sites must always be booked in standard class [^r9]. Bookings must be completed via the accounts office using the designated corporate travel account rather than paid on personal cards [^r10].

### 5. Claims

Expense claims must be entered on screen [[screen-ap-exp-09|AP-EXP-09]] within 60 days of incurring the expense, supported by an itemised receipt for every line item exceeding £10, and approved by the line manager [^r11]. Attached paper receipts must clearly display the business reason for each expense item [^r14]. Travel expenses incurred on company business require line manager sign-off before paperwork is lodged with the finance office [^r15]. Following manager approval entry in Ledgerline, the claim moves automatically to the purchase ledger clerk for payment processing [^r12]. Claims submitted late risk deferral to the subsequent processing run for bank reimbursement [^r16]. Employees who misplace a receipt or require a blank copy of the standard claim sheet can request help from the accounts office [^r17].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Accommodation and subsistence rates (future_dated):** Today under v2.4, overnight hotel ceilings are £140 outside London and £190 in London (inclusive of breakfast), and overnight subsistence is £35 per day [^r6][^r7]. From 1 January 2027 under v3.0, nightly hotel limits increase to £150 outside London and £210 in London, the subsistence rate increases to £40 per day, and written approval from the department head is required to exceed room ceilings [^r18][^r19][^r20][^r23].
* **Claim submission window and documentation (future_dated):** Today under v2.4, claims must be entered on screen [[screen-ap-exp-09|AP-EXP-09]] within 60 days of the expense, and receipts are mandatory for lines over £10 [^r11]. From 1 January 2027 under v3.0, the entry window shortens to 45 days, and claims must include original itemised VAT receipts rather than card counterfoils [^r21][^r22].
