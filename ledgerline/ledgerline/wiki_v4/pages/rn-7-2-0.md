Ledgerline ERP 7.2.0 is the major platform release deployed across Northbridge Cycles' operational sites in April 2023 [^r1][^r9]. It established core system infrastructure including electronic purchasing approval workflows, battery serial tracking, automated end-of-line testing capture, and service account key management [^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5]. Today, these functions underpin day-to-day manufacturing operations, procurement approvals, and shop-floor quality logging [^r2][^r3][^r4].

## Changes

Ledgerline ERP 7.2.0 introduced several operational capabilities across modules:

* **Purchasing approval inbox**: Introduced screen [[screen-pur-poa-04|PUR-POA-04]] for managing requests and purchase orders with automated e-mail alerts routed to designated approvers [^r2].
* **Lot and serial tracking**: Added screen [[screen-inv-lot-10|INV-LOT-10]] to track individual battery pack serial numbers across intake, line-side issue, and frame fitting [^r3].
* **End-of-line test capture**: Added screen [[screen-qa-btt-03|QA-BTT-03]] to capture battery test results loaded directly from physical test rigs via background processing [^r4].
* **Integration authentication**: Added screen [[screen-sys-api-14|SYS-API-14]] for service account key management, replacing standard user passwords for external integrations and background jobs [^r5].

The release also noted several known issues:

* Dot-matrix print queues overrun allotted column widths on the stock valuation summary report, causing right-hand totals to wrap onto subsequent lines [^r6].
* Sales order entry account searches experience delays when querying customer accounts that possess extensive transaction histories [^r7].
* Terminal displays occasionally fail to refresh overall works order status automatically following the posting of a completed assembly batch, requiring operators to press the redisplay key [^r8].

## Effect at Northbridge

The Ledgerline 7.2.0 release was made available on 17 April 2023 for all Ledgerline 7 customers, covering both the Manchester assembly plant and the Leeds unit [^r1]. At Northbridge Cycles, deployment was scheduled during the [[decision-2023-02-14-ledgerline-7-2-upgrade|Easter weekend 2023]] rollout window, activating the electronic approval inbox workflow across procurement operations [^r9].

The functional additions altered multiple standard operational workflows:

* Procurement requests and purchase orders moved to automated electronic routing via screen [[screen-pur-poa-04|PUR-POA-04]], supporting [[sop-pur-002|SOP-PUR-002]] [^r2].
* Assembly line operations began logging battery serial numbers directly through screen [[screen-inv-lot-10|INV-LOT-10]] from receiving to frame assembly [^r3].
* Quality control battery testing rigs feed directly into screen [[screen-qa-btt-03|QA-BTT-03]] via automated background tasks [^r4].
* System integrations and automated background scripts switched from password-based credentials to managed keys using screen [[screen-sys-api-14|SYS-API-14]] in alignment with [[sop-it-002|SOP-IT-002]] [^r5].
