Bronwen Price ([[user-bprice|BPRICE]]) oversees site operations at Northbridge Cycles' Leeds unit [^r1]. She coordinates warehouse space management, inter-site logistics, and prep operations between Leeds and Manchester [^r4][^r9][^r11]. In operational governance, she leads site-level committee reviews covering safety, supplier delivery performance, and operational steering [^r2][^r3][^r6].

## Operations and site management

Price represented site operations at the production planning meeting on 2022-03-03 [^r1]. She attended the [[decision-2025-03-18-leeds-unit-update|Leeds unit update]] meeting on 18 March 2025 [^r5]. At the sales meeting on 10 March 2024, Price confirmed that frame preparation turnaround at the Leeds unit remained consistent, supporting assembly lines without disruption [^r4].

To manage storage congestion at the Leeds unit, Price proposed on 21 October 2024 to relocate slower-moving spare parts into the secondary room near the trade counter to keep dispatch gangways clear [^r9]. In the same communication, she requested that the Manchester plant provide earlier advance notice of bulk inventory transfers through the core system to permit floor space to be cleared before goods arrive [^r10].

## Inter-site stock transfers

On 9 September 2026, Price instructed [[user-cshaw|Callum Shaw]] to enforce standard transfer routines for all inter-site stock movements between Leeds and Manchester rather than booking stock adjustment write-outs [^r11].

## Quality and supplier oversight

Price owned the [[decision-2022-10-21-supplier-review|supplier review]] held on 2022-10-21 concerning inbound component deliveries and supplier performance [^r2]. At the weekly purchasing meeting on 24 January 2026, she addressed defective battery cell casings stamped with burrs received at the Leeds unit, which caused uneven running across preparation benches and necessitated manual deburring [^r7][^r8]. This led to an agreed action to schedule an on-site review with the stamping firm's technical representative [^r8].

## Committee and steering roles

Price acted as the owner of and attended the Health and Safety Committee meeting on 2023-12-25 [^r3]. She also attended and owned the [[decision-2026-01-11-it-steering|IT steering]] meeting held on 2026-01-11 [^r6].
