## Paint shop curing and preparation

Completed frame assemblies were rejected at final inspection due to uneven lacquer coverage and runs along the bottom bracket shell[^r2]. These defects were caused by fluctuating curing humidity and incorrect solvent cloth usage in prep[^r2]. Colin was assigned to review curing bay extraction and drying controls with the maintenance engineer and issue revised cleaning cloth handling notices in the prep area[^r3].

## Wheel tension calibration and warranty claims

An increase in warranty claims from independent bike shops involved front hub noise and loose spokes resulting from uncalibrated mechanical wheel tension testing[^r4]. David was tasked with arranging immediate recalibration of the spoke tension gauge and verifying operator log sheets[^r5]. Sarah was assigned to draft a technical guidance bulletin for bike shops covering hub adjustment warranty claims[^r5].

## Leeds to Manchester goods-in transit bay stock

Delivery notes from the Leeds unit caused stock ledger anomalies where physical pallets sat in Manchester transit bays while system stock showed nil, prompting emergency purchase requests[^r6]. Graham was assigned to perform a full bin count in the Manchester goods-in transit bay to eliminate ledger discrepancies[^r7].

## Battery housing clips and press tooling

City commuter assembly lines experienced damaged plastic retaining clips on battery housings because operators forced alloy locking plates into downtube recesses using soft mallets due to worn stamping press locating pins[^r8]. Colin was assigned to inspect the bracket stamping press and replace the worn locating pins[^r9]. David was assigned to update the assembly station method sheet to prohibit mallet use during battery latch fitting[^r9].

## Handlebar display cable routing

Retailer bike shops caused pinched wires at full fork lock when routing handlebar display auxiliary cables[^r10]. Sarah was assigned to amend the retailer workshop service sheet to illustrate correct frame routing paths for these cables[^r10].
