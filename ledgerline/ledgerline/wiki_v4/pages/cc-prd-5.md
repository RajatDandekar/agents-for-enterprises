Cost centre PRD-5 is a legacy Ledgerline 6.x cost centre for the City line that is designated as do not use [^r1]. The cost centre was closed on 2019-03-31 and cannot be used for current postings or requisitions [^r1]. It remains listed in the master records under owner GSUTCLIFFE and operating unit NBC-MAN-01 [^r1].

## Details

Cost centre PRD-5 is recorded under unit NBC-MAN-01 with GSUTCLIFFE assigned as the owner [^r1]. The account carries a status of CLOSED effective 2019-03-31 [^r1].
