This page documents the historical 2016 product range specifications for electric bicycles produced by Northbridge Cycles across its manufacturing sites [^r1]. It covers the engineering configurations, factory assembly allocations, and component baselines for the company's three distinct frame styles [^r1][^r2][^r4]. It also preserves the purchasing consultation requirements enforced when suppliers alter powertrain or electrical interface dimensions [^r7].

## Production Overview

Northbridge Cycles manufactures three distinct styles of electric bicycles across the Manchester plant and the Leeds unit: urban commuter, low-step utility, and trekking and touring models [^r1].

## Model Configurations

### Urban Commuter

The urban commuter model constitutes the largest share of daily assembly at the Manchester plant [^r2]. Its mechanical and drive specification includes a rigid alloy frame, an integrated bottom-bracket drive system, an internal main-tube battery, internal hub gearing, and hydraulic rim calipers [^r2]. Standard factory equipment fitted to this machine includes full-length mudguards, an alloy rear pannier rack, integrated lighting powered directly by the central battery pack, swept handlebars, an adjustable stem, and puncture-resistant road tyres [^r3].

### Low-Step Utility

Assembly of the low-step utility model is carried out primarily at the Leeds unit [^r4]. The powertrain and braking system comprise a front hub motor, an external battery mounted beneath the rear carrier rack, an enclosed chain case, and front and rear roller brakes [^r4]. Completed low-step utility bicycles are delivered to retail stockists equipped with a wide gel saddle, a front wicker basket support, and an extra-wide double kickstand [^r5].

### Trekking and Touring

The trekking and touring model is engineered with a reinforced diamond frame, front suspension forks, a high-torque mid-motor, external derailleur gearing with a multi-sprocket cassette, hydraulic disc brakes, and all-terrain tyres [^r6]. 

## Purchasing Controls

Purchasing personnel must consult the engineering office prior to issuing purchase orders if component suppliers notify Northbridge Cycles of any modifications affecting motor mountings, battery casing dimensions, or sensor plugs [^r7].
