This page catalogues the `ERR-KBN` error codes generated by Ledgerline inventory kanban processing services as of 26 October 2026. These error codes are raised across kanban card retrieval, signalling, and closure workflows. Individual codes link to dedicated error pages for full operational diagnostics and resolution procedures.

### ERR-KBN-0001
* **Message**: KANBAN CARD NOT FOUND [^r1][^r3][^r4]
* **Cause**: The requested kanban card cannot be found by the system [^r3].
* **Remedy**: See [[err-kbn-0001|ERR-KBN-0001 Kanban Card Not Found or Duplicate Signal]].
* **Raised by**: `kbn_close` [^r1], `kbn_get` [^r3], and `kbn_signal` [^r4].

### ERR-KBN-0801
* **Message**: KANBAN CARD CLOSED (<status>) [^r2]
* **Cause**: The kanban card is in a closed status during service execution [^r2].
* **Remedy**: See [[err-kbn-0801|ERR-KBN-0801 Kanban Card Status Transition or Warehouse Validation Error]].
* **Raised by**: `kbn_close` [^r2].
