Operational and supplier management decisions recorded during the weekly purchasing meeting on 16 October 2022.[^r1] The meeting addressed delivery delays at the Manchester plant, casing quality defects at Leeds, procedural rules for warehouse transfer ticket entry, and supplier freight booking compliance.[^r2][^r4][^r7][^r9] It also established the rejection of an uncontracted energy price uplift on aluminium rim sections.[^r8]

## Decisions and operational actions

### Inward deliveries and plant staging
Wiring harness and motor mounting deliveries to the Manchester plant experienced ongoing supplier staffing constraints, resulting in late arrivals and congestion across the staging area near the main roller shutters.[^r2] Purchasing took the action to contact the wiring harness supplier to enforce a revised delivery timetable for all open orders remaining on the system.[^r3] In addition, the contracted road haulier for palletised inward freight repeatedly missed morning delivery booking slots at Manchester, causing vehicles to be turned away, delaying receipt processing, and distorting system lead-time calculations.[^r9] Purchasing scheduled an immediate review of delivery logs with the haulage contractor to reinforce mandatory advance booking compliance.[^r10]

### Component quality and supplier reviews
Battery cell casings delivered to the Leeds facility displayed inconsistent stamping quality, with burrs preventing correct seating and forcing consignment rejections as well as the diversion of floor personnel to manual deburring.[^r4] An on-site technical inspection was arranged at the Leeds unit with the stamping supplier's technical representative to evaluate the rejected crates.[^r5]

### Inventory transfers and requisition controls
Discrepancies arose between physical stock counts and material requirements planning screens for alloy tubing and headset bearings because inventory movements between the primary Manchester warehouse and sub-assembly lines were not recorded at movement time.[^r6] To prevent the generation of duplicate or unnecessary automated purchase requisitions, stores supervisors were instructed that all stock transfer tickets must be posted in the system prior to the end of each operational shift.[^r7]

### Commercial terms and maintenance sourcing
A formal request from the primary rim supplier for price increases on cold-drawn aluminium sections, submitted citing increased energy tariffs, was formally rejected based on existing annual contract terms.[^r8] Separately, replacement drive belts for the paint-line overhead conveyor were redirected to a specialist distributor after the incumbent standard trade vendor ceased holding local inventory.[^r11]
