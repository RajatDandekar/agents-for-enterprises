The `TYR-*` family comprises bicycle tyres used across Northbridge Cycles production lines and service stock. As of 26 October 2026 at 09:00 GMT, inventory is distributed between warehouses WH-MAN-STR and WH-LDS-STR, covering city (CTY), trekking (TRK), mountain bike (MTB), and puncture-resistant (PCT) variants in multiple rim sizes and widths [^r1][^r3][^r11]. Quantities on hand, active allocations, and replenishment orders are tracked in [[data-stock-on-hand|Stock_on_hand_2026-10-26_0900.xlsx]] [^r1][^r28].
