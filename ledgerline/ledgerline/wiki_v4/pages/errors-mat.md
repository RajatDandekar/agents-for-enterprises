`ERR-MAT` error codes are generated by Ledgerline ERP 7.2 during three-way invoice matching across accounts payable services and entry screens [^r1][^r12]. They stop invoice posting when values, quantities, or reference documents diverge beyond permitted thresholds or when required roles are missing [^r2][^r3][^r17]. As of 26 October 2026, standard matching services enforce these validation errors across invoice entry and matching screens such as [[screen-ap-mat-02|AP-MAT-02]] [^r8][^r18].

### ERR-MAT-0001

* **Message**: THREE-WAY MATCH NOT FOUND [^r1][^r12][^r15][^r16]
* **Cause**: Indicates a general matching engine failure, concurrent record locking, an attempt to execute `mat_override` with a blank `COMMENT` field, or that no three-way match record exists [^r1][^r7][^r15][^r16].
* **Remedy**: Ensure referenced records are unlocked, verify match parameters, and supply a valid reason in the `COMMENT` field when submitting an override [^r7].
* **Raised by**: [[services-ap-mat|mat_get]] [^r15], [[services-ap-mat|mat_override]] [^r16].

### ERR-MAT-0410

* **Message**: MATCH FAILED: PRICE OUTSIDE TOLERANCE [^r2][^r12][^r13][^r18]
* **Cause**: The net figure entered in invoice entry differs from agreed purchase order line values beyond established company tolerance limits [^r2][^r5][^r18].
* **Remedy**: Review invoice line pricing against the purchase order, adjust variances within permitted tolerances, or escalate for authorised price approval [^r5].
* **Raised by**: [[services-ap-inv|inv_post]] [^r13], [[services-ap-mat|mat_run]] [^r18].

### ERR-MAT-0411

* **Message**: MATCH FAILED: QTY INVOICED > QTY RECEIVED [^r3][^r12][^r14][^r19]
* **Cause**: Invoiced quantity or billed value exceeds the unbilled confirmed goods receipt balance recorded by goods inward for the specified purchase order number [^r3][^r6][^r19].
* **Remedy**: Verify received quantities with the warehouse, confirm pending goods receipt notes, or request a revised invoice from the vendor [^r6].
* **Raised by**: [[services-ap-inv|inv_post]] [^r14], [[services-ap-mat|mat_run]] [^r19].

### ERR-MAT-0412

* **Message**: NO GRN FOR PO LINE [^r4][^r12][^r20]
* **Cause**: Attempting invoice matching against a purchase order line for which no goods receipt note (GRN) exists [^r4][^r20].
* **Remedy**: Await warehouse receipt processing and generation of the GRN before attempting matching or invoice posting [^r4][^r20].
* **Raised by**: [[services-ap-mat|mat_run]] [^r20].

### ERR-MAT-0420

* **Message**: OVERRIDE NEEDS AP-MGR ROLE [^r12][^r17]
* **Cause**: An accounts payable match override was attempted by a user account lacking the required `AP-MGR` role [^r12][^r17].
* **Remedy**: Request an override from an authorised accounts payable manager holding the `AP-MGR` role [^r17].
* **Raised by**: [[services-ap-mat|mat_override]] [^r17]. Link: [[err-mat-0420|ERR-MAT-0420]].

## Versions and conflicts

* **ERR-MAT-0410**: Under the standard service definitions, [[data-data-dictionary|data dictionary]], and [[man-ap-72|MAN-AP-72]] system messages, ERR-MAT-0410 represents `MATCH FAILED: PRICE OUTSIDE TOLERANCE` (variance exceeding tolerance) [^r2][^r5][^r12][^r13][^r18]. In the screen-level message reference for [[screen-ap-mat-02|AP-MAT-02]] on page 9 of MAN-AP-72, it is instead documented as an invalid, unapproved, cancelled, or missing purchase order [^r8] (`contradiction`).
* **ERR-MAT-0411**: In service definitions and data dictionary tables, ERR-MAT-0411 represents `MATCH FAILED: QTY INVOICED > QTY RECEIVED` [^r3][^r6][^r12][^r14][^r19]. In the AP-MAT-02 screen message reference on page 9 of MAN-AP-72, it is defined as no corresponding goods receipt note existing for the line [^r9] (`contradiction`).
* **ERR-MAT-0412**: Service definitions, system tables, and Section 20 define ERR-MAT-0412 as `NO GRN FOR PO LINE` [^r4][^r12][^r20]. The screen reference on page 9 of MAN-AP-72 defines ERR-MAT-0412 as a price variance exceeding tolerance (`TOL %`) [^r10] (`contradiction`).
* **ERR-MAT-0420**: Service definitions and Section 20 list ERR-MAT-0420 as `OVERRIDE NEEDS AP-MGR ROLE` [^r12][^r17]. The AP-MAT-02 screen reference on page 9 of MAN-AP-72 defines ERR-MAT-0420 as a quantity variance exceeding confirmed warehouse receipts [^r11] (`contradiction`).
