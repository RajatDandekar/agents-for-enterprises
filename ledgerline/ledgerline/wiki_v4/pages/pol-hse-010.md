POL-HSE-010 establishes overall health and safety responsibilities and baseline operational safety controls for Northbridge Cycles [^r1][^r2]. It governs plant safety rules across Manchester assembly and battery operations and the Leeds distribution warehouse, setting mandatory procedures for mechanical handling, lithium battery fire response, first aid, and site access [^r1][^r3][^r5][^r7]. As of 26 October 2026, compliance requirements on site incorporate annual forklift refresher training mandates for Manchester drivers alongside operational actions confirmed by the Health and Safety Committee [^r27][^r34].

## Clauses

### 1. Statement
POL-HSE-010 is version 5.0, effective from 2025-01-01, owned by HSE, approved by plant head [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber]], and designated as INTERNAL [^r1].

### 2. Responsibilities
Northbridge Cycles' managing director retains overall accountability for company safety standards, ensuring adequate funding and equipment are provided [^r2]. Operational managers and unit supervisors hold direct responsibility for their local floors, overseeing mechanical handling, assembly lines, battery storage, and packaging in Manchester, and warehousing, despatch, and crated bicycle handling in Leeds [^r3]. When hazards or near misses occur, staff must notify their supervisor immediately, and details must be transcribed into the central safety log on Ledgerline ERP without delay so corrective maintenance can be raised with the works engineer [^r4].

### 3. Forklifts
Under clause 3.1, only staff with a current forklift licence, renewed every 3 years, may drive a forklift [^r5]. Approved forklift licence holders collect ignition keys from the stores office, and operators must complete a physical walkaround check logging tyre condition, hydraulic fluid levels, horn operation, and steering response before starting a shift [^r6].

### 4. Battery fires
Under clause 4.1, if a battery pack smokes or burns, staff must raise the alarm, leave the area, and call the fire wardens, and only trained fire wardens may approach a burning pack [^r7]. Standard water or carbon dioxide extinguishers are ineffective on burning battery packs and can cause dangerous flare-ups [^r8].

### 5. First aid
Marked green first aid stations containing treated dressings, eye washes, and bandages are positioned throughout the workshops, warehouse aisles, and general offices, supported by a dedicated rest space at each site [^r9]. Every first aid attendance must be recorded in the incident log, and broken seals on emergency boxes must be reported immediately for restocking [^r10].

### 6. Visitors and contractors
All guests, agency staff, and trade contractors must report to main reception to sign the register, collect a visitor pass, and complete a site induction covering evacuation routes, assembly points, and hazards associated with battery assembly lines and testing rigs [^r11]. High-visibility waistcoats and safety footwear are mandatory outside office corridors, and hosts must supervise visitors continuously unless an independent contractor holds an approved permit to work [^r12].

## Operational warehouse and facility rules

Mandatory health and safety arrangements apply to all staff visiting or working within the warehouse and goods inwards bays at both the Manchester factory and the Leeds distribution unit [^r13]. Purchasing department and commercial office staff must never enter operational warehouse areas without personal protective equipment, which is kept in designated lockers near pedestrian entry doors [^r14]. High-visibility vests or jackets must be fastened and worn at all times, safety footwear with protective toe caps must be worn past office boundary lines, and eye protection and gloves must be obtained from the shift supervisor when inspecting unboxed mechanical parts or raw metal tubing [^r15].

Pedestrians in handling areas must keep to painted walkways, use designated crossings, never enter an aisle where an overhead reach truck is actively working, make eye contact with drivers before passing stationary equipment, avoid using mobile phones or handheld computers while walking, and never walk beneath elevated forks [^r16]. Staff must use mechanical lifting aids or team lifting for crated frames, drive units, or master cartons of hub motors, must keep spines straight and bend knees when examining lower shelving, and must never climb storage racking to inspect stock, using instead approved order-picker equipment operated by trained staff [^r17]. Loose strapping, timber pallets, and protective shrink-wrap must be cleared immediately into recycling skips to prevent trip hazards [^r18]. All spills, accidental impacts, and near misses must be recorded promptly in the physical incident register kept at the supervisor desk [^r19].

## Facility controls and safety committee actions

At the Manchester plant, clear gangways must be maintained around battery charging bays at all times to prevent trip hazards and reduce fire risk, and surplus cell packaging must be cleared to external recycling skips at the end of each shift [^r20][^r21][^r27][^r28]. Floor markings around charging bays were scheduled for repainting, and obscured emergency sand bucket signage in the Manchester battery holding area requires keeping pallet trucks clear [^r21][^r28]. Torn flexible ducting on local exhaust ventilation units in the Manchester fettling and frame finishing area requires replacement from maintenance stores to control dust levels [^r24][^r31]. To address neck discomfort reported by stores personnel using the Ledgerline ERP terminal in the Manchester warehouse, an adjustable workstation arm was assigned to replace a filing cabinet monitor mount [^r26][^r33]. Assembly line hand and wrist strain caused by down-tube internal cable routing is addressed by trialling tilted assembly jigs and rotating operators across wiring stations and mechanical sub-assembly [^r23][^r30].

At the Leeds distribution unit, delivery drivers must be instructed to adhere to designated pedestrian walkways rather than crossing the forklift operating zone, and the loading bay convex mirror beside the main roller shutter door must be cleaned and adjusted [^r22][^r29]. Grates on the external drainage channel near the test rig wash-down bay and fire exit at Leeds are checked on the weekly facilities checklist to prevent pooling water and slip hazards [^r25][^r32].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Forklift refresher training cadence:** Mandatory site compliance rules issued by email on 2026-03-04 require all counterbalance and reach truck operators in Manchester to undergo annual refresher training and pre-shift inspection reviews [^r34]. This operational mandate overrides the 3-year licence renewal cycle stated in POL-HSE-010 clause 3.1 [^r5]. (kind: `email_overrides_sop`)
* **Incident logging format:** POL-HSE-010 clause 2 specifies that hazard and near-miss details must be transcribed into the central safety log on Ledgerline ERP without delay [^r4]. Training documentation states that spills, impacts, and near misses must be recorded in the physical incident register kept at the supervisor desk [^r19]. (kind: `contradiction`)
* **Health and safety committee minutes duplication:** The meeting minutes of 2026-06-10 repeat near-verbatim the exact workplace safety actions recorded in the minutes of 2023-12-25, covering battery gangway clearance, sand bucket signage, Leeds yard walkways, down-tube wiring ergonomics, fettling LEV ducting, wash-down drainage, and stores terminal mounting [^r20][^r21][^r22][^r23][^r24][^r25][^r26][^r27][^r28][^r29][^r30][^r31][^r32][^r33].
