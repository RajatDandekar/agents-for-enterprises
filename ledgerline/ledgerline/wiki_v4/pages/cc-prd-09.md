Cost centre PRD-09 covers battery refurbishment operations at Northbridge Cycles within operational unit NBC-MAN-01 under owner [[user-pjansen|Pieter Jansen]][^r1]. It serves as a mandatory processing destination for returned warranty battery packs and quarantined stock requiring rework[^r7][^r8]. As of 26 October 2026 09:00, the cost centre is OPEN with an available budget of £486,932.90 for period 2026-Q4[^r1][^r2].

## Operations and workflow

All warranty battery packs returned from bike shops must be quarantined immediately upon arrival and routed directly to battery refurbishment under PRD-09 or scrapped for recycling, and must never be returned to general inventory stock[^r7]. Under quality procedures, cost centre PRD-09 represents one of three official disposition routes determined by [[cc-qa-01|Quality]] within 24 hours of quarantine, alongside recycling or return to supplier[^r8]. The cost centre processes refurbished packs, handling stock associated with [[items-bat|BAT-* items]][^r7][^r8].
