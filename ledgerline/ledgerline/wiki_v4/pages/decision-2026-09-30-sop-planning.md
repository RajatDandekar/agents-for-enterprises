On 30 September 2026, the Sales and Operations Planning meeting set the line transition plan between legacy production and the Medlock 2 spring launch run [^r1][^r2]. As of today, 26 October 2026, cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] is closed and cost centre [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] is open for pre-series setup expenses [^r3][^r5]. Launch assembly runs for the new platform take place in January 2027 [^r2].

## Context and attendees

Henrik Weber, Isla Morgan, Stefan Berg, Daniel Okafor, and Grace Whitaker attended the S&OP meeting on 30 September 2026 [^r1]. The group convened to plan factory readiness, staging space, and delivery schedules for the Medlock 2 ramp [^r1][^r2][^r6][^r7].

## Line transition and cost centre adjustments

Cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] closed on 15 October 2026, requiring all open assembly orders tied to legacy frames to be settled or written off by that date [^r5]. Obsolete jigs, legacy frame fixtures, and physical inventory from the closing [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] line are relocated to the Leeds unit to release staging bays in Manchester [^r6]. Cost centre [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] opened on 1 October 2026 to allow non-capitalised workshop consumables and maintenance call-outs to be charged prior to initial pre-series validation [^r3].

## Production ramp and material scheduling

The Medlock 2 spring launch run begins assembly in January 2027 [^r2]. Once running at full rate, the launch run is planned to produce approximately 600 [[items-bat|battery packs]] a day [^r4]. To prevent warehouse staff from exceeding battery store floor loading limits, raw cell consignments must arrive in planned weekly drops [^r7].

## Versions and conflicts

- **Launch build timing**: As of 26 October 2026, [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] is open exclusively for preparatory maintenance and consumable bookings [^r3]. The production build for the Medlock 2 spring launch run starts in January 2027, eventually reaching 600 packs per day [^r2][^r4] (`future_dated`).
