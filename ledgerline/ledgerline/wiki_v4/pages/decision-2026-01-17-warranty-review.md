The warranty review held on 17 January 2026 established corrective technical, assembly, and administrative actions to address recurring customer warranty claims and bike shop disputes [^r1]. It assigned operational responsibilities across manufacturing lines, engineering records, parts reconciliation, and bike shop intake procedures [^r2][^r5][^r6][^r9]. These decisions govern quality checks on Manchester frame runs and intake requirements for bike shop warranty claims [^r3][^r9].

## Attendees

The regular warranty review on 17 January 2026 was attended by [[user-cshaw|Callum Shaw]], Joanna Fletcher, Grace Whitaker, Marta Kowalski, and Aiko Tanaka [^r1].

## Decisions and action items

* **Frame welding and dropout inspection:** The quality engineer was assigned to review jig calibration records and inspect welded assemblies prior to paint after customer returns cited premature belt-drive wear and rear hub tracking issues caused by slight dropout deviation on certain production runs [^r2].
* **Drive-unit mounting verification:** Following investigation into recurring bottom bracket chatter, the assembly supervisor was assigned to inspect drive-unit mounting bolts on all frames departing the Manchester line to verify the external mounting interface before dispatch [^r3].
* **Down-tube damping service bulletin:** The technical services lead was assigned to issue an interim service bulletin to approved bike shops introducing an updated rubber damping strip inside the down-tube housing to stop battery pack vibration and intermittent electrical cut-outs [^r4].
* **Battery cradle drawing update:** A design engineer was tasked with submitting an updated drawing in Ledgerline ERP for the battery cradle mounting bracket because the standard bill of materials retained the previous shim specification [^r5].
* **Handle-bar switch RTV reconciliation:** The warranty administrator was directed to reconcile open return-to-vendor claims under [[sop-inv-002|SOP-INV-002]] in Ledgerline ERP and seek vendor credit after replacement handle-bar display switches depleted Leeds spares stock without faulty cores being processed [^r6].
* **Harness inventory check:** The stores supervisor was instructed to verify current bin balances for waterproof harness assemblies at both the Leeds and Manchester facilities [^r7].
* **Transit packaging guidelines:** To resolve transit liability disputes arising from bike shops re-using worn packaging, the Leeds unit supervisor was tasked with circulating packing guidelines to all retail network accounts [^r8].
* **Warranty intake form update:** Under [[sop-sal-002|SOP-SAL-002]], the technical support clerk was instructed to update warranty intake forms in the e-mail system to mandate photographs prior to collection [^r9].
