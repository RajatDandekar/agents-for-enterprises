On 21 October 2022, a regular supplier review established operational actions across receiving, purchasing, and warehouse units at Manchester and Leeds.[^r1] The review addressed missing delivery documentation, invoice freight variances, carrier delays on rejected stock, and accurate logging of schedule changes.[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5] These measures ensure accurate supplier lead time performance reporting and prevent processing bottlenecks across receiving bays and ledger matching.[^r2][^r3][^r5]

## Attendees

The meeting was attended by [[user-bprice|Bronwen Price]], Callum Shaw, Isla Morgan, and Rhys Pryce.[^r1]

## Issues and decisions

### Delivery paperwork and receiving delays
Pallet consignments from primary frame vendors frequently arrived without standard printed delivery paperwork attached, causing receiving bay cross-referencing delays in Manchester.[^r2]

### Freight terms and surcharge matching
Purchasing staff were requested to review and confirm open order freight terms with suppliers to prevent manual price variance flags and delivery surcharge discrepancies during ledger matching.[^r3]

### Quarantine holding congestion
Slow carrier collection of rejected components returned under debit notes led to congestion within the Leeds assembly unit's quarantine holding area.[^r4]

### Purchase order schedule amendments
Staff were required to record manual amendments to purchase order schedules promptly against core ledger files so that vendor lead time metrics reflect supplier compliance rather than internal delays.[^r5]
