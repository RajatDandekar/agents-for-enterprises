Amendment AMD-PUR-2026-03 modifies the direct purchase order threshold in [[pol-pur-001|POL-PUR-001]] §3.3 [^r5][^r6]. Today, direct purchase orders without a purchase request remain permitted up to £50,000 gross [^r2][^r7]. From 1 December 2026, the allowable threshold will decrease to £25,000 gross, with system enforcement scheduled for the evening of 30 November 2026 [^r1][^r3][^r4][^r6].

## Clauses

### 1. What changes
Until 30 November 2026, the direct purchase order threshold under [[pol-pur-001|POL-PUR-001]] §3.3 remains at £50,000 gross [^r2][^r7]. From 1 December 2026, clause §3.3 is replaced so that direct purchase orders without a request are permitted only when the gross value, including VAT, is £25,000 or less [^r3][^r6].

### 2. Why
An internal audit for the first half of 2026-27 identified 212 direct orders valued between £25,000 and £50,000 [^r8]. Many of these purchases represented repeat orders for identical items that should have been planned via purchase requests [^r8].

### 3. What changes in Ledgerline
On the evening of 30 November 2026, IT will update the system configuration parameter PUR.DIR_LIMIT in Ledgerline from £50,000 to £25,000 pursuant to [[man-pur-72|MAN-PUR-72]] §23.4 [^r1][^r4][^r9]. From 1 December 2026, any direct order exceeding £25,000 gross will be rejected in Ledgerline with error [[err-po-0610|ERR-PO-0610]] [^r10]. Direct purchase orders saved prior to 1 December 2026 are not affected by the revised limit [^r11].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Today's rule:** Direct purchase orders without a purchase request are permitted up to £50,000 gross through 30 November 2026 [^r2][^r7].
* **Future rule:** Effective 1 December 2026, direct purchase orders without a request are capped at £25,000 gross, and orders above this threshold fail with [[err-po-0610|ERR-PO-0610]] [^r3][^r6][^r10].
