Tom Ashworth (`TASHWORTH`) is a member of Northbridge Cycles' finance and accounts payable team who handles supplier statement reconciliations, duplicate entry resolution, and ledger investigations. As of 2026, he coordinates accounts payable balance reviews ahead of payment runs and participates in month-end closing procedures [^r1][^r6].

## Role and activities

### Supplier reconciliations and invoice resolution
On 24 February 2021, Tom Ashworth investigated supplier monthly statements where the supplier closing balance exceeded the Ledgerline purchase ledger balance due to missing invoices and unapplied credit notes [^r2]. He identified that the missing invoices were associated with Manchester plant deliveries where goods received notes had either not been processed by stores or had been matched against different paperwork [^r3]. Ashworth requested purchasing to locate and post the receiving documentation, and coordinated with supplier accounts to ensure Northbridge credit notes for returned damaged components were applied prior to payment runs [^r4].

On 27 May 2021, Ashworth resolved an issue regarding a duplicate invoice by placing a temporary hold on the duplicate ledger entry, reversing the accrual, and logging notes on the supplier account [^r5].

### Payment reviews and month-end close
On 5 March 2026, Ashworth instructed Accounts Payable to review a frame supplier's ledger balance in Ledgerline ERP following a request for early settlement ahead of the standard weekly payment run [^r6]. On 30 June 2026, he attended the month-end review meeting addressing the accounts payable cut-off [^r1].
