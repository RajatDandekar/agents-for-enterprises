Northbridge Cycles conducts an annual refresher training programme for all Manchester warehouse, assembly, and stores staff who hold an internal permit to operate counterbalance or reach trucks [^r1]. Attendance at scheduled sessions is mandatory regardless of remaining permit validity, and unexcused absences result in immediate suspension of truck operating authorisation [^r3][^r6]. The training covers warehouse aisle manoeuvring, heavy-load handling across the production floor, equipment inspections, and battery charging routines [^r2].

## Training Curriculum and Delivery

Forklift refresher training is delivered during normal shift hours and consists of short classroom reviews held in the training room alongside practical assessments conducted in the goods-in yard [^r4]. Sessions address safe manoeuvring within racking aisles, pre-shift daily inspection routines, battery charging procedures, and the safe handling of heavy battery packs and frame crates around the production floor [^r2].

## Attendance and Compliance Requirements

All personnel across the Manchester warehouse, assembly, and stores operations who hold an internal operating permit for counterbalance or reach trucks must take part in the annual programme [^r1]. Staff members are required to attend their assigned sessions even if their current internal truck permit has not yet reached its expiry date [^r3].

All attendees must report to training wearing standard personal protective equipment, specifically high-visibility vests and safety boots [^r5]. If an operator fails to attend a rostered session without obtaining prior agreement from their line manager, their truck operating authorisation is suspended until an alternative session can be scheduled [^r6].
