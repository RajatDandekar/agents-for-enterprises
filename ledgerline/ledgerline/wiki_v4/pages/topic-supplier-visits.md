Supplier visits serve as an essential element of quality assurance, contract review, and ongoing vendor management for Northbridge Cycles Ltd whether hosting vendor representatives or visiting manufacturers.[^r1] Visiting vendor facilities allows staff to inspect manufacturing capabilities and standards directly, while hosting suppliers on site supports defect resolution and release planning.[^r2][^r3] All visits require thorough preparation in Ledgerline ERP, strict compliance with site security and commercial confidentiality, and formal follow-up reporting.[^r5][^r7][^r12]

## Purpose and Scope

Outward visits to vendor facilities enable purchasing staff, manufacturing engineers, and quality inspectors to audit production lines, check frame fabrication capacity, verify battery assembly standards, and assess electrical component reliability.[^r2] Hosting suppliers on site allows Northbridge to address recurring delivery defects, inspect engineering change notes, and coordinate production schedules for upcoming bike model releases.[^r3]

## Arranging Outward Visits

Staff travelling to supplier premises must obtain prior approval from the purchasing department manager and arrange travel via approved internal channels, keeping expenses reasonable and justifiable.[^r4] Before departing, the lead visitor must inspect open purchase orders and outstanding delivery discrepancies in Ledgerline ERP, check quality reject rates and defect investigations, prepare a written agenda, and confirm PPE and site access rules.[^r5] Any verbal commercial agreements made during supplier visits remain strictly subject to formal written confirmation and standard purchase order terms.[^r6]

## Hosting Supplier Representatives

Staff hosting supplier representatives at the Manchester works or the Leeds unit are responsible for their safety, conduct, and commercial confidentiality at all times.[^r7] Hosts must ensure supplier visitors are immediately registered at reception, issued visitor security passes, and sign a health and safety briefing sheet before entering production or warehouse zones.[^r8] Visitors must be accompanied by their host at all times on company premises and must never be left unattended in engineering bays or storage yards.[^r9] Discussions regarding forward production volumes, component costs, and design alterations must be strictly restricted to authorised purchasing personnel.[^r10] At the end of the visit, hosts must escort supplier visitors back to reception to sign out and return their passes.[^r11]

## Reporting and System Recording

After any on-site or off-site supplier visit, the lead employee must write a contact report specifying attendees, key discussion topics, agreed actions, owners, and target completion schedules.[^r12] Supplier visit contact reports must be emailed to the purchasing team, manufacturing engineering desk, and store management, with key outcomes, agreed lead-time changes, and quality review dates logged against the vendor master record in Ledgerline ERP.[^r13]
