This operational decision established the purchasing office replacement printer as the primary output destination for printed purchase documentation and supplier copies from 12 January 2019 [^r1]. It redirected default print queues away from the legacy shared machine near the stationery cupboard [^r1][^r2]. For Northbridge Cycles today, it records the physical output routing and standard stationery configuration configured for purchase order print routines [^r2][^r3].

## Background and decision

Effective 12 January 2019, all printed purchase documentation and supplier copies were directed to the new replacement printer in the purchasing office rather than the old machine by the stationery cupboard [^r1]. The change centralised purchase document handling within the purchasing department [^r1].

## Technical and operational changes

The IT support desk remapped the default print queue in the enterprise software so standard order print routines automatically target the purchasing office printer [^r2]. Print trays on the purchasing office order printer were stocked with approved pre-printed company letterhead and plain backing paper [^r3].
