Goods receiving and intake operations encompass the physical intake, staging, and system processing of inbound supplier deliveries at Northbridge Cycles sites. Congestion at inbound facilities directly affects inventory visibility, yard safety, and downstream transport links between facilities. As of October 2026, intake velocity at key hubs remains critical to maintaining schedule integrity across production locations.

## Operational Issues and Yard Congestion

Simultaneous Monday morning deliveries at the Manchester goods inward bay have resulted in yard blockages, pallets remaining outside on the apron, and safety hazards for forklifts operating between rows [^r1]. In addition to physical yard constraints, slow receipt processing into the system directly delayed parts transfer from Manchester to the Leeds site via the transfer wagon [^r2].
