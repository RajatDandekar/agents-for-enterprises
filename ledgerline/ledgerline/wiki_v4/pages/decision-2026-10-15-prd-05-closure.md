Cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] (city e-bike line) was closed in Ledgerline on the afternoon of 15 October 2026 [^r1]. As of today, 26 October 2026, any purchasing document saved against this cost centre remains stuck in Draft status with warning [[w-cc-114|W-CC-114]] and will not reach the supplier [^r2]. Purchasing staff whose default profile still points to this line must have their default cost centre updated [^r3].

## Decision details

On the afternoon of 15 October 2026, cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] for the city e-bike line was formally closed in Ledgerline [^r1]. 

## Operational impact

Any purchase order or record saved against closed cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] remains in Draft status, triggers warning [[w-cc-114|W-CC-114]], and will not be dispatched to the supplier [^r2]. Purchasing staff whose default cost centre remains set to PRD-05 must contact Joanna to update it to an active code [^r3].
