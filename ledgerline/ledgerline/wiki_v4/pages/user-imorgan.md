Isla Morgan (`IMORGAN`) is the sales manager at Northbridge Cycles, owning the core order-to-despatch and warranty processes across retail channels [^r2][^r3]. In Ledgerline and master data records, she serves as the assigned account representative for independent bike shops and functions as the business owner for retail electronic data interchange [^r1][^r4][^r5]. Morgan also represents sales operations across cross-departmental safety, IT, systems, and warehouse logistics steering forums [^r7][^r8][^r9][^r10].

## Role and responsibilities

As sales manager, Morgan is the author and business owner of [[sop-sal-001|SOP-SAL-001 Sales Order to Despatch]] and [[sop-sal-002|SOP-SAL-002 Warranty Claims from Bike Shops]] [^r2][^r3]. In the integration landscape, she is documented as the business owner for inbound interface `INT-001`, which ingests EDI `ORDERS` transactions from retail partners [^r4]. Within [[data-customers|Customers (bike shops)]], Morgan acts as the assigned sales representative for accounts such as Aarhus Pedal Power [^r1]. Transaction records in [[data-sales-orders-2026|sales_orders_2026]] record her managing order transactions, including orders `SO/2026/000001` for The Rennes Bike Shop and `SO/2026/000005` for Buxton Pedal Power [^r5].

## Operational coordination and decisions

Morgan coordinates trade, packaging, and order flow policies across Northbridge departments:

* **Packaging and stores:** On 6 March 2019, Morgan agreed on an updated accessory packaging arrangement with the Manchester plant packing team and Leeds stores unit using heavier outer cartons and pre-formed pulp trays, requesting that [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]] update system packaging records [^r11][^r12]. At the Stores and Logistics meeting on 1 October 2024, she addressed returns handling, sales office documentation, and dispatch packaging logistics [^r9].
* **Event and staff coordination:** On 17 September 2019, Morgan coordinated volunteer rotas across Manchester assembly, warehouse, and office units for the trade exhibition stand, confirming that volunteer travel, lodging, and subsistence complied with standard travel policy [^r13][^r14].
* **Quality and warranty feedback:** On 25 December 2021, Morgan notified [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber]] of an increase in bike shop warranty claims concerning rear hub assemblies and wiring looms, attributing failures to moisture ingress and loose connectors and proposing enhanced line assembly checks [^r15][^r16].
* **Production planning and order staging:** On 17 April 2025, Morgan instructed production planning that sales staff were manually logging advance reservations for the initial spring range [^r17]. She mandated that bike shops must not receive provisional delivery commitments before frame consignments pass inspection in Manchester and electrical kits are staged for final assembly, and instructed the dispatch desk not to print consignment paperwork for split shipments until complete trade packages are staged in the warehouse [^r18][^r19].
