Warning code W-CC-114 indicates that a purchase order contains an order line or header assigned to a closed or dormant cost centre [^r1][^r2][^r9]. The document saves with an order number in status DR (Draft) with editable lines instead of being rejected outright, displaying '<number> SAVED' on the status bar [^r6][^r9]. Because the order remains in Draft, it sits unapproved without reaching the supplier, and downstream steps such as printing, electronic transmission, receipting, and general ledger commitment posting are locked until resolved [^r10][^r11].

## Meaning
W-CC-114 is a non-fatal warning code returned in field `warn_cd` and displayed in the header's Warning field as well as the order list [^r1][^r7]. It signifies that the cost centre assigned to the document or order line is marked as CLOSED by Finance [^r2][^r6]. The document successfully saves with a generated document number, but it is held in status DR [^r2][^r6].

## Cause
The warning is triggered when an unapproved Draft purchase order or order line is raised or saved against a department cost centre that has been closed or rendered dormant in Finance, such as [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] [^r2][^r4][^r9][^r11].

## How to fix
To resolve W-CC-114, check [[screen-fin-cc-01|FIN-CC-01]] to identify an active departmental cost centre [^r3]. Update the cost centre on the line details via [[screen-pur-po-01|PUR-PO-01]] or amend the unapproved Draft order on [[screen-pur-pom-06|PUR-POM-06]] to an open account and re-save [^r3][^r5][^r7]. Re-saving the order against an active cost centre clears the warning and advances the document to status AP or PA according to standard rules, unlocking printing, electronic transmission, receipting, and GL commitment posting [^r5][^r10].

## Raised by
The warning is raised by the [[services-pur-po|PUR po services]] `po_save` and `po_amend` [^r8].

## Versions and conflicts
* **Superseded procedure wording (2025-02-01 vs 2023-05-01):** Under current [[sop-pur-002|SOP-PUR-002]] v1.4, warning W-CC-114 accompanies an order held in status DR when a cost centre assigned to an order line has been closed by Finance, retaining editable lines [^r2][^r9]. In superseded [[sop-pur-002|SOP-PUR-002]] v1.3 (2023-05-01), the warning was described specifically as appearing on the order header when the cost centre assigned on the original request was closed [^r12].
