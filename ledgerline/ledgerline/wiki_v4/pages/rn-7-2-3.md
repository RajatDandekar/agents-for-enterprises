Ledgerline ERP 7.2.3 is a maintenance release issued on 15 January 2025 that applies to both the Manchester plant and the Leeds unit [^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, it enforces explicit data retrieval via [[err-scr-0221|ERR-SCR-0221]] before purchase order saving and routes closed cost-centre orders into draft status under warning [[w-cc-114|W-CC-114]] [^r2][^r4]. It also mandates fetch tokens for integration services and updates Chapter 17 diagnostic catalogues across all core screens and services [^r3][^r6].

## Changes

Saving a purchase order on screen [[screen-pur-po-01|PUR-PO-01]] requires an explicit retrieval of supplier terms and item data; attempting to save without a prior fetch halts the transaction and raises [[err-scr-0221|ERR-SCR-0221 FETCH NOT DONE]] [^r2]. External integration routines writing purchase orders through [[services-pur-po]] must execute `po_fetch` prior to `po_save` and pass the returned `fetch_tok` token within the payload [^r3].

Orders raised against closed cost centres are no longer rejected immediately by the system [^r4]. Instead, the system saves them in Draft status with warning [[w-cc-114|W-CC-114]] without committing funds to the general ledger [^r4]. These draft purchase orders remain locked against printing, electronic transmission, and receipting until a user reassigns them to an active cost centre and saves the record [^r5].

Chapter 17 of the user manual error catalogue was updated to align with on-screen diagnostics, console exceptions, and event log entries across core system screens and services [^r6].

## Effect at Northbridge

At Northbridge's Manchester and Leeds operations, purchasing staff and automated integrations must observe new operational requirements and interface workarounds [^r1][^r3][^r8].

Purchasing workflows must accommodate the mandatory fetch sequence on screen [[screen-pur-po-01|PUR-PO-01]] and ensure closed cost centres are updated to release locked [[w-cc-114|W-CC-114]] orders [^r2][^r5]. Three known system issues require specific operational mitigations [^r7][^r8][^r9]:
* Multi-page standard works order travellers encounter column divider misalignment when printed, which system administrators can correct by adjusting default printer margins [^r7].
* Searching the central parts master within sales quotation entry slows down when filtering by general description, requiring staff in Leeds and Manchester to select from the category dropdown instead [^r8].
* Generating an on-screen preview of the stock variance summary truncates the final column on standard resolution displays, requiring users to export data to a spreadsheet or send it to a spool printer [^r9].
