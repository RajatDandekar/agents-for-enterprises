This page catalogues the ERR-PKB-* error codes generated by Ledgerline production kanban operations as of 26 October 2026. These messages are raised by backend services during kanban retrieval, record saving, and status signalling. Operations encountering these errors typically involve kanban record lookups or quantity validations.

### ERR-PKB-0001

* **Message**: PRODUCTION KANBAN NOT FOUND [^r1][^r3]
* **Cause**: The requested production kanban record does not exist in the system [^r1][^r3].
* **Remedy**: Verify the kanban identifier and ensure the record exists before querying or signalling [^r1][^r3].
* **Raised by**: Raised by services `pkb_get` and `pkb_signal` [^r1][^r3].
* **Detailed page**: [[err-pkb-0001|ERR-PKB-0001 Primary Kanban Validation Failure]]

### ERR-PKB-0201

* **Message**: QTY MUST BE > 0 [^r2]
* **Cause**: The kanban transaction specifies a quantity that is not greater than zero [^r2].
* **Remedy**: Enter a positive quantity value greater than zero [^r2].
* **Raised by**: Raised during execution of service `pkb_save` [^r2].
* **Detailed page**: [[err-pkb-0201|ERR-PKB-0201 Duplicate Active Kanban Cycle]]
