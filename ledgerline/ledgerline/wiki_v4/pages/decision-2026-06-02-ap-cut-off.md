This decision moves the monthly Accounts Payable processing cut-off forward to working day two at 12:00 starting from the June 2026 period close, enabling reporting to the board a day earlier [^r5]. All supplier invoices arriving after this deadline are deferred and posted into the subsequent financial period [^r6]. The operational shift overrides the previous timetable in [[sop-ap-002|SOP-AP-002]] while the formal procedure document awaits update [^r7].

## Details

Effective from the June 2026 close, the Accounts Payable cut-off takes place on working day two at 12:00 rather than working day three [^r5]. Financial Controller [[user-gwhitaker|Grace Whitaker]] confirmed that invoices received past this cut-off are deferred into the next period, noting that [[sop-ap-002|SOP-AP-002]] would be formally reissued when time permits [^r6][^r7].

The initial period close under the revised schedule completed successfully, allowing the purchase ledger to balance and post without earlier delays [^r1]. Late postal invoices arriving after the cut-off must be held over to the following period and require an accrual entry agreed with the financial controller [^r2]. Goods receiving delivery notes at the Leeds unit must be forwarded to accounts payable by the agreed morning deadline [^r4]. In addition, all component scrap sheets must be entered into the system prior to the end of the shift preceding the cut-off to prevent discrepancies between physical stock and perpetual inventory [^r3].
