Screen INV-CSG-17 (Consignment Stock Entry) records incoming supplier consignment deliveries and tracks their consumption in Ledgerline [^r1][^r18]. It allows Northbridge Cycles to increment on-hand physical inventory without booking an unvouched liability or affecting owned stock valuation until parts are consumed [^r19][^r20]. When parts are issued to assembly lines or dispatched, settlement transfers ownership and posts accounting entries [^r4][^r5][^r20].

## Fields

| Field label | Field name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| SUP CD | `sup_cd` | Vendor code; mandatory field under menu Inventory > Consignment stock [^r1][^r12][^r15]. |
| ITEM CD | `itm_cd` | Component or part item code; mandatory [^r1][^r2][^r15]. |
| QTY | `qty` | Counted consignment quantity; mandatory [^r1][^r2][^r15]. |
| CSG NO | `csg_no` | Serial consignment reference number generated by the system; left empty during new receipt creation [^r1][^r2][^r3][^r11]. |
| STATUS | `status` | Record status indicator, assigned as active/open upon initial save; left empty during new receipt creation [^r1][^r2][^r3][^r11]. |

To record new incoming consignment stock, users enter `sup_cd`, `itm_cd`, and `qty`, leaving `csg_no` and `status` empty, and trigger `csg_save` [^r2]. Ledgerline generates the next serial consignment number in `csg_no`, sets `status` to active, and warehouse staff must note the generated `csg_no` on the physical vendor delivery note [^r3][^r11].

Consignment parts provide an explicit ownership indicator alongside location entries on stock maintenance and warehouse enquiry displays [^r18]. Ownership transfer and supplier self-billing accrual or invoice match entries trigger only upon consumption transfer onto active assembly lines [^r20].

## Keys and services

INV-CSG-17 connects to the [[services-inv-csg|INV consignment services]] introduced in Ledgerline 7.1 [^r1][^r13]:

* `csg_save`: Commits consignment stock receipts requiring supplier code, item code, and quantity, assigning the next serial `csg_no` and setting `status` to active [^r1][^r3][^r15].
* `csg_get`: Read service (POST `/ll72/svc/csg_get`) using input field `csg_no` to retrieve parcels and verify `status` prior to settlement [^r1][^r4][^r14].
* `csg_search`: Read service used to search records; querying by `sup_cd` alone returns all open consignments for that vendor [^r1][^r10][^r16].
* `csg_settle`: Internal settlement service executed when parts are consumed for assembly or bicycle shop dispatch, adjusting stock balances, setting consignment status, and writing ledger entries [^r1][^r4][^r5][^r17].

## Messages

* [[err-csg-0001]]: Displayed when executing `csg_save` or `csg_settle` without completing mandatory entries (`sup_cd`, `itm_cd`, `qty`) or upon a loss of connection [^r6].
* [[err-csg-0201]]: Displayed when `csg_settle` is selected against a parcel that is already fully settled, cancelled, or frozen by central finance, or when attempting to overwrite an archived record via `csg_save` [^r7].
* [[err-itm-0011]]: Displayed when the entered `itm_cd` does not exist, is inactive, discontinued, or not authorised for consignment handling [^r8].
* [[err-sup-0030]]: Displayed when the entered `sup_cd` is unrecognised, inactive, or lacks an active consignment contract setup in accounts [^r9].

## Versions and conflicts

* **New delivery entry and initial status**: In current documentation (v7.2.3), `csg_no` and `status` are left empty on delivery creation, and `csg_save` generates `csg_no` and sets `status` to active [^r2][^r3][^r11]. Superseded manual MAN-INV-72 v7.2.2 (2024-06-10) marked `csg_no` as a mandatory input field and indicated that `csg_save` marked `status` as "held on site" (`superseded`) [^r21][^r22].
* **Error message triggers**: In current documentation (v7.2.3), ERR-CSG-0001 indicates missing mandatory fields or connection drops, and ERR-CSG-0201 indicates settlement of an already settled, cancelled, or frozen record or overwriting an archive [^r6][^r7]. Superseded manual MAN-INV-72 v7.2.2 defined ERR-CSG-0001 as consignment number not found and ERR-CSG-0201 as settlement quantity exceeding balance (`superseded`) [^r23].
