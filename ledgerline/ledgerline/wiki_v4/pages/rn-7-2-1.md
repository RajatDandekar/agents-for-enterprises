Ledgerline ERP 7.2.1 is a maintenance release issued on 6 November 2023 for all Ledgerline 7 customers [^r1]. It introduces visual red text highlighting for expired certificates across supplier and purchase order screens, though it does not prevent transactional processing against those accounts [^r2][^r3]. The release also enforces strict date validation on external certificate imports and identifies printer and query defects affecting plant operations [^r6][^r7][^r8].

## Changes
The supplier record and purchase order maintenance displays show expired certificates highlighted in red text on the certificate tab [^r2]. Whenever a record is opened or refreshed, the underlying service evaluates the recorded expiry date against the system date, applying red highlighting only to expired lines while active and pending records remain in standard font [^r4]. Amending an expired certificate date to a valid future date clears the red highlighting immediately without requiring a client session restart [^r5]. External integration import routines that post certificate renewals must reject rows and log exceptions if incoming records contain blank or malformed dates [^r6].

The update introduces several known defects:
* Despatch note summary delivery address carriage returns fail on certain laser printers at the Manchester plant, overlapping carrier account details onto carriage instructions [^r7].
* Querying inventory across high-volume hardware components incurs noticeable delays unless filtered by entering a primary category prefix [^r8].
* The signature block is omitted from the final page footer of batch trace reports when a frame assembly references multiple sub-assemblies across split production shifts [^r9].

## Effect at Northbridge
The visual highlighting assists buyers in spotting lapsed documentation, but it does not automatically place suppliers on stop or block users from raising purchase orders against accounts with expired records [^r3]. Buyers handling compliance items such as UN 38.3 documentation under [[sop-pur-005|SOP-PUR-005 Certificate Renewal (UN 38.3)]] must manually monitor accounts rather than relying on system order blocks [^r3]. Warehouse and despatch teams at the Manchester plant must verify laser-printed despatch note summaries to ensure overlapping carrier text remains legible [^r7]. Inventory clerks must prefix queries with category codes when searching hardware lines to avoid system delays, and quality personnel must check multi-shift split assembly batch trace footers for missing sign-off blocks [^r8][^r9].
