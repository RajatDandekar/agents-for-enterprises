On 16 February 2015, the purchasing department established procedural cutover steps to prepare for the migration to Ledgerline 7[^r1][^r3]. The migration was initiated to resolve recurring duplicate order and invoice entries occurring between the Manchester and Leeds sites[^r1]. While the transition tasks reflect historical cutover actions superseded by subsequent operations, the record documents the baseline data verification required when retiring legacy ledgers[^r1][^r2][^r3].

## Migration instructions

Prior to the cutover in February 2015, purchasing staff were instructed to complete specific data-cleansing and reconciliation tasks before the legacy files were locked[^r2]:
* Check every outstanding purchase order against paper records[^r2].
* Confirm recent receipts of frames, motors, and batteries[^r2].
* Ensure no unapproved notes remained on desks[^r2].
* Verify current payment terms and delivery addresses in supplier records[^r2].

## Legacy system cutover

During the transition to Ledgerline 7, the legacy ledger was scheduled to be set to read-only following a brief screen walk-through[^r3].
