Northbridge Cycles operates its procurement and supplier administration functions from the commercial unit in Leeds alongside manufacturing operations in Manchester [^r1]. The unit oversees component sourcing, contract negotiation, routine purchase order administration, and incoming invoice clearance [^r1]. It coordinates continuous deliveries of critical parts, including frames, motors, battery casings, cabling, and gearing, to Manchester production lines [^r2].

## Purchasing management

Purchasing management at Leeds oversees commercial contracts, unit pricing, standard lead times, and agreed delivery schedules [^r3]. The team conducts vendor reviews, examines vendor scorecards, and agrees replenishment targets in conjunction with Manchester production planners [^r3].

## Buying and operational replenishment

Operational buying staff handle routine replenishment by raising purchase orders aligned with contract terms and reorder triggers [^r4]. Buyers expedite outstanding orders, manage split consignments and delivery reschedules, identify alternative component sources when needed, and coordinate directly with Manchester goods inward [^r4].

## Purchase administration and accounts payable

The purchase administration desk maintains vendor records and conducts three-way matching between incoming supplier invoices, purchase orders, and electronic goods received notes [^r5]. Staff at this desk issue debit notes, manage supplier payment queries, and archive customs, delivery, and compliance documentation [^r5]. Visitors from other departments arriving at the Leeds commercial unit are required to sign the visitors register at reception [^r6].
