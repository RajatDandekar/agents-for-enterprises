Daniel Okafor is the active Purchase Manager at Northbridge Cycles operating under user ID DOKAFOR within operating unit NBC-MAN-01 [^r1]. He holds an individual purchase order approval limit of £1,000,000 gross, reports directly to [[user-hweber|Henrik Weber]], and is assigned default cost centre [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] [^r1][^r2]. As owner and approver of core purchasing policies and standard operating procedures, he exercises governance over supplier onboarding, rate maintenance, and high-value purchase order authorisations [^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5][^r7][^r8].

## Responsibilities and Governance

Daniel Okafor holds the [[role-pur-mgr|PUR-MGR]] role in Ledgerline and serves as the primary contact for purchasing policies and new starter induction [^r1][^r13]. He authored revisions 2.0 and 3.0 of [[pol-apr-002|POL-APR-002]] and approved version 2.3 of [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]] on 2026-04-01 [^r2][^r3][^r62]. Under company policies, he must review and approve all supplier onboarding documentation before master accounts are generated in Ledgerline, and he holds exclusive authority to submit written requests to IT for unblocking blocked suppliers or addressing duplicate supplier records [^r4][^r5][^r63]. On 12 October 2026, he formally issued the notification for [[amd-pur-2026-03|AMD-PUR-2026-03]] to the purchasing team [^r59].

Okafor owns several procurement procedures, including [[sop-pur-002|SOP-PUR-002]] (versions 1.3 and 1.4), [[sop-pur-003|SOP-PUR-003]] (Purchase Order Amendment and Cancellation), [[sop-pur-004|SOP-PUR-004]] (Rate Contract Maintenance), and [[sop-pur-006|SOP-PUR-006]] (Quotations and Supplier Selection) [^r6][^r7][^r8][^r11][^r64]. Under SOP-PUR-006, buyers must seek his written dispensation if fewer than three quotes can be obtained for requirements over £100,000 gross [^r12]. He approved version 1.1 of [[sop-pur-005|SOP-PUR-005]] on 2026-09-01, under which he receives escalation notifications 60 days before any [[topic-un-38-3-certificates|UN 38.3]] certificate expires and reviews discrepancies in laboratory reports [^r9][^r10].

On 2 September 2026, Okafor issued instructions requiring that all approvals for lithium-cell components across [[items-bat|BAT-*]], [[items-mtr|MTR-*]], or [[items-chg|CHG-*]] lines include "CERT OK" and the certificate reference in the comment field [^r54]. In departmental operations, he moved weekly purchasing meetings permanently to Thursday afternoons in September 2025, and manages staff user profile reviews and authorisation queues ahead of month-end accounts runs [^r50][^r51][^r53].

## Order Approvals and Supplier Operations

Purchase orders that exceed the limits of [[role-pur-exec|purchase executives]] are routed to Okafor for managerial sign-off up to his £1,000,000 gross threshold [^r1][^r16][^r17][^r28][^r61]. In transactions across 2026, he approved substantial orders across Manchester and Leeds cost centres—such as [[cc-lds-prd-01|LDS-PRD-01]]—for batteries, frames, grips, tyres, displays, and [[items-brk|brake]] components [^r14][^r15][^r16][^r17][^r18][^r19][^r20][^r21][^r22][^r23][^r24][^r25][^r26][^r27][^r28][^r29][^r30][^r31][^r32][^r33][^r34][^r35]. These included paper and system sign-off for PO/2026/00452 covering 1,800 BAT-500 packs on 2026-04-06, as well as acknowledging charger order PO/2026/01179 for approval on 2026-10-26 [^r36][^r60].

Okafor directly participates in commercial reviews, including supplier performance scoring, carrier delivery notes, and contract rate schedules across the Manchester plant and Leeds unit [^r38][^r39][^r41][^r42][^r43][^r45][^r46]. He manages ongoing vendor relations with [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]], leading the supplier review on 2026-09-17 with [[user-lmoreno|Luis Moreno]] [^r40]. On 15 June 2026, he authorised Voltway Cells battery packs to bypass quality quarantine and enter WH-MAN-STR directly based on a valid UN 38.3 test report and clean two-year quality record [^r52].
