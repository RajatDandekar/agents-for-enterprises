The purchasing new starter induction provides core operational training for staff procuring goods across Northbridge Cycles' Manchester and Leeds operations [^r1][^r2]. Updated in May 2024, it outlines standard purchasing workflows in Ledgerline ERP 7.2 alongside core system security controls [^r1][^r3]. It also identifies functional ownership within the procurement department and IT support channels for new starters [^r7].

## Team structure and scope

The purchasing team comprises [[role-pur-exec|purchase executives]], a senior buyer, and a purchase manager [^r2]. The department conducts purchasing for both the Manchester plant and the Leeds unit [^r2].

## Purchasing lifecycle and Ledgerline access

All purchasing transactions at Northbridge are executed through Ledgerline ERP 7.2 [^r3]. The transaction flow progresses through purchase requisition (PR), purchase order (PO), approval, release, goods received note (GRN), and invoice matching [^r4]. Suppliers receive only orders that have reached released status [^r4].

System access is governed by the following session and security rules:
* Ledgerline ERP user sessions automatically terminate after 20 minutes of inactivity [^r5].
* Entering an incorrect password three consecutive times triggers an automatic 30-minute account lockout [^r6].

## Key contacts

New starters are directed to designated contacts based on the query type:
* [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] covers purchasing policies [^r1][^r7].
* [[user-lmoreno|Luis Moreno]] handles suppliers and certificates [^r1][^r7].
* The IT service desk resolves Ledgerline technical issues via internal extension 2210 [^r7].
