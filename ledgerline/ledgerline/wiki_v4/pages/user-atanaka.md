Aiko Tanaka is an active Stores team member at Northbridge Cycles based in Manchester operating unit NBC-MAN-01 [^r1]. Tanaka reports to [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] under default cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] and holds a £0 gross approval limit under [[role-stores|role STORES]] [^r1]. In day-to-day operations, Tanaka processes inbound receipts in Ledgerline, coordinates cycle count reconciliation across sites, and serves as a key stores contact for receiving bay management [^r1][^r2][^r15][^r17].

## Responsibilities and system activity

Tanaka records incoming shipments into warehouse WH-MAN-STR against active purchase orders in [[data-grn-register-2026|Ledgerline]] [^r2][^r3]. Recorded receipts include GRN/2026/28618 on 2026-01-10 for 225 units of lacquer LAC-SLV against PO/2026/00001 and delivery note SP/DN/53879, GRN/2026/28622 on 2026-01-17 for 2,000 units of LGT-REB against PO/2026/00003 from supplier WL-CAR-0539, and GRN/2026/28666 on 2026-02-06 for 120,000 units of CHG-4A-01 from supplier CC-SAL-0031 [^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5].

In stocktaking, Tanaka coordinates warehouse cycle counts across both sites, overseeing paperwork collection and entering inventory variance records into the system [^r15]. Warehouse staff are instructed to escalate damaged cartons and inventory count discrepancies directly to Tanaka [^r16]. Tanaka is also tasked with checking the purchase ledger for partial deliveries—such as flagging outstanding balances on brake pad shipments requested by [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]] on 2026-07-23—to prevent premature invoice passing prior to final system booking [^r20].

## Operations and storekeeping issues

Tanaka has regularly raised dock intake controls and warehouse flow challenges with management:
* **Unreferenced deliveries**: On 2022-08-26, Tanaka alerted [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] about deliveries arriving without purchase order numbers on the delivery note, warning that staging unreferenced pallets on the loading bay blocks scheduled intake, impedes invoice matching, and corrupts stock counts [^r13][^r14].
* **Intake bay congestion**: On 2026-03-16, Tanaka reported severe Monday morning congestion at the Manchester receiving bay due to simultaneous haulier arrivals, which led to backlogs of intake paperwork, safe forklift transit restrictions, and hold-ups for the transfer wagon to Leeds [^r17][^r18]. Tanaka proposed spreading haulier delivery slots across the day and adding intake desk staff during morning peak hours [^r19].
