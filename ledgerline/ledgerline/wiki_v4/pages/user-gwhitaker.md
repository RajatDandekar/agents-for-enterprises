Grace Whitaker is Northbridge Cycles' Financial Controller and owns key accounting procedures across accounts payable, costing, and financial reporting [^r1][^r2][^r8]. She conducts the final review and sign-off for accounts payable period close, authorizes supplier payment batches, and approves stock variance reconciliations [^r5][^r6][^r9][^r16]. Whitaker also owns outbound reporting interface INT-006 and leads key monthly finance operational reviews [^r10][^r13][^r15].

## Responsibilities and approvals

As Financial Controller, Grace Whitaker is the author and owner of procedure [[sop-fin-001|SOP-FIN-001 Cost Centre Open and Close]], and approved data policy [[pol-drp-009|POL-DRP-009 Data Retention Policy]] [^r1][^r8]. She authored and approved revision 2.4 of [[pol-tex-007|POL-TEX-007 Travel and Expenses Policy]] effective 1 July 2024, adjusting hotel caps [^r2]. She also approved core finance and inventory operational procedures, including [[sop-ap-001|SOP-AP-001 Three-Way Invoice Matching]], [[sop-ap-002|SOP-AP-002 Accounts Payable Period Close]], [[sop-ap-003|SOP-AP-003 Supplier Payment Run]], and [[sop-ap-004|SOP-AP-004 Supplier Bank Detail Changes]] [^r3][^r4][^r6][^r7]. Whitaker must formally approve all stock variance reconciliations under [[sop-inv-003|SOP-INV-003 Stock Count (cycle and annual)]] before adjustments update inventory records [^r9].

In month-end and period close operations under [[sop-ap-002|SOP-AP-002]], Whitaker reviews preliminary aged creditors trial balances, evaluates proposed accruals, and signs off the final period close in the system [^r5]. Post-cut-off supplier invoice accruals must be agreed directly with her [^r14]. For disbursements, she receives and reviews summary schedules for trade supplier payment runs to authorise batch posting and release to the bank [^r16]. Additionally, Whitaker is the registered owner of interface INT-006, which provides outbound database view reporting extracts [^r10].

## Operational directives and meetings

On 2 June 2026, Whitaker instructed the Accounts Payable team that the period close cut-off was brought forward to working day 2 at 12:00 starting from the June 2026 close ([[decision-2026-06-02-ap-cut-off|Decision 2026-06-02 AP Cut-off]]) [^r21]. On 15 October 2026, she confirmed the closure of cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] and warned purchasing personnel that subsequent transactions referencing it would sit in Draft status under warning [[w-cc-114|W-CC-114]] [^r22].

During annual budget preparations on 16 October 2025, Whitaker directed purchasing staff to assemble departmental spending forecasts across the Manchester works and Leeds unit, instruct buyers to evaluate purchase order commitments against assembly and repair stock levels to identify excess inventory from minimum order quantities, and record expected component, metal, consumable, freight, or rebate changes directly into budget models [^r17][^r18][^r19]. On 7 January 2026, she instructed Manchester staff to promptly submit outstanding expense claims with attached receipts and manager approval [^r20]. Whitaker attended the Battery Safety Committee on 15 October 2022 with [[user-imorgan|Isla Morgan]] and [[user-sberg|Stefan Berg]], owned the Quality meeting on 23 May 2026, led the month-end close review on 30 June 2026, and owned the Ledgerline key users meeting on 18 September 2026 [^r11][^r12][^r13][^r15].
