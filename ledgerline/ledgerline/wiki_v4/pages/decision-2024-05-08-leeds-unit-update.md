Operational review of the Leeds unit held on 8 May 2024 addressing transfer discrepancies from Manchester, staging bay congestion, rework stock visibility, and transport scheduling[^r1]. Key operational adjustments were agreed to reconcile physical transfers, clear loading shutters, and correct stock status synchronization between sites[^r2][^r4][^r5].

## Context and attendees

On 8 May 2024, [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]], [[user-sberg|Stefan Berg]], and [[user-rpryce|Rhys Pryce]] met to review operations at the Leeds unit[^r1].

## Operational decisions

### Inbound delivery notes and crate contents
Delivery notes sent from Manchester on the carrier do not always match the physical contents of crates, requiring Leeds staff to spend substantial time reconciling paperwork against unloaded stock[^r2]. Manchester must check packing line processes and confirm transfer paperwork is signed off before the carrier departs[^r3].

### Frame staging and holding pens
Due to space constraints in racking, completed frames at Leeds were held in staging bays and congested rear loading shutters[^r4]. The attendees agreed that frames must be moved promptly to designated holding pens after inspection[^r4].

### Rework stock availability and ledger entry
When assemblies are split for rework at Leeds, stock records do not reflect movement until the weekly reconciliation batch runs from Manchester, causing local screens to show stock as available when set aside for testing[^r5]. The entry steps for rework items must be reviewed with the ledger office to keep stock availability accurate between sites[^r6].

### Carrier dispatch and collection times
Early afternoon collections by the primary carrier have left assembly work on the loading deck until the next working day[^r7]. Manchester dispatch must liaise with the transport contractor regarding this schedule[^r7].

### Maintenance fixtures
Routine maintenance of wheel-truing equipment at Leeds has been delayed awaiting replacement fixtures to be located in Manchester stores and sent on the next internal run[^r8].
