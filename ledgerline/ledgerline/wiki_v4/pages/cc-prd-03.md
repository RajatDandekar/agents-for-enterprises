Cost centre PRD-03 covers manufacturing operations for the Cargo e-bike line within manufacturing unit NBC-MAN-01[^r1]. It is an active cost centre managed by [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] (DOKAFOR)[^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, it holds an available budget of £680,000 for period 2026-Q4[^r2].

## Budget and commitments

For financial period 2026-Q4 as of 26 October 2026 09:00, cost centre PRD-03 has an allocated budget of £800,000 and committed expenditure of £120,000, leaving an available balance of £680,000[^r2][^r3].

## Operations and procurement

Cost centre PRD-03 serves as the default cost centre assigned to Senior buyer [[user-lmoreno|Luis Moreno]] (LMORENO)[^r4].

The cost centre records procurement transactions for components used on the cargo line, including fasteners and frame parts[^r5]. In late April 2026, purchase orders charged to PRD-03 included PO/2026/00551 for FST-BLT-M4X35-SS, PO/2026/00561 for [[items-frm|FRM-TAM-L-SLV]] valued at £610,200.00 gross, and PO/2026/00571 for FST-BLT-M10X10-BK[^r5].
