Cost centre MNT-02 covers Leeds maintenance operations under manufacturing unit NBC-MAN-01 and owner BPRICE [^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, it holds OPEN status with an allocated 2026-Q4 budget of £128,000 against committed spend of £33,024.26 [^r1][^r2]. Maintenance charges for the Leeds facility and warehouse WH-LDS-STR are booked against this code [^r3].

## Allocation and budget

Cost centre MNT-02 (Leeds maintenance) is assigned to business unit NBC-MAN-01 with owner BPRICE and maintains an OPEN operating status [^r1]. For 2026-Q4 as of 26 October 2026 09:00, the cost centre has an allocated budget of £128,000, committed expenditure of £33,024.26, and an available balance of £94,975.74 [^r2].

## Operating charges

Cost centre MNT-02 is charged for maintenance supplies and MRO orders destined for warehouse WH-LDS-STR [^r3]. In January 2026, it was charged on purchase orders PO/2026/00014 for item MRO-00745 (£3,741.05 gross), PO/2026/00032 for item MRO-00535 (£1,184.59 gross), and PO/2026/00050 for item MRO-00406 (£4,267.73 gross) [^r3]. In July 2026, cost centre MNT-02 was charged on released purchase order PO/2026/01020 for 2 units of MRO-00958 from Viaduct Engineering Su (VES-BRI-0680) totalling £47.57 gross [^r4].
