The HBR family encompasses bicycle handlebars used across assembly operations at Northbridge Cycles, catalogued across flat (FLT), riser (RIS), and swept (SWP) profiles in varying widths [^r1][^r2][^r3]. As of 09:00 GMT on 26 October 2026, inventory is held primarily at Manchester stores (WH-MAN-STR), with select variants also stocked at Leeds stores (WH-LDS-STR) [^r1][^r2][^r7][^r10][^r12]. Total stock across the family includes major commitments on order for high-demand lines such as HBR-FLT-620 and HBR-SWP-620 [^r4][^r6]. Detailed balance records are tracked through the [[data-stock-on-hand|stock on hand]] register [^r1][^r2].
