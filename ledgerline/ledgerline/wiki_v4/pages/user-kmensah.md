Kwame Mensah (`KMENSAH`) is a purchase executive at Northbridge Cycles responsible for placing purchase orders and managing buyer allocations across key parts [^r1][^r3]. He handles procurement for frames, tyres, maintenance materials, and [[items-bat|battery packs]], working across cost centres for both Manchester works and the Leeds facility [^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6][^r16]. In addition to operational ordering, he monitors order queues, liaises on supplier pricing and delivery intervals, and coordinates store handling standards [^r16][^r19][^r22][^r24].

## Purchasing and order processing

Kwame Mensah is the assigned buyer in [[data-item-master|ITEM_MASTER_2026-10-26]] for spare part items including SPR-ANC-07 [^r1]. He routinely raises standard and volume purchase orders across several production lines:
* On 9 February 2026, he placed order PO/2026/00175 for 2,700 units of BAT-500 with [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] [^r2].
* On 6 March 2026, he raised PO/2026/00300 for 1,800 BAT-500 packs from Voltway Cells under [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] [^r3].
* In July 2026, he generated multiple maintenance orders for cost centre [[cc-mnt-01|MNT-01]], including MRO-00897 on PO/2026/01004, MRO-00714 on PO/2026/01007, MRO-00039 on PO/2026/01011, and MRO-00748 on PO/2026/01021 [^r4].
* On 30 July 2026, he raised PO/2026/01024 for 5,000 units of TYR-28-CTY-2.25 from Derwent Tyre Co. plc for [[cc-prd-02|PRD-02]] (£94,500.00 gross), followed on 31 July 2026 by PO/2026/01027 for 400 units of FRM-TAM-L-RED from Highfield Tube & Frame for [[cc-prd-03|PRD-03]] (£88,142.40 gross) [^r5].
* On 22 October 2026, he raised order PO/2026/01178 for 11,200 units of FRM-DEA-S-RED to Łódź Fabrications Sp. at unit rate 173.65 (£2,333,856.00 gross) under status AP against [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] [^r6].

## Departmental handover and coverage

Kwame Mensah covers purchasing queues during staff absences:
* In February 2025, [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]] requested that he cover pending purchasing requests, release auto-generated works orders once allocations became available, and approve standard parts requisitions for the Leeds workshop [^r14]. Requisitions that were unusual or exceeded standard thresholds were to wait unless an assembly line stoppage was imminent, in which case he held processing authorization [^r15].
* In July 2025, while covering for [[user-lmoreno|Luis Moreno]], he monitored the daily purchase order queue in Ledgerline across both Manchester works and the unit in Leeds, tracking urgent requirements for production planners and coordinating with stores supervisors regarding supplier delays [^r16][^r17]. Routine backorder queries from bike shops were redirected to the sales office [^r18].

## System administration and operational coordination

On 17 January 2021, Mensah flagged an operational workflow error in which a purchase requisition submitted to the approval inbox of [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]] became stalled with sign-off and return actions greyed out [^r13].

On 7 December 2025, he requested that Luis Moreno adjust outward default delivery lead times for tyre suppliers in Ledgerline to account for freight disruptions [^r19]. He identified that obsolete lead-time parameters had caused replenishment runs to assume earlier receipt dates, creating tyre shortages on the Manchester assembly line and exhausting buffer stock at Leeds [^r20]. He scheduled a review of reorder thresholds with Moreno ahead of the next batch run following the vendor lead-time adjustments [^r21].

On 26 May 2026, he issued instructions to stores enforcing strict handling and labelling protocols for returnable crates sent back from bike shops to Leeds and Manchester [^r22].

On 25 September 2026, Mensah queried Daniel Okafor on whether a 1 October 2026 price revision from Voltway Cells would impact open purchase order PO/2026/01120 [^r23]. Okafor confirmed to Mensah that the BAT-500 unit price remained fixed at £114.00, rejecting Voltway Cells' proposed £119.00 quote [^r24].
