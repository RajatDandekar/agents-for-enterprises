D. Okafor is a staff member in the Purchase department at Northbridge Cycles [^r1]. On 2026-10-12, Okafor authored [[amd-pur-2026-03|AMD-PUR-2026-03]], an upcoming amendment to the [[pol-pur-001|Purchase Policy]] [^r1].
