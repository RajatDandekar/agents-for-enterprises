Northbridge Cycles organised an inter-site charity cycle ride between the Manchester plant and the Leeds unit following canal towpaths and minor roads over the Pennines to raise funds for local hospices [^r1]. The event established operational procedures for internal equipment loans, route maintenance, and battery handling across sites [^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5]. It documents the integration of departmental resources, including stores logistics and accounts handling, for company-wide community initiatives [^r2][^r6].

## Route and Support Logistics

The cycle ride followed a route along canal towpaths and minor roads across the Pennines connecting the Manchester and Leeds facilities [^r1]. To support participants along the course, the purchasing department sourced spare components, inner tubes, tyres, and repair tools from regular production stores for roving support vans [^r2]. The purchasing team also procured high-visibility vests, rain capes, and protective equipment from standard indirect consumables vendors [^r2].

Emergency mechanical repairs along the Pennine route were coordinated directly with local bike shops [^r3]. Overnight battery charging facilities were established at designated rest halts using standard production charging rigs [^r3].

## Equipment and Battery Preparation

Any Northbridge Cycles frame or drive system taken from the Manchester or Leeds facilities for the event required prior consent from the shop floor manager [^r4]. All borrowed equipment had to be signed out through the internal loan register and undergo inspection by a senior assembly technician prior to use [^r4].

Riders using electric bicycles were subject to specific battery transport protocols [^r5]. Participants were required to clearly label spare battery packs with rider details and hand them over to the logistics team prior to departure so they could be carried in support vehicles [^r5].

## Donations and Fundraising

The event raised money for hospices located in Greater Manchester and West Yorkshire [^r6]. Funds were collected either through payroll giving arranged via the accounts team or by banking cash collections directly through the cashier counter [^r6].
