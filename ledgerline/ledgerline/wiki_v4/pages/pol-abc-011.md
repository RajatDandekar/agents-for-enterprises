POL-ABC-011 governs the acceptance and offering of gifts, hospitality, and inducements across Northbridge Cycles, operating under Finance ownership since 1 January 2024 [^r1]. It establishes strict reporting controls including mandatory declaration of gifts exceeding £50, a total ban on gifts during active tenders, and advance approval thresholds for corporate hospitality [^r6][^r7][^r11]. Compliance applies to all personnel to ensure commercial decisions remain uncompromised and fully documented [^r2][^r3].

## Clauses

### 1. Principles
Northbridge Cycles prohibits offering or accepting inducements to influence commercial decisions, secure contracts, or gain improper advantage with suppliers, bike shops, or public bodies [^r2]. All gifts or offers of hospitality must be declared through line management before acceptance, or immediately following receipt where advance notice was impossible, and recorded in the central compliance register [^r3]. Payments to third parties must follow established finance controls and be supported by proper invoices and standard purchase orders [^r4]. Employees must never accept cash, gift vouchers, or loans under any circumstances [^r5]. Staff must decline any offer of gifts, entertainment, or hospitality made during active tender processes or price negotiations, regardless of value [^r6].

### 2. Gifts
Any gift worth more than £50 from a supplier or customer must be declared in the gifts register within five working days [^r7]. The gifts register is held by the company secretary, and for gifts over £50 department heads determine whether the item is retained, shared with the wider team, or returned [^r8]. Purchasing staff must not accept any gift, regardless of value, from a supplier taking part in an open quotation or tender [^r9]. The prohibition on accepting gifts from suppliers in an open tender applies to all purchasing staff, even if the individual is not the lead buyer on that tender [^r10].

### 3. Hospitality
Hospitality worth more than £150 a head requires advance written approval from the [[role-plant-head|plant head]] via the internal e-mail system [^r11]. When calculating the cost per head for hospitality against the £150 threshold, staff must include travel, admission passes, and related extras [^r12].
