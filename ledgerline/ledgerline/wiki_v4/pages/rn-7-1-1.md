Ledgerline ERP 7.1.1 is a maintenance release issued on 12 February 2018 that provides agreed system modifications for Northbridge Cycles Ltd to track, dispatch, and return demonstration electric bicycles [^r1]. It introduces dedicated loan functionality under change code [[screen-sal-dmo-17|SAL-DMO-17]] in the Sales module [^r2]. This release formally retired the earlier practice of recording bike loans as zero-value sales ledger orders [^r3].

## Changes

The sole functional enhancement in Ledgerline ERP 7.1.1 implements demonstration bike loan handling under change code [[screen-sal-dmo-17|SAL-DMO-17]] within the Sales module [^r2]. 

The release notes also identify two known issues:
* Multi-line item descriptions wrap incorrectly on standard impact printer drivers when printing purchase orders or dispatch notes, causing lower summary fields to shift across lower perforations [^r7].
* Opening the component search window from the works order entry screen on large assembly structures causes significant delays, which affected users at the Leeds unit [^r8].

## Effect at Northbridge

Key users must not book loans as zero-value orders on the standard sales ledger, as the dedicated routines replace that workaround entirely [^r3]. Before any demo loan can be booked, retailer accounts must undergo credit and account status verification [^r4].

At dispatch, staff must confirm serial numbers at the point of release so that specific frame and battery records remain linked to the borrowing retailer until returned [^r5]. Stock control staff are required to review open loan listings on a weekly basis to follow up on overdue bicycles with the relevant sales representatives [^r6]. To mitigate search delays on large assembly structures in works order entry, operators at Leeds are advised to enter the primary part group first to restrict returned records [^r8].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Today's rule**: Demo electric bicycle loans are processed through dedicated demo loan routines under [[screen-sal-dmo-17|SAL-DMO-17]] [^r2][^r3].
* **Older rule**: Demo loans were booked as zero-value orders on the standard sales ledger [^r3].
* **Sources and dates**: RN-7.1.1 (12 February 2018) [^r1][^r3].
* **Kind**: `superseded` [^r3].
