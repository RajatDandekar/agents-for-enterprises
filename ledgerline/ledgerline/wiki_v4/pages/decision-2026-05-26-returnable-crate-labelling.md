This decision sets out operational rules agreed on 26 May 2026 for the outbound preparation, transit handling, and depot receipt of returnable crates [^r1][^r4]. It establishes mandatory labelling practices and carrier checks to avoid misrouting between sites and bike shop customers [^r1][^r2][^r3]. It also enforces intake quarantine and inventory reconciliation in Ledgerline ERP for all returned crates arriving at Manchester and Leeds [^r4][^r5][^r6].

## Decision details

Before returnable crates leave the loading bay, reusable plastic sleeves must always be attached to the outer battens [^r1]. Each returnable crate must carry the standard destination slip on the side panel [^r2]. Drivers must verify that returning transit labels are present on returnable crates before signing carrier paperwork [^r3].

## Receiving and reconciliation

Upon the arrival of empty crates in Manchester or Leeds, goods receiving must immediately peel off old routing stickers [^r4]. If an unlabelled crate arrives, staff must quarantine it in the holding area until stores traces the delivery note against the dispatch log [^r5]. Stores books returnable packaging into Ledgerline ERP to reconcile balances against customer bike shop accounts [^r6].
