This decision mandates that incoming battery packs from [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] bypass the standard quality quarantine location and transfer directly into Manchester main stores. Operating with immediate effect from 15 June 2026, the exception applies specifically to [[items-bat|BAT-* items]] supplied by Voltway Cells rather than remaining in general goods inward or staging in quarantine [^r1][^r2]. Quality assurance sampling is conducted at the receiving bay prior to stock put-away [^r4].

## Context and justification

Under standard operating rules, lithium-cell shipments are routed through inspection holding areas prior to release [^r5]. Voltway Cells qualified for direct receipt into main stores (WH-MAN-STR) because the supplier maintains a valid [[topic-un-38-3-certificates|UN 38.3 certificate]] on file and has maintained two consecutive years without a single rejected lot [^r3].

## Receiving and inspection process

With immediate effect from 15 June 2026, all Voltway Cells battery packs ([[items-bat|BAT-* items]]) are booked straight into Manchester main stores (WH-MAN-STR) rather than being routed into WH-MAN-QC on receipt [^r2]. Quality Manager [[user-pjansen|Pieter Jansen]] takes the required Acceptance Quality Limit (AQL) sample directly from the receiving bay before put-away into store racking occurs [^r4]. An e-mail directive dated 15 June 2026 confirms that battery packs arriving from Voltway Cells must go straight into WH-MAN-STR rather than remaining in Manchester general goods inward [^r1].

All other lithium-cell parts from other vendors must continue to be received into WH-MAN-QC as stipulated in the standard goods receipt procedure [^r5].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Today's rule (in force from 2026-06-15):** Voltway Cells [[items-bat|BAT-* items]] are received directly into WH-MAN-STR, bypassing WH-MAN-QC, with AQL sampling completed at the receiving bay prior to put-away [^r2][^r4].
* **Standard procedure:** The general goods receipt procedure stipulates that all lithium-cell parts must be received into WH-MAN-QC for incoming inspection [^r5].

* **Kind:** `email_overrides_sop`. The operational e-mail from Quality Manager [[user-pjansen|Pieter Jansen]] overrides the standard incoming inspection procedure for this specific qualified supplier [^r2][^r4][^r5].
