SOP-AP-002 governs the month-end closing routine for the accounts payable sub-ledger across Northbridge Cycles [^r1][^r2]. Owned by [[user-mkowalski|Marta Kowalski]] and approved by [[user-gwhitaker|Grace Whitaker]], it coordinates cut-off deadlines, accrual jobs, and sub-ledger locking [^r1][^r5]. As of June 2026, the operational invoice cut-off occurs at 12:00 on working day 2 rather than working day 3 to accelerate board reporting [^r17].

## Steps

### Preparation and cut-off
In daily duties, the Manchester purchase ledger clerk registers incoming delivery notes, matches site invoices, and reviews the unregistered invoice log, while the Leeds administrator collects delivery paperwork, logs workshop receipts, and forwards matched invoices to central finance [^r7]. Paper delivery notes from the Leeds receiving team must reach accounts payable by the morning cut-off deadline to facilitate month-end matching [^r15]. Prior to the ledger freeze, purchasing must complete all entry work, agree uninvoiced delivery accruals with finance, and formally notify the Manchester finance team [^r11][^r12][^r13]. Leaving orders pending in draft status prevents finance from agreeing accruals and introduces discrepancies with goods inwards [^r16].

The accounts payable invoice cut-off takes place at 12:00 on working day 2 [^r17]. Invoices arriving after this cut-off are held over and posted into the subsequent accounting period, with late items managed through an accrual entry approved by the financial controller [^r14][^r18].

### Accruals and ledger lock
On working day 1 at 21:00, the goods received not invoiced (GRNI) accrual batch job JOB-AP-040 runs [^r3].

The accounts supervisor inspects disputed balances, reviews parked documents, and verifies high-volume supplier statement reconciliations, while the financial controller examines the preliminary aged creditors trial balance and proposed accruals [^r8].

On the afternoon of working day 3, the AP manager executes the period close routine on screen [[screen-ap-per-11|AP-PER-11]], calling backend service `per_close` from [[services-ap-per|services-ap-per]] [^r5][^r10]. Ledgerline refuses the close and issues error [[err-per-0010|ERR-PER-0010]] if unposted documents remain, or error ERR-PER-0011 from [[errors-per|errors-per]] if the GRNI accrual job has not been executed [^r5][^r10].

### Reporting and statutory sign-off
On working day 4, Finance reviews the reverse-charge listing generated by job JOB-AP-045 to prepare the VAT return [^r6]. 

Following the ledger lock, the finance clerk prints the final aged creditors listing, the period summary report, and the reconciliation sheet [^r9]. These reports must be countersigned by the finance manager together with the bank reconciliation, then archived in the monthly purchase ledger binder located in Manchester [^r9]. Final close sign-off is completed by the financial controller [^r8].

## Versions and conflicts

* **AP Invoice Cut-off**: In force since the June 2026 close, the AP invoice cut-off is 12:00 on working day 2, established via management decision to enable earlier board reporting; invoices received after this deadline are posted to the following period and accrued with controller sign-off [^r14][^r17][^r18]. SOP-AP-002 v3.0 specifies a cut-off at 12:00 on working day 3 [^r4]. The written procedure has not yet been reissued to incorporate the change (`email_overrides_sop`) [^r4][^r17].
