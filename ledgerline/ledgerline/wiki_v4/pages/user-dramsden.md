## Service Definitions and System Maintenance

Derek Ramsden left Northbridge Cycles in 2012[^r1][^r9]. Prior to his departure, he served as the owner and maintainer of several core XML gateway service definitions across accounts payable and sales modules[^r1][^r5][^r12][^r13][^r15]. Following his departure in 2012, system ownership of these definitions was transferred to Joanna Fletcher (JFLETCHER) in IT[^r2][^r3][^r4][^r6][^r7][^r8][^r10][^r11][^r14].

These transferred service definitions encompass:
* Accounts payable banking services, including `bnk_get`, `bnk_save`, `bnk_search`, and `bnk_verify` in [[services-ap-bnk]][^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4].
* Accounts payable payment services, including `pay_approve`, `pay_file`, `pay_get`, and `pay_propose` in [[services-ap-pay]][^r5][^r6][^r7][^r8].
* Sales EDI integrations, including `edi_desadv_out`, `edi_invoic_out`, `edi_ord_ack`, and `edi_ord_in` in [[services-sal-edi]][^r9][^r10][^r11][^r12].
* Sales freight and carrier interfaces, including `frt_book`, `frt_quote`, and `frt_track` in [[services-sal-frt]][^r13][^r14][^r15].

The XML exports for `bnk_get` and `edi_desadv_out` were generated on 3 November 2016, noting Ramsden's earlier 2012 departure[^r1][^r9]. Historically, Derek Ramsden in IT was also the documented owner of the superseded Ledgerline 6 Purchasing Quick Guide version 6.4, which took effect on 1 May 2009[^r41].

## System Configuration Log

In [[data-nbc-change-log|NBC_customisations_and_patch_log]], entry NBC-2015-004 records that Derek Ramsden configured parameter `PUR.DIR_LIMIT` to 30000 on 9 March 2015 in accordance with company policy v3.0[^r16].

## Intranet Documentation Attribution

A large batch of purchasing training and procedure pages exported from the legacy server NBC-INTRANET01 in 2019 attribute their last modification to Derek Ramsden under user account DRAMSDEN on 3 November 2016[^r17][^r20][^r23][^r30][^r36][^r39]. In the 2016 intranet directory, Ramsden was listed as IT manager at extension 2201, and he was recorded as the author or last modifier of the contacts page on 3 November 2016[^r18][^r19].

Legacy intranet articles modified on 3 November 2016 under user ID DRAMSDEN include:
* Core procedural guides: the Purchasing homepage[^r22], the training guide 'How to raise an order'[^r21], and the approval limits reference page[^r17].
* Operational instructions: error message catalogues[^r20], purchasing frequently asked questions regarding supplier setup[^r24], the glossary of Ledgerline terms covering receipt and matching[^r25], and system performance tips in 'Ledgerline tips and tricks'[^r29].
* Warehouse, inventory, and finance guidance: goods receipt tips[^r26], warehouse health and safety covering battery storage and hazardous goods[^r27], finance collaboration guidelines for month-end close[^r28], the 2016 purchasing month-end checklist[^r31], working with stores on stocktakes[^r38], and the 2016 year-end stock count instructions for off-site inventory[^r39].
* Supplier and product registers: the supplier list[^r40], supplier visits procedures[^r36], guidelines on European suppliers covering invoice and payment processing[^r37], quality notices and vendor ratings[^r35], packaging ideas for small items[^r33], the 2016 e-bike range overview[^r32], the Spring 2016 purchasing team news[^r34], the charity cycle ride announcement[^r23], and Leeds team administration contacts[^r30].

## Versions and conflicts

* **Contradiction (Departure date vs. subsequent activity stamps)**: Multiple service definitions explicitly record that Derek Ramsden left Northbridge Cycles in 2012, with service ownership transferred to Joanna Fletcher in IT[^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4][^r6][^r7][^r8][^r9][^r10][^r11][^r14]. However, configuration log NBC-2015-004 attributes a system change on 9 March 2015 to Derek Ramsden[^r16], and 24 legacy intranet training pages record last modification by user account DRAMSDEN on 3 November 2016, including the 2016 year-end stock count guidance which notes this discrepancy directly[^r17][^r19][^r20][^r21][^r22][^r23][^r24][^r25][^r26][^r27][^r28][^r29][^r30][^r31][^r32][^r33][^r34][^r35][^r36][^r37][^r38][^r39][^r40].
* **Superseded (Documentation ownership)**: Derek Ramsden was the documented owner of the Ledgerline 6 Purchasing Quick Guide version 6.4, effective 1 May 2009[^r41]. This guide is superseded, and system ownership of service definitions previously held by Ramsden was reassigned to Joanna Fletcher[^r2][^r4][^r7][^r10][^r41].
