Cost centre PRD-07 (Spring launch run) is an active production cost centre in manufacturing unit NBC-MAN-01 under owner [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor (DOKAFOR)]][^r1]. It covers the Medlock 2 spring launch run building at Manchester, which starts assembly in January 2027 and targets approximately 600 battery packs a day once at rate[^r11][^r12][^r13]. The cost centre was opened effective 1 October 2026 to capture maintenance call-outs, non-capitalised workshop consumables, and advance material purchase commitments ahead of pre-series validation[^r10][^r14].

## Purpose and operational scope

Cost centre PRD-07 was established effective 1 October 2026 for the Medlock 2 launch run, with assembly scheduled to commence in January 2027[^r10][^r13][^r14]. It captures maintenance call-outs and non-capitalised workshop consumables required ahead of pre-series validation[^r10]. In full production, the launch run aims to build roughly 600 battery packs per day[^r11]. 

In purchasing and requisition workflows, PRD-07 serves as the default cost centre for Purchase manager [[user-dokafor|Daniel Okafor]][^r7]. All launch orders and requisitions for launch run battery packs must be charged against PRD-07 to commit assembly materials to the Manchester shop floor[^r12][^r14].
