The `FRM-*` family encompasses all structural bicycle frames used across Northbridge Cycles production lines, spanning models such as Ancoats, Deansgate, Medlock, Irk, Tamworth, Rochdale, and Spryte [^r52]. All frame items are classified under category RAW with lead-time code LC 0 [^r52]. Stock is held and issued primarily out of Manchester stores (WH-MAN-STR) and Leeds stores (WH-LDS-STR) to support manufacturing work orders [^r1][^r2][^r65].
