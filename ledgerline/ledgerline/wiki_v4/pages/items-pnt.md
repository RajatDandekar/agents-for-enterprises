The PNT-* item family covers powder coat finishes stocked for bicycle frame treatment and finishing across Northbridge Cycles production sites. As of 26 October 2026 at 09:00 GMT, inventory is primarily held at WH-MAN-STR with secondary balances at WH-LDS-STR, supporting production runs alongside [[items-frm|frame components]] and surface preparation items like [[items-prm|primers]] and [[items-lac|lacquers]]. Full current stock figures and commitments are tracked through [[data-stock-on-hand|Stock_on_hand_2026-10-26_0900]].

## Stock levels and allocation

Inventory balances as of 26 October 2026 at 09:00 GMT are distributed across locations as follows:

* **PNT-BLK** (black powder coat): 802 units on hand and 158 allocated at WH-MAN-STR [^r1].
* **PNT-GRN** (green powder coat): 4,325 units on hand and 261 allocated at WH-MAN-STR [^r2].
* **PNT-SLV** (silver powder coat): 28 units on hand, 11 allocated, and 50 on order at WH-MAN-STR [^r3].
* **PNT-RED** (red powder coat): 1,842 units on hand, 136 allocated, and 800 on order at WH-MAN-STR, with an additional 791 units on hand at WH-LDS-STR [^r4].
* **PNT-BLU** (blue powder coat): 33 units on hand and 12 allocated at WH-MAN-STR, alongside 24 units on hand at WH-LDS-STR [^r5].
* **PNT-WHT** (white powder coat): 112 units on hand and 5 allocated at WH-MAN-STR [^r6].
* **PNT-GRY** (grey powder coat): 67 units on hand and 12 allocated at WH-MAN-STR [^r7].
