## Responsibilities and System Ownership

Joanna Fletcher serves as the documented IT owner for core Ledgerline SOAP gateway service definitions, succeeding [[user-dramsden|Derek Ramsden]][^r5][^r9][^r34]. Her verified ownership encompasses Accounts Payable bank services (`bnk_block`, `bnk_get`, `bnk_save`, `bnk_search`, and `bnk_verify`) managed via [[services-ap-bnk|AP bnk services]] and [[screen-ap-bnk-06|AP-BNK-06]][^r5][^r6][^r7][^r8][^r9].
