The Ledgerline glossary defines standard operational and transactional terminology used across procurement, stock control, and finance workflows. It provides foundational reference concepts for purchasing controls, supplier relationships, inventory tracking, and invoice validation. These standard definitions govern daily procurement processing and receiving across Northbridge facilities.

## Master records

* **Approved Vendor**: A supplier that has completed required commercial checks and quality reviews, without which purchasing orders cannot be raised[^r1].
* **Part Profile**: The central master record defining an item's accepted technical specification, standard lead period, default packaging quantity, and default stock location[^r2].
* **Source List**: Maintains the link between a component part and permitted vendors, denoting the primary vendor and any agreed secondary suppliers[^r3].
* **Nominal Allocation**: The default financial ledger string assigned to a component or non-stock purchase that dictates departmental cost bearing upon invoice entry[^r4].

## Purchasing transactions

* **Purchase Requisition**: Requires departmental manager authorisation before the buying office can convert it into an order[^r5].
* **Purchase Order**: Acts as the formal contract sent to an approved vendor committing the business to agreed prices and delivery schedule once approved[^r6].
* **Blanket Order**: Established with an agreed vendor for regular deliveries of high-volume items such as frame hardware, wiring looms, or motor housings[^r7].
* **Scheduled Call-off**: A release document generated against an active blanket order instructing delivery of specific quantities to Manchester or Leeds by an agreed day[^r8].

## Receipt and matching

* **Goods Inward Sheet**: Created by the stores team at the gate to register physical receipt of containers prior to inspection or inventory booking[^r9].
* **Inspection Hold**: Stock placed under this status cannot be transferred to production lines or issued to assembly kits until cleared by quality assurance[^r10].
* **Discrepancy Note**: Generated when delivered goods deviate in quantity or specification from the original order, or arrive with transport damage[^r11].
* **Three-Way Match**: Ledgerline's automated function verifying that purchase order, goods received record, and vendor invoice prices and quantities align within agreed tolerances[^r12].
