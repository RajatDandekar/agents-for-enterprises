C. Shaw appears in Northbridge Cycles records as warehouse count personnel at Manchester Main Stores[^r1]. On 25 March 2023, Shaw acted as the count checker for Count Team 3 in Aisle B during the Annual Stock Count[^r1].

## Record

C. Shaw participated in the Annual Stock Count conducted on 25 March 2023 at Manchester Main Stores[^r1]. Assigned to Count Team 3 alongside warehouse staff covering Aisle B, Shaw carried out the duties of count checker for recorded inventory sheets[^r1]. Stock counting procedures at Northbridge facilities follow [[sop-inv-003|SOP-INV-003]].
