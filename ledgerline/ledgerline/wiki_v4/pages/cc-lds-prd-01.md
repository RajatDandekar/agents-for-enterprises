Cost centre LDS-PRD-01 covers Leeds assembly operations within operating unit NBC-LDS-01 under owner BPRICE [^r1]. As of 26 October 2026 09:00 GMT, it operates with an OPEN status and has an available 2026-Q4 budget of £99,798.63 against an allocated £128,000 [^r1][^r2]. The cost centre is actively debited for assembly materials, structural frames, hardware, packaging, and braking components delivered to Leeds storage [^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6].

## Budget and status

Cost centre LDS-PRD-01 is OPEN in operating unit NBC-LDS-01 under owner BPRICE [^r1]. For 2026-Q4 as of 26 October 2026 09:00, cost centre LDS-PRD-01 has a budget amount of £128,000, committed spend of £28,201.37, and available budget of £99,798.63 [^r2]. Details of its master setup and quarterly allocations are registered in [[data-cost-centres-and-budgets|Cost Centres and Budgets FY2026-27]] [^r1][^r2].
