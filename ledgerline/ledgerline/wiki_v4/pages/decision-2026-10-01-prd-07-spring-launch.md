Operational directive issued via e-mail on 1 October 2026 establishing cost centre [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] for the Medlock 2 spring launch run building in January [^r1]. Purchasing must allocate all purchase orders for the spring launch run to [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] [^r2]. Cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] closed on 15 October 2026 [^r3].

## Context and instructions

Cost centre [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] (Spring launch run) opened on 1 October 2026 to support the Medlock 2 spring launch run scheduled for assembly in January [^r1]. Purchasing is required to charge every purchase order related to this spring launch run directly to cost centre [[cc-prd-07|PRD-07]] [^r2]. Cost centre [[cc-prd-05|PRD-05]] (City e-bike line) closes on 15 October 2026 [^r3].
