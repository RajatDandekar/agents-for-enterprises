Ethan Chen (user ID `ECHEN`) is a sales representative at Northbridge Cycles responsible for managing regional retail accounts and processing incoming orders[^r1][^r2]. In the customer register, he serves as the primary sales contact for bike shops across the UK and mainland Europe[^r1]. In 2026 transactions, he actively books sales orders for commercial accounts[^r2].

## Account assignments

Ethan Chen is the assigned sales representative in [[data-customers|Customers (bike shops)]] for several customer accounts, including Groningen Wheels, The Ilkley Bike Works, The Leuven Bike Shop, and The Kendal Pedal Power[^r1].

## Sales transactions

In [[data-sales-orders-2026|sales_orders_2026]], Chen acts as the booking sales representative for 2026 sales orders, including order `SO/2026/000002` for Kendal Spokes and order `SO/2026/000004` for Annecy Pedal Power[^r2].
