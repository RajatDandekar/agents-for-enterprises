## Operational Issues and Context

On 2 March 2023, [[user-sberg|Stefan Berg]], Bronwen Price, Marta Kowalski, Grace Whitaker, [[user-eclarke|Emma Clarke]], and [[user-cshaw|Callum Shaw]] attended the regular production planning meeting [^r1].

Multiple site-specific bottlenecks and supply concerns were reviewed during the meeting:
* At Manchester, frame preparation ran ahead of sub-assembly, causing gangway congestion near the paint area because secondary racking was occupied by boxed motors awaiting bench inspection [^r2].
* Wheel building at the Leeds facility encountered issues with carrier-delivered rims showing inconsistent spoke-hole deburring, which required manual fettling prior to lacing [^r3].
* Physical bin counts and the central system were out of step for small hardware such as cable guides and crank bolts, prompting assembly leads to borrow from service stock when terminal quantities fell short [^r4].
* Purchasing reported that the principal battery supplier warned of delayed sea consignments caused by container availability constraints at the departure port, leading purchasing to request that production preserve serviceable warranty returns for use on test rigs [^r6].

## Agreed Decisions and Procedural Changes

To resolve inventory mismatches, quality compliance, and dispatch routing, the following operational procedures were agreed:
* **Hardware stock transfers**: Stores instructed assembly leads to log all inter-departmental hardware movements immediately on paper transfer sheets instead of waiting until shift end [^r5].
* **Torque wrench calibration**: Quality assurance mandated that final assembly station torque wrench calibration clipboards must be initialled at the start of each shift before completed bicycles can receive final sign-off tags [^r7].
* **Retailer courier collections**: Works orders designated for direct collection by retailer couriers must be marked clearly on the dispatch whiteboard to prevent accidental consolidation into standard carrier runs [^r8].
* **Leeds extraction duct maintenance**: Scheduled cleaning and filter changes on the Leeds prep bay extraction ducting must be conducted during shift changeover to avoid halting preparation work [^r9].
