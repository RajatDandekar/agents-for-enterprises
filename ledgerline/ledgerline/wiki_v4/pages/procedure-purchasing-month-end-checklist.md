The purchasing month-end checklist defines the required purchasing closing tasks across both the Manchester factory and the Leeds unit [^r1]. It governs the review of unbilled receipts, purchase order housekeeping, query resolution, and formal handover to finance [^r1][^r2][^r14]. Compliance ensures physical deliveries match ledger records and unposted paperwork is eliminated before the sub-ledger is frozen [^r2][^r14].

## Steps

### Goods received not invoiced (GRNI) review
Purchasing staff must review the goods received not invoiced (GRNI) report in full to verify physical receipts match ledger records and resolve temporary items before closing [^r2]. Staff must check with warehouse teams in Manchester and Leeds to confirm all delivery notes for the month are keyed into the system [^r3]. Long-standing unbilled receipts must be investigated with suppliers to obtain missing invoices or credit notes where deliveries remain unmatched [^r4]. Purchasing must agree accrual adjustments with accounts for deliveries received without paperwork before the cut-off [^r5].

### Purchase order housekeeping
Completed purchase orders with residual balances from short deliveries or minor price variances must be closed or cancelled during housekeeping [^r6]. On-hold orders must be reviewed to resolve disputes regarding prices, damaged parts, or missing quality certificates [^r7]. Purchasing staff must verify that amended purchase order lines have received proper internal authorisations before the period cut-off [^r8]. Delivery schedules for frame fabrications, motors, battery packs, and wiring looms must reflect realistic supplier lead times for the following month [^r9].

### Supplier query resolution and ledger reconciliation
Purchasing officers must resolve complex invoice discrepancies with suppliers when matching approved invoices against POs and deliveries [^r10]. Approved credit notes for returned materials, scrap, or warranty claims must be forwarded to the purchase ledger clerk for immediate allocation [^r11]. Purchasing must confirm that direct deliveries to bike shops for warranty or fleet replacements are confirmed and receipted in the system [^r12]. The debit balance report must be reviewed at month-end to investigate payments on account and unallocated credit notes [^r13].

### Final period sign-off
Purchasing must verify no unposted paper delivery notes or supplier invoices remain in trays at either site before notifying finance in Manchester to freeze the sub-ledger [^r14]. Signed supplier delivery notes, completed rejection slips, and order change forms must be filed in departmental archives in line with company procedures [^r15].
