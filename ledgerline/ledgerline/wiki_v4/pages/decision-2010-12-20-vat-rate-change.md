Finance department memorandum establishing the operational transition following the increase in the UK standard VAT rate to 20% on 4 January 2011 [^r1]. It instructs Ledgerline users on the closure of the old rate code and the mandatory adoption of the new code for UK suppliers [^r2][^r3]. Existing purchase orders posted prior to the change maintain their original tax rates [^r4].

## Details

On 4 January 2011, the UK VAT standard rate increased to 20% [^r1]. Tax code V175-UK was closed on 3 January 2011 [^r2]. Effective 4 January 2011, Ledgerline users must select tax code VAT20-UK for UK suppliers [^r3]. Purchase orders posted prior to the effective date maintain their previously applied tax rate [^r4].
