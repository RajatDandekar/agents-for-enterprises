The NBC Intranet Contacts Directory is an archived reference exported from legacy server NBC-INTRANET01 prior to its decommissioning [^r10]. It documents operational reporting lines, escalation paths, and departmental contact procedures across the Manchester factory and Leeds unit as established in November 2016 [^r1][^r10]. As an export retained in training documentation, it provides historical context on legacy workflow routing, Ledgerline support logging, and paper-based administrative approvals [^r3][^r8][^r10].

## What it holds
The directory sets out internal contact routes between the Manchester factory and Leeds unit, mandating that routine operational issues be raised via immediate line managers or departmental clerks prior to escalating to central administration [^r1]. It outlines procedures for logging business system support via the internal help desk, processing Ledgerline administrative actions, and reporting hardware faults, noting that manufacturing stoppages and dispatch terminals receive processing priority [^r2][^r3]. It also details routing for parts transport, supplier delivery disputes, retail customer inquiries, paper wage and leave approvals, and facility safety incidents [^r5][^r6][^r7][^r8][^r9].

## Columns
As an HTML document rather than a tabular database, the file organises functional routing by functional topic and departmental responsibility rather than structured field columns [^r1][^r10]. Contact instructions specify operational categories, required reference data (such as batch, order, and site location details), and designated internal destination offices [^r4][^r5][^r8].

## How to look up a row
Staff navigate the source HTML document by locating the relevant functional department or operational query type [^r1][^r10]:
* **System and IT issues:** Support queries for Ledgerline or hardware must be logged through the internal help desk with batch or order details and site location, rather than direct calls to the IT room [^r2][^r3][^r4].
* **Logistics and transfers:** Enquiries regarding parts deliveries, carrier bookings, and site-to-site warehouse transfers between Manchester and Leeds must be directed to the sending location's dispatch office, while supplier or haulier delivery disputes route to the purchasing desk [^r5][^r6].
* **Customer and commercial accounts:** External bike shop queries on accounts, shipments, or warranty returns are routed to customer service representatives via the internal sales line [^r7].
* **Payroll and personnel:** Wages, overtime sheets, and annual leave balances route to the Manchester accounts office following physical card and paper request sign-off by shift supervisors [^r8].
* **Facilities and safety:** Urgent building and safety hazards are reported directly to the works maintenance office [^r9].
