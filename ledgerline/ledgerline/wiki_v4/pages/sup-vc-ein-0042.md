Voltway Cells is an active battery and component supplier based in Eindhoven, Netherlands under code VC-EIN-0042 [^r11][^r48]. It supplies battery packs and frame mounts to Northbridge Cycles under delivered DAP Manchester terms, with battery packs delivered directly into Manchester main stores rather than quality control [^r18][^r64][^r66]. As of October 2026, the supplier continues to provide battery packs for upcoming production launches with an agreed BAT-500 unit rate of £119.00 [^r57][^r75][^r76].

## Record

Voltway Cells is registered in the [[data-supplier-master|supplier master]] under supplier code VC-EIN-0042 as an active battery vendor [^r11][^r48]. Recognized name variants include Voltway Cells B.V. and VoltWay [^r80]. Under [[pol-sup-003|POL-SUP-003]], Voltway Cells in Eindhoven is distinguished as a separate legal entity from [[sup-vpc-rot-0107|Voltway Paints & Coatings]] in Rotterdam by city and VAT number [^r16][^r82]. 

The company was previously registered under code [[sup-vc-ein-0009|VC-EIN-0009]], which was blocked and renamed '(old code, do not use)' following the [[rn-7-1|Ledgerline 7.1]] migration and new numbering scheme approved by G. Sutcliffe on 21 March 2019 [^r15][^r17][^r50][^r52][^r59][^r60]. All purchase orders raised against the legacy code are refused [^r60].

Replenishment lead time from order placement for battery packs is six weeks [^r56]. As of September 2026, the supplier has maintained on-time deliveries with zero rejected lots across two years [^r53]. In early 2021, Northbridge experienced ongoing variance in incoming batches and autumn 2020 transit delays that required supplementary purchase orders to secure production slots [^r61][^r62][^r63].

All agreed contract rates are delivered DAP Manchester, and purchase orders must never include freight lines or charges; any invoice containing freight charges must be returned [^r64][^r65]. Because Voltway Cells holds a valid UN 38.3 report and an unblemished two-year quality record, battery packs ([[items-bat|BAT-* items]]) bypass WH-MAN-QC and are received directly into warehouse WH-MAN-STR per a 15 June 2026 decision [^r18][^r66]. Battery packs must never be left in the Manchester general goods inward bay, and Quality carries out AQL sampling in the receiving bay before put-away [^r19][^r67]. Northbridge continues to order launch packs from Voltway Cells rather than switching to alternatives [^r75].

## Versions and conflicts

* **BAT-500 price increase**: Today's active contract rate for BAT-500 is £119.00 per unit effective 1 October 2026 through 31 March 2027 [^r3][^r57][^r68][^r74][^r76]. On 24 September 2026, Femke de Vries notified Northbridge of the price adjustment caused by rising cell prices [^r54][^r68][^r70][^r71][^r72]. Daniel Okafor sent an email on 25 September 2026 rejecting the increase and insisting the rate stay at £114.00 [^r73]. However, the updated rate was formalized in the rate contracts register, verified in SOP-PUR-001, and confirmed in subsequent correspondence [^r3][^r57][^r74][^r76] (`contradiction`).
* **Supplier code migration**: Active supplier code VC-EIN-0042 replaces the superseded code VC-EIN-0009 following the March 2019 Ledgerline 7.1 system update [^r11][^r50][^r52][^r59][^r79]. The legacy code was designated 'old code, do not use' and set to blocked status in master listings [^r12][^r15][^r17][^r49][^r60][^r78][^r81] (`superseded`).
* **Contract rate revisions (2025-26 to 2026-27)**: Rates for 2026-27 superseded the 2025-26 contracts across all items: BAT-375 increased from £89.00 to £92.00 [^r1][^r6]; BAT-500 increased from £109.50 to £114.00 (and subsequently to £119.00) [^r2][^r3][^r7]; BAT-750 increased from £141.00 to £146.00 [^r4][^r8]; MNT-FRM-01 increased from £17.90 to £18.50 [^r5][^r9]; and MNT-RCK-02 increased from £15.20 to £15.75 [^r5][^r10] (`superseded`).
