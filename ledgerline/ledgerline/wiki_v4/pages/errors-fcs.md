ERR-FCS error codes are generated by Ledgerline sales forecasting routines during forecast entry, record retrieval, and forecast uploads [^r1][^r2][^r3][^r4]. In transactional operations on [[screen-sal-fcs-14|SAL-FCS-14 Sales Forecast Entry]], they signal either missing forecast records or validation failures on forecast quantity and period availability [^r1][^r2][^r3]. Discrepancies exist between the system service definitions and [[man-sal-72|MAN-SAL-72 Ledgerline ERP 7.2 User Manual: Sales and]] regarding the assignment of codes ERR-FCS-0001 and ERR-FCS-0201 across [[services-sal-fcs|SAL fcs services]] [^r1][^r2][^r3][^r5].

### ERR-FCS-0001

* **Message:** SALES FORECAST NOT FOUND [^r2].
* **Cause:** In the service definition, service `fcs_get` raises this error when a specified `fcs_id` cannot be found [^r2]. According to manual [[man-sal-72|MAN-SAL-72]], it is raised during forecast creation or update when mandatory fields (ITEM CD, PERIOD, QTY) are missing, the target calendar period is closed, or a duplicate record encounters a database lock [^r1].
* **Remedy:** Verify that the forecast identifier exists before querying, ensure mandatory fields (ITEM CD, PERIOD, QTY) are populated, verify that the calendar period is open, and retry if locked by a concurrent session [^r1][^r2].
* **Raised by:** Service `fcs_get` in service definitions; service `fcs_save` in user documentation [^r1][^r2].

### ERR-FCS-0201

* **Message:** QTY MUST BE > 0 [^r3][^r4].
* **Cause:** Raised during sales forecast save or upload operations when the forecast quantity is not greater than zero [^r3][^r4]. In contrast, user manual [[man-sal-72|MAN-SAL-72]] describes ERR-FCS-0201 as an entry lookup failure when the database finds no record matching the supplied FCS ID or ITEM CD and PERIOD combination, or when the specified period is closed to planning entries [^r5].
* **Remedy:** Ensure the forecast quantity value is greater than zero before submitting [[screen-sal-fcs-14|SAL-FCS-14 Sales Forecast Entry]] or executing uploads, and verify that the target planning period is open [^r3][^r4][^r5].
* **Raised by:** Services `fcs_save` and `fcs_upload` in service definitions; service `fcs_get` in user documentation [^r3][^r4][^r5].

## Versions and conflicts

* **ERR-FCS-0001 definition conflict:** The underlying service schema defines ERR-FCS-0001 as "SALES FORECAST NOT FOUND" raised by service `fcs_get` when an `fcs_id` is missing [^r2]. In [[man-sal-72|MAN-SAL-72 v7.2.3]] (2025-01-15, p. 57), ERR-FCS-0001 is instead documented as raised by `fcs_save` when mandatory fields (ITEM CD, PERIOD, QTY) are missing, the target calendar period is closed, or duplicate records hit database locks (`contradiction`) [^r1].
* **ERR-FCS-0201 definition conflict:** The service definitions for `fcs_save` and `fcs_upload` define ERR-FCS-0201 with message "QTY MUST BE > 0" for non-positive forecast quantities [^r3][^r4]. In [[man-sal-72|MAN-SAL-72 v7.2.3]] (2025-01-15, p. 57), ERR-FCS-0201 is documented as raised by `fcs_get` when no record matches the FCS ID or ITEM CD/PERIOD combination, or when the period is closed (`contradiction`) [^r5].
