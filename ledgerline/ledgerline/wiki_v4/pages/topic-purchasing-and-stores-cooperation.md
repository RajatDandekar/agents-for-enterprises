Cooperation guidelines govern the working relationship between purchasing staff and stores teams across the Manchester plant and Leeds unit to maintain stock records and feed assembly lines [^r1]. Purchasing provides advance visibility of supplier shipments on Ledgerline, handles supplier discrepancies, and assists stores with inventory reconciliations [^r1][^r2][^r7]. Following these procedures ensures warehouse space and labour are scheduled effectively and discrepancies are resolved promptly [^r2][^r7].

## Daily communication and delivery notifications

Purchasing staff are required to update purchase orders in Ledgerline as supplier confirmations are received, giving stores early notice of expected intake to plan unloading space and labour [^r2]. Buyers must promptly send an e-mail to the stores supervisor whenever a supplier notifies them of an early, delayed, or split delivery [^r3]. Prior to shipment arrival, purchasing must check that supplier delivery paperwork matches purchase order details to minimise goods inward queries [^r4].

Critical assembly items must be flagged on the system so warehouse staff can fast-track them straight to the shop floor upon arrival [^r5]. When booking material transfers from the Leeds unit into the Manchester facility, internal delivery notes must be verified immediately to log any in-transit shortages [^r6].

## Handling goods inward discrepancies

When deliveries arrive with damage, shortages, or incorrect documentation, stores personnel log receipt discrepancies in Ledgerline, and purchasing buyers are responsible for resolving them with vendors [^r7]. Buyers review shortage reports from goods inward and contact suppliers directly to secure immediate replacements or credit notes [^r8].

When rejected components need manufacturer inspection or rework, buyers organise return transport using approved carriers [^r9]. Buyers must also check with the stores supervisor to establish whether rejected parts should be stored in the quarantine cage or left on the loading bay for collection [^r10]. Delivery discrepancies cannot be cleared on Ledgerline until formal agreement is reached on credit paperwork or replacement stock [^r11].

## Stocktakes and stock control

Purchasing supports stores ahead of scheduled inventory counts by clarifying open supplier consignments, ensuring parts on site awaiting inspection are not double-counted [^r12]. In addition, purchasing investigates recurring count discrepancies on proprietary electric components, cables, and mechanical drive parts [^r13]. Buyers also collaborate with stores to evaluate non-moving and obsolete inventory to arrange vendor returns or scrap obsolete stock [^r14].
