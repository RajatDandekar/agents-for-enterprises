Graham Sutcliffe was Northbridge Cycles' Purchase manager, responsible for purchasing operations and supplier administration across major ERP transitions [^r1]. In 2015, he coordinated the department's cutover to Ledgerline 7 [^r3]. He also served as the escalation authority for unblocking suppliers and overseen legacy supplier code deprecations following the upgrade to [[rn-7-1|Ledgerline 7.1]] [^r2][^r4].

## Responsibilities and history

As of the [[data-intranet-contacts-2016|2016 intranet directory]], Graham Sutcliffe was listed as Purchase manager at telephone extension 2231 [^r1]. Under the 2016 Purchasing intranet guidelines, staff who encountered error [[err-sup-0031|ERR-SUP-0031]] were instructed to contact Sutcliffe directly to unblock supplier records [^r2].

On 16 February 2015, Sutcliffe coordinated the purchasing department's migration to Ledgerline 7, directing staff to audit outstanding purchase orders, supplier records, and stock receipts [^r3]. Later, on 20 March 2019, following the upgrade to Ledgerline 7.1, he notified the purchasing team that [[sup-vc-ein-0042|Voltway Cells]] had been reassigned code VC-EIN-0042 and that the legacy supplier code [[sup-vc-ein-0009|VC-EIN-0009]] was blocked [^r4].
