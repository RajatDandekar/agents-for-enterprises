HR-CONF-011 v0.3 is a draft confidential consultation plan setting out the proposed consolidation of stores operations from the Leeds unit into Manchester main stores. As of 26 October 2026, the plan is not in force and remains strictly confidential to the project steering group. If adopted, collective consultation opens on 11 January 2027 ahead of a proposed operational transfer date of 1 April 2027, placing six stores roles at Leeds at risk of redundancy.

## Clauses

### §1. Proposal

Under draft consultation plan HR-CONF-011 v0.3, it is proposed to move all stores work at the Leeds unit to Manchester main stores from 1 April 2027, placing six stores roles at Leeds at risk of redundancy.[^r1] Physical inventory held at Leeds is intended to be transferred to Manchester main stores, and material requisitions for Leeds assembly work will subsequently be fulfilled directly from Manchester.[^r2]

### §2. Consultation timetable

Collective consultation for the proposed Leeds stores consolidation would open on 11 January 2027 and last at least 30 days.[^r3] No notice of redundancy may be issued while collective consultation remains ongoing.[^r4]

### §3. Communication

Nothing in the Leeds stores consolidation plan may be shared outside the steering group consisting of Henrik Weber, Bronwen Price, and Sofia Lindqvist prior to the announcement.[^r5] Any unexpected operational queries regarding Leeds stockholding are directed to Henrik Weber.[^r6]

## Versions and conflicts

Today (26 October 2026), stores work and physical inventory remain based at Leeds. From 1 April 2027, all stores work and physical inventory are proposed to move to Manchester main stores under HR-CONF-011 v0.3 (source: HR-CONF-011, §1; kind: future_dated).[^r1][^r2]
