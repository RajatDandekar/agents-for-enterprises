HR-CONF-009 is Northbridge Cycles' confidential disciplinary register tracking all formal and informal disciplinary actions across company locations. Individual employee names are excluded from the register and kept strictly within separate HR case files [^r1]. As of 26 October 2026, the register records eight cases opened during 2026 across stores, assembly, purchasing, sales, and quality departments [^r2][^r3][^r4][^r5][^r6][^r7][^r8][^r9].

## Clauses

### 1. Record confidentiality

Names of individuals involved in disciplinary proceedings are maintained separately in HR case files rather than in the register [^r1].

### 2. Case entries (2026)

* **DC-2026-001**: Opened on 19 January 2026 in Manchester stores regarding lateness, resulting in an informal outcome and closed status [^r2].
* **DC-2026-002**: Opened on 3 February 2026 in Leeds assembly for a safety breach involving operating a forklift without a licence, resulting in a first written warning and closed status [^r3].
* **DC-2026-003**: Opened on 27 March 2026 in Manchester assembly concerning absence, ending with an informal outcome and closed status [^r4].
* **DC-2026-004**: Opened on 12 May 2026 in Purchasing regarding an undeclared gift under [[pol-abc-011|POL-ABC-011]], resulting in a first written warning and closed status [^r5].
* **DC-2026-005**: Opened on 30 June 2026 in Manchester stores regarding a dropped battery pack that was not reported, resulting in a final written warning currently under appeal [^r6].
* **DC-2026-006**: Opened on 21 August 2026 in Sales regarding misuse of a company card and remains under open investigation [^r7].
* **DC-2026-007**: Opened on 14 September 2026 in Quality for a falsified inspection record, resulting in dismissal and closed status [^r8].
* **DC-2026-008**: Opened on 6 October 2026 in Leeds stores for lateness, with an informal outcome and open status [^r9].
