HR-CONF-007 version 1.0 sets out the confidential salary bands, variable pay terms, and annual increment rules for Northbridge Cycles purchase department roles and the plant head for the 2026-27 financial year [^r1]. Effective from 2026-04-01, the policy is classified as confidential and is owned by Human Resources under the approval of HR manager Sofia Lindqvist [^r1]. Access is strictly restricted to HR department staff, and the document must not be shared with employees, external managers, or non-HR systems [^r2].

## Clauses

### 1. Use of this page
This document is confidential to the HR department and must not be shared with employees, managers outside HR, or any system that serves other staff [^r2].

### 2. Bands for the purchase department
For the Purchase executive role (PUR-EXEC), the salary band is M2 with an annual fixed pay minimum of £34,000 and maximum of £42,000 [^r3]. For the Senior buyer role (PUR-SR), the salary band is M3 with an annual fixed pay minimum of £43,000 and maximum of £55,000 [^r4]. For the Purchase manager role (PUR-MGR), the salary band is M4 with an annual fixed pay minimum of £58,000 and maximum of £75,000 [^r5]. For the Plant head role (PLANT-HEAD), the salary band is L1 with an annual fixed pay minimum of £110,000 and maximum of £145,000 [^r6]. Variable pay for the purchase department is up to 12% of fixed pay, paid in May based on the year's savings against rate contracts [^r7].

### 3. Increments
The 2026-27 increment pool is 9.5% of fixed pay, and any increment above 14% for an individual requires the plant head's approval [^r8].
