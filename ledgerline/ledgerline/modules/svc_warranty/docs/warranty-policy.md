# Warranty Policy (POL-SVC-021)

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out what Northbridge Cycles repairs or replaces free of charge, for how long, and how the warranty is checked in Ledgerline.

## 2. Warranty periods
| Part group | Parts | Warranty |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Body and base | 60 months |
| Lid | Stainless steel lid | 24 months |
| Handles | Long and short ergonomic grips | 12 months |
| Whistle | Whistle (weight valve) | 6 months |
| Safety valve | Safety valve | 6 months |
| Battery pack | Silicone battery pack | 6 months |

## 3. When the warranty starts
3.1 The warranty starts on the **date of sale to the customer**, as shown on the dealer's invoice.
3.2 Dealers register each sale in Ledgerline (screen SVC-SER-01). If a sale is not registered, the service desk registers it from a copy of the dealer's invoice before checking the warranty.
3.3 **If no sale is registered, Ledgerline counts the warranty from the date of manufacture.** A e-bike that sat in a dealer's shop for months can then look out of warranty when it is not. Always check the basis Ledgerline used (SALE or MFG).

## 4. What the customer gets
4.1 A part that fails in warranty from a manufacturing defect or normal use is replaced free. The dealer fits it.
4.2 A part out of warranty is replaced at the service price plus VAT. The part is issued only after the customer has paid at the dealer and the receipt number is entered.
4.3 A replaced part is never sent out against a different serial number from the one on the e-bike.

## 5. Records
5.1 Every replacement has a service ticket in Ledgerline with the serial number, part, complaint and cause.
5.2 Do not tell the dealer a part has been replaced or dispatched until the ticket shows the part issued (PI) or closed (CL).
