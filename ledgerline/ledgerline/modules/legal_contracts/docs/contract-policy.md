# Contract Policy (POL-LEG-001)

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how Northbridge Cycles raises, reviews, signs and records contracts, so that every contract is on terms we can live with and signed by someone allowed to sign it.

## 2. Scope
It applies to non-disclosure agreements (NDA), master supply agreements (MSA) and service agreements (SLA) for the Manchester plant (NBC-MAN-01) and the Leeds unit (NBC-LDS-01), raised in Ledgerline on screen LEG-REQ-01.

## 3. Our paper first
3.1 Always start from our own template ("our paper"). The current mutual NDA is **NDA-MUT-v4**.
3.2 A contract on our paper with no change to the playbook terms (NDA Playbook, LEG-PB-004) goes straight to the signatory. It does not need Legal.
3.3 A contract on the other side's paper ("their paper") always goes to Legal for a full clause review, whatever it says.

## 4. Counterparties
4.1 Every contract is with a party on the **contract party register**. Register a new party on LEG-REQ-01 with its PAN and VAT number (Counterparty KYC SOP, LEG-SOP-006).
4.2 The contract party register is not the supplier master. **An NDA does not make anyone a supplier.** Do not create a supplier to raise a contract; supplier onboarding is a separate purchase process (Supplier Policy, POL-SUP-003).
4.3 Check the register for the party before registering it. Names repeat: confirm the city and the VAT number.

## 5. Terms
5.1 An NDA runs for at most **3 years** (36 months). A longer term needs Legal's clearance and is the exception.
5.2 Disputes are heard by the courts at **Manchester**. Any other place, in India or abroad, needs Legal's clearance.
5.3 The term starts on the start date and ends the day before the same date in the last year (24 months from 26 Oct 2026 ends on 25 Oct 2028).

## 6. Signature
6.1 Contracts are signed only by the authorised signatory named in the Delegation of Authority (POL-DOA-002).
6.2 Nobody signs or clears a contract they raised.

## 7. Records
7.1 An executed contract goes into the contract register (LEG-REG-03) with its expiry date, and a renewal reminder is set 60 days before it expires.
7.2 Do not tell a counterparty that an agreement is in place until its status in Ledgerline is **Executed (EX)**. A request that is saved, parked, under review or waiting for a signature is not an agreement.
