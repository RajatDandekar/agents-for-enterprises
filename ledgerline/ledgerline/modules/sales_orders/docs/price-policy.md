# Price Policy (POL-SAL-021)

## 1. Purpose
This policy says which price each customer pays for Northbridge e-bikes, so that every quotation can be defended.

## 2. Price lists
2.1 There are four price lists: **DLR** (registered dealers), **DST** (distributors), **RTL** (retailers) and **MRP**. Each customer is assigned **one** list in the customer master. **Dealer price is only for customers registered as dealers (type DLR).** A retailer asking for dealer price is quoted the retail list, or the request goes to the sales manager.
2.2 The list is fixed in Ledgerline. To move a customer to another list, raise a customer master change with the sales manager's approval; never type another list's price.

## 3. Which price
3.1 Quote the price valid **on the quotation date**. The current dealer price for the city e-bike stainless steel e-bike (PC-SS-05L) is £1,450, valid from 1 October 2026.
3.2 **Expired prices must not be used.** Ledgerline checks that a typed rate appears on the customer's price list but does **not** check its dates. Prices from older circulars and old quotations are for reference only.
3.3 The aluminium city e-bike e-bike (PC-AL-05L) is a different product at a different price. "Five-litre e-bike" on its own means the stainless steel PC-SS-05L.

## 4. Quantities
4.1 The minimum quantity for a dealer quotation is 50 pieces of one model.
4.2 A quotation is valid for 15 days unless the sales manager agrees otherwise in writing.

## 5. Tax
5.1 Prices are before VAT. See the VAT note (TAX-NOTE-07) for the tax code.
