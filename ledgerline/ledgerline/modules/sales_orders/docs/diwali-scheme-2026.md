# spring-launch Dealer Scheme 2026 (CIR-SAL-2026-14)

1. From **15 October to 15 November 2026**, registered dealers (type DLR) get a scheme discount of **3%** on the city e-bike and 7.city e-bike stainless steel e-bikes (PC-SS-05L, PC-SS-07L).
2. The scheme applies to quotations issued in the scheme period for at least 200 pieces of one model.
3. The scheme discount is entered in DISC %. It counts towards the discount authority (POL-SAL-022), so a 3% scheme quotation by a sales executive goes to the sales manager in Ledgerline.
4. Distributors, retailers and the factory outlet are not covered.
5. The scheme does not change the list price. Do not type a lower rate.
