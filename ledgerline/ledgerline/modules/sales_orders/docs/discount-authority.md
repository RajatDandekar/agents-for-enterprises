# Discount Authority Matrix (POL-SAL-022)

| Role | Who | Discount on list price, without further approval |
|---|---|---|
| Sales executive (SAL-EXEC) | Arjun Rao | up to 2% |
| Sales manager (SAL-MGR) | Sunita Iyer | up to 5% |
| Plant head (PLANT-HEAD) | Henrik Weber | up to 10% |

Rules
1. A discount above your own authority is entered in DISC % and the quotation is saved; Ledgerline holds it as PA and sends it to your manager. It must not be sent to the customer until it is issued (IS).
2. A lower **rate** is a discount. Never type a lower or older rate to avoid an approval.
3. Nobody approves a discount on their own quotation. The integration service account never approves discounts.
4. Scheme discounts announced by circular count towards these limits.
