# Customer Credit Policy (POL-FIN-031)

## 1. Credit limit
1.1 Every credit customer has a credit limit in the customer master.
1.2 **Exposure** = unpaid invoices (outstanding) + orders open or released but not yet invoiced.

## 2. Credit hold
2.1 When a new order would take exposure above the limit, Ledgerline saves the order **on credit hold (CH)**. The order is saved, but it is not released, no stock is reserved and the warehouse never sees it.
2.2 An order on credit hold must not be promised to the customer or reported as booked for dispatch.
2.3 A credit hold is lifted only by (a) the customer paying enough of the outstanding, or (b) a temporary limit increase approved by the Finance Controller (Vikram Nair). Sales staff cannot lift it.
2.4 Tell the customer the amount by which the order exceeds the limit, and the options in 2.3.

## 3. Cash sales
3.1 A direct cash sale from stock (order type CSH) needs no quotation and no credit check, up to £100,000 gross. Above that, raise a quotation.
