# Leave Approval Matrix (POL-HR-012)

| Who applies | Normal approver | spring-launch run (2–14 Nov), production and stores | Held (HD) applications |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase staff | Purchase manager (DOKAFOR) | not restricted | HR executive (MGOKHALE) clears, then the manager |
| Stores staff | Purchase manager (DOKAFOR) | Plant head (HWEBER) | HR executive clears, then the approver |
| Production staff | Plant head (HWEBER) | Plant head (HWEBER) | HR executive clears, then the plant head |
| Managers | Plant head (HWEBER) | Plant head (HWEBER) | HR executive clears |

Rules
1. The approver decides on screen HR-LV-04 within two working days.
2. Nobody approves their own leave, and the integration service account never approves leave for anyone.
3. If the normal approver is on leave, the application waits; it is not re-routed automatically. Forward it (FWD TO) to the approver's manager.
4. HR does not approve leave. HR only clears held applications: L = accept the unpaid days and send on, R = return to the employee.
