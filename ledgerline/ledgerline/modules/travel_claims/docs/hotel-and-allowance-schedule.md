# Hotel and Daily Allowance Schedule (POL-ADM-022)

Hotel caps are per night, including taxes. The daily allowance (DA) covers food and small expenses and is paid for every day of the trip, counting the day out and the day back.

| Grade | Hotel, class A city | Hotel, class B | Hotel, class C | DA, A | DA, B | DA, C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E2 | £4,000 | £3,000 | £2,000 | £800 | £600 | £500 |
| E3 | £5,500 | £4,000 | £2,500 | £1,000 | £800 | £600 |
| E4 | £6,500 | £5,000 | £3,000 | £1,200 | £1,000 | £700 |
| M1 | £8,000 | £6,000 | £4,000 | £1,500 | £1,200 | £900 |
| M2 | £9,000 | £7,000 | £4,500 | £1,800 | £1,500 | £1,000 |
| M3 | £12,000 | £9,000 | £6,000 | £2,500 | £2,000 | £1,500 |

City classes: **A** Birmingham, Manchester, Rotterdam, Gdańsk. **B** Eindhoven, Lyon, Rajahmundry. **C** Leeds and other towns.

Notes
1. A hotel bill above the cap is paid **up to the cap**. The traveller bears the difference.
2. Local travel (taxi, auto) is paid at actuals with bills. Without bills it is paid up to £300 a day, on the traveller's declaration.
