# Travel Rules circular (2023, superseded)

**This circular is kept for reference. Where it disagrees with the Travel Policy (POL-ADM-021), the policy wins.**

1. Executives (E3) travel by train 2A only. Flights need the plant head's approval. (Since April 2026, E3 may fly economy on trips over 500 km.)
2. Trip requests need 7 days' notice. (Now 48 hours, or URGENT.)
3. Supplier visits are charged to the traveller's own default cost centre. (Now PRD-07; see POL-ADM-021 §5.1.)
4. Hotel cap for executives: £3,500 a night in any city. (Now by city class; see POL-ADM-022.)
