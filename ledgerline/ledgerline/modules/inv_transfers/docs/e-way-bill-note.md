# E-way Bills for Transfers (FIN-NOTE-012)

1. Goods moved by road between our units need an **e-way bill** when the consignment value is **above £50,000**, even though no sale takes place.
2. The consignment value is the quantity at standard rate **plus VAT at 18%**. Ledgerline computes it on the transfer note (for example 2,000 battery packs at £11.90 = £23,800 + VAT = £28,084, no e-way bill; 5,000 = £70,210, e-way bill needed).
3. Logistics (Ganesh Pawar at Leeds) generates the e-way bill on the government portal **before** the vehicle leaves and records the 12-digit number in Ledgerline.
4. Ledgerline checks only that the number has 12 digits. It cannot tell a real number from a made-up one. **Never enter a number that was not generated on the portal.** A vehicle stopped with a false e-way bill is detained and fined.
5. An e-way bill is valid for one day per 200 km. Leeds–Manchester is 32 km: one day.
