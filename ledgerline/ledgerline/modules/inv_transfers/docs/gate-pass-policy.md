# Gate Pass Policy (POL-SEC-004)

## 1. Kinds of gate pass
1.1 **NRGP** (non-returnable): goods leaving for good, including transfers to the other unit.
1.2 **RGP** (returnable): tools, dies or samples that must come back, with a return date.

## 2. Who does what
2.1 The issuing stores creates the gate pass in Ledgerline against an approved transfer note.
2.2 Security checks the vehicle, counts the packages and checks the gate pass **out** in Ledgerline before the vehicle leaves.
2.3 Only vehicles in the vehicle master may carry stores material. MH-14-GH-2209 is the scrap tempo and must not carry production material.

## 3. Day and night
3.1 Day movement is 07:00 to 20:00.
3.2 Ledgerline asks for approval of any night move between units, but sends it to the requester's manager.

## 4. Night movement
4.1 Material leaving a gate between 20:00 and 07:00 is a night movement.
4.2 **Every night movement needs the plant head's approval** (Henrik Weber, HWEBER), whatever its value. Send the transfer note to him (FWD TO: HWEBER). An approval by anyone else does not count, and security will not check the vehicle out.
4.3 Night movements carry a security escort slip signed by the shift supervisor.

## 5. Records
5.1 A gate pass that is created but not checked out within 24 hours is cancelled by security.
