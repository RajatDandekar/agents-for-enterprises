# General Terms of Purchase, extract (CLS-SUP-002)

**Clause 10. Delivery.** The supplier delivers on the dates in the latest released revision of the order. Deliveries are accepted Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 6 PM, except plant holidays.

**Clause 11. Changes and cancellation.**
11.1 Northbridge Cycles may change the quantity or dates of an order by sending a revision.
11.2 If a quantity is cut, or an order short-closed, **within the supplier's lead time before a scheduled delivery**, the supplier may claim the cost of goods already made for that delivery, up to 10% of the value cut.
11.3 A revision is binding only when the supplier receives it from Ledgerline. Phone calls and emails from staff are not revisions.

**Clause 12. Price.** The rate on the order holds for the whole order, including revisions. A rate change needs a new order.
