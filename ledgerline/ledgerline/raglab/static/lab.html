Step 5 · The golden set

How good is this setting, measured?

Precomputed for every setting on the same 205 answerable golden questions, on one Modal machine (an L4 GPU container with 4 vCPUs). Our targets: recall@5 at least 90%, retrieval p95 at most 200 ms.
