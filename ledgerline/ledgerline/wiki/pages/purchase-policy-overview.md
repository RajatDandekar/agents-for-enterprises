# General Purchasing Policy
This policy sets out how Northbridge Cycles buys materials, components, and services. It ensures every rupee is spent with proper approval, from a qualified supplier, and against an open budget. All rules apply starting 2026-04-01 across applicable company units through Ledgerline ERP.

## Rules
- Buy all materials, components, and services with proper approval, from a qualified supplier, and against an open budget [POL-PUR-001 §1].
- Apply this policy to every purchase made for the Manchester plant (NBC-MAN-01) and the Leeds unit (NBC-LDS-01) through Ledgerline ERP [POL-PUR-001 §2].
- Use the rate specified in the current rate contract [POL-PUR-001 §7.1].
- Record the manager's written reason in the remarks for any order placed at a rate more than 2% above the contract rate [POL-PUR-001 §7.2].
- Ensure every order carries its Ledgerline-assigned document number and reaches Released status before instructing the supplier to deliver [POL-PUR-001 §9.1].

## Watch out
- Do not use rates from older orders in the order history as binding prices; they are for reference only [POL-PUR-001 §7.1].
- Do not tell a requester that an order has been placed until its status in Ledgerline is Released [POL-PUR-001 §9.2].
