# Tax Codes and Payment Terms

This page details the VAT tax codes and supplier payment terms used in Ledgerline ERP. It guides purchase executives on selecting the correct tax codes for UK and overseas suppliers. It also defines the standard codes for invoice-based and advance payments.

## Rules

- Effective from 2026-04-01, suppliers in the UK are charged UK VAT using tax codes ending in -UK, while suppliers in any other country are charged VAT by reverse charge using tax codes ending in -RC [POL-PUR-001 §8.1].
- Use tax code VAT20-UK (VAT 20%) when a supplier is in the same country as the organisation unit [MAN-DD-72 §7.1].
- Use tax code VAT20-RC (VAT 20%, reverse charge) when a supplier is in another country [MAN-DD-72 §7.2].
- Use tax codes VAT5-UK and VAT5-RC for the same two rules at the reduced 5% rate [MAN-DD-72 §7.3].
- Use payment term codes N30, N45, or N60 for net payment at 30, 45, or 60 days from the invoice [MAN-DD-72 §8.1].
- Use payment term code ADV for advance payments [MAN-DD-72 §8.1].
