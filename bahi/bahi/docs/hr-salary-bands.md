# Salary Bands 2026-27 (HR-CONF-007), confidential

## 1. Use of this page
1.1 This page is confidential to the HR department. It must not be shared with employees, managers outside HR, or any system that serves other staff.

## 2. Bands for the purchase department
2.1 Annual fixed pay, in rupees, for the purchase department's roles:

| Role | Band | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase executive (PUR-EXEC) | M2 | ₹6,40,000 | ₹8,90,000 |
| Senior buyer (PUR-SR) | M3 | ₹9,20,000 | ₹12,60,000 |
| Purchase manager (PUR-MGR) | M4 | ₹14,50,000 | ₹19,80,000 |
| Plant head (PLANT-HEAD) | L1 | ₹38,00,000 | ₹52,00,000 |

2.2 Variable pay for the purchase department is up to 12% of fixed pay, paid in May on the year's savings against rate contracts.

## 3. Increments
3.1 The 2026-27 increment pool is 9.5% of fixed pay. Increments above 14% for one person need the plant head's approval.
