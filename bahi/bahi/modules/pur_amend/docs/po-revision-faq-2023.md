# PO Revision FAQ (2023, superseded)

**Written for Bahi 6.x. Where it disagrees with the PO Amendment Policy (POL-PUR-005), the policy wins.**

Q. Who approves a change to a released order?
A. The plant head, always. (Now: the original approver, and only when the cut is over 20% or the date moves over 5 days.)

Q. Can I reduce an order below what has been received?
A. Yes, Bahi adjusts the receipt. (No longer possible: Bahi 7.2 refuses it with ERR-PA-0310; short-close instead.)

Q. Do I need to change the delivery schedule when I change the quantity?
A. No, Bahi spreads the new quantity over the old lines. (No longer true: you must enter the new lines, or the amendment is held.)

Q. Can I phone the supplier with the new date?
A. Yes, and amend the order later. (Not allowed: the supplier is told only by the released revision.)
