# Leave Policy (POL-HR-011)

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out the leave every employee of Rasoi Steel Works may take, how to apply for it in Bahi, and who approves it.

## 2. Scope
It applies to all permanent employees of the Pune plant (RSW-PUN-01) and the Chakan unit (RSW-CHK-01). Contract workers follow their contractor's rules.

## 3. Kinds of leave
3.1 **Casual leave (CL):** 8 days a calendar year, for short personal needs. At most 3 days at a stretch. No advance notice is needed.
3.2 **Earned leave (EL):** 15 days a year (30 for staff with more than 20 years' service). Apply at least **3 calendar days** before the first day of leave.
3.3 **Sick leave (SL):** 8 days a year. May be applied after the event. More than 2 days needs a doctor's certificate given to HR.
3.4 **Compensatory off (CO):** one day off for each plant holiday worked, under the Comp-off Rules (HR-SOP-014).
3.5 **Leave without pay (LWP):** only when no paid leave is left, and only with HR's agreement (§6).

## 4. Balances
4.1 Your balance is shown on screen HR-LV-01. Balance = opening − availed − pending.
4.2 Days that are applied for but not yet approved count as pending and cannot be used twice.
4.3 Sundays and plant holidays inside a leave period are not counted as leave (see the Holiday Calendar, HR-CAL-2026).
4.4 Choose the leave type yourself. **Bahi does not switch you to another type when one runs out.**

## 5. Applying and approval
5.1 Apply on screen HR-LV-02 and send it to your leave approver (normally your reporting manager).
5.2 **The Diwali production run, 2 to 14 November 2026.** Production and stores staff may not take earned leave or casual leave in this period without the **plant head's** approval. Send such applications to the plant head (FWD TO: APATIL). Bahi does not check this; the approver must.
5.3 Nobody may approve their own leave.
5.4 Leave is taken only after it is approved. An application that is pending or held is not approval.

## 6. Leave without pay
6.1 If a paid leave type does not have enough balance, Bahi saves the extra days as **leave without pay** and holds the application for HR (status HD). It does not reach your manager until HR clears it.
6.2 Unpaid days are deducted from the next salary. An employee must be told, before applying, if any day will be unpaid.
6.3 To avoid unpaid days, use another paid leave type that has a balance, or split the leave across two applications.

## 7. Cancelling
7.1 A pending, held or approved application can be cancelled from HR-LV-02 before the leave starts. Cancelling returns the days to the balance.
