# Leave FAQ (2024, superseded)

**This FAQ was written for the 2024 rules. Where it disagrees with the Leave Policy (POL-HR-011), the policy wins.**

Q. How much notice do I need for earned leave?
A. Seven days. (Changed to 3 days from 1 January 2026.)

Q. Can I carry casual leave into next year?
A. No. Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December.

Q. What happens if my casual leave runs out?
A. Bahi will automatically use your earned leave. (No longer true in Bahi 7.2: the extra days become leave without pay.)

Q. Who approves leave in stores?
A. The stores in-charge. (Stores now reports to the purchase manager.)
