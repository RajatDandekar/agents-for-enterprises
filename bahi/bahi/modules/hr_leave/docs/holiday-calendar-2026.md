# Plant Holiday Calendar 2026 (HR-CAL-2026)

The plant works Monday to Saturday. Sunday is the weekly off. These are the paid holidays for both units:

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| 26 Jan | Mon | Republic Day |
| 19 Mar | Thu | Gudi Padwa |
| 1 May | Fri | Maharashtra Day |
| 15 Aug | Sat | Independence Day |
| 14 Sep | Mon | Ganesh Chaturthi |
| 2 Oct | Fri | Gandhi Jayanti |
| 20 Oct | Tue | Dussehra |
| 10 Nov | Tue | Diwali (Bali Pratipada) |
| 11 Nov | Wed | Bhau Beej |

Notes
1. A holiday inside a leave period is not counted as leave.
2. Staff asked to work on a holiday earn one compensatory off (see HR-SOP-014).
3. Lakshmi Puja falls on Sunday 8 November this year, so it is not a separate holiday.
