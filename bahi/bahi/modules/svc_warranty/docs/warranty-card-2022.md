# Customer Warranty Card (2022 print, superseded)

**This is the text of the old card still found in some boxes. Where it disagrees with the Warranty Policy (POL-SVC-021), the policy wins.**

- Your cooker is guaranteed for 5 years from the date of manufacture. (Now: from the date of sale.)
- Whistle and gasket are guaranteed for 12 months. (Now: 6 months.)
- Any part will be replaced free within the guarantee period, whatever the cause. (Now: exclusions apply, see POL-SVC-022.)
- Bring the card stamped by the dealer to any authorised dealer.
