# Delegation of Authority: Contracts (POL-DOA-002)

## 1. Who signs what

| Contract | Authorised signatory | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Non-disclosure agreement (NDA), any value | **Plant head (APATIL) only** | Confidential information is at stake whatever the value |
| Service agreement up to ₹5,00,000 a year | Purchase manager (RMEHTA) | |
| Service agreement above ₹5,00,000 a year | Plant head (APATIL) | |
| Master supply agreement, any value | Plant head (APATIL) | Above ₹50,00,000 also needs Finance (VNAIR) |

## 2. Rules
2.1 The signatory must be the person named above, not "the next manager up".
2.2 **Bahi routes signatures by value**, through the same approval engine as purchase orders. For an NDA, which has no value, that engine stops at the requester's own manager. Bahi does not know rule 1. When you raise an NDA, send it to the plant head yourself (FWD TO: APATIL).
2.3 The integration service account (SVC_AGENT) never signs or clears anything.
2.4 Nobody signs a contract they raised.
