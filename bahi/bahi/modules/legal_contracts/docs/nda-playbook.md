# NDA Playbook (LEG-PB-004)

The playbook is what Legal accepts without discussion. Bahi compares every request against it (clause scan) and records each difference as a deviation on LEG-REV-02.

| Clause | Our position | Bahi check |
|---|---|---|
| NDA-PAPER | Our template NDA-MUT-v4 (mutual) or NDA-ONE-v2 (one-way, we disclose) | paper = OURS |
| NDA-TERM | Up to 36 months; 24 months is normal for a supplier evaluation | term ≤ 36 |
| NDA-JURIS | Courts at Pune | juris = PUNE |

## Choosing the template
1. Both sides will share drawings, prices or process details: **NDA-MUT-v4 (mutual)**. This is the normal case with a new supplier.
2. Only we share (for example, we send a drawing for a quotation): NDA-ONE-v2.
3. NDA-MUT-v3 was withdrawn on 31 March 2025 and Bahi refuses it (ERR-CT-0212).

## When the other side sends their own NDA
1. Raise it as THEIRS with their document reference. It goes to Legal (Rekha Menon) for review.
2. Typical problems: a term of five years or more, courts abroad or in another city, a one-way NDA that protects only them, a clause that stops us hiring their staff.
3. Legal clears it, returns it, or asks them to sign ours instead. Tell the requester which deviations Legal must look at.
