# Note on NDA templates (2023, superseded)

**This note describes the 2023 practice. Where it disagrees with the NDA Playbook (LEG-PB-004) or the Delegation of Authority (POL-DOA-002), those win.**

1. Use template NDA-MUT-v3 for every supplier. (Withdrawn 31 March 2025; use NDA-MUT-v4.)
2. The standard NDA term is five years. (Now at most three years; two is normal.)
3. Any manager may sign an NDA. (Now only the plant head.)
4. Register the party in the supplier master first so Bahi can find it. (No longer: contracts have their own party register.)
