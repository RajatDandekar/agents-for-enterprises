# GST on pressure cookers (TAX-NOTE-07)

1. In the Bahi sandbox, all Rasoi pressure cookers carry GST at **12%** (tax codes GST12-*).
2. The place of supply is the customer's state:
   - customer in **Maharashtra** (same state as the Pune plant): **GST12-INTRA** (CGST 6% + SGST 6%);
   - customer in **any other state**: **GST12-INTER** (IGST 12%).
3. The quotation screen's Price button always offers GST12-INTRA. For a customer outside Maharashtra, change it; otherwise Bahi refuses with ERR-SO-0412.
4. Example: 500 × ₹1,450 = ₹7,25,000 basic; GST 12% = ₹87,000; gross ₹8,12,000.
