# Payment Run Calendar (POL-FIN-022)

Suppliers are paid once a week, by bank transfer, in the **Friday payment run**. The cut-off for proposing an invoice for a run is **Thursday, 6 PM**, the day before.

| Run (Friday) | Cut-off (Thursday) |
|---|---|
| 23 Oct 2026 | 22 Oct (done) |
| 30 Oct 2026 | 29 Oct |
| 6 Nov 2026 | 5 Nov |
| 13 Nov 2026 | 12 Nov |
| 20 Nov 2026 | 19 Nov |
| 27 Nov 2026 | 26 Nov |

Notes
1. A payment proposal must name a run date from this table (FIN-AP-05, PAY DT). Any other date is refused (ERR-AP-0412).
2. A proposal is in the run only when its status is SC (scheduled). PK (parked) and PA (waiting for approval) are not paid.
3. After the run, Bahi mails a remittance advice to the supplier.
