# Travel Expense Claim Procedure (FIN-SOP-031)

1. Claim on screen TRV-CLM-03 against the trip number, after you are back.
2. Claim within **30 days** of the return date. Bahi refuses later claims (ERR-TV-0703); they are not paid.
3. One claim per trip. If you forgot something, ask finance to return the claim; do not raise a second one.
4. Enter the hotel amount and the number of nights, local travel, and whether bills are attached (Y/N). Hand the bills to finance within 7 days.
5. Bahi works out what is admissible: hotel up to the cap (POL-ADM-022), local travel without bills up to ₹300 a day, and the daily allowance. **Tell the traveller what will not be paid before submitting.**
6. Finance (the finance controller) settles the claim. Settlement deducts any advance and posts the balance to the employee's account, or recovers it from salary if the advance was larger.
7. Nobody settles their own claim. The integration service account never settles claims.
