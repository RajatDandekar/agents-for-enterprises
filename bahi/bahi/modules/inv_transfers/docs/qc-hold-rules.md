# QC Hold Rules (QA-SOP-009)

1. When a lot fails or awaits an inspection, Quality puts it on **QC hold** in Bahi with a lot number and a reason.
2. **QC-held stock never moves**: not to the line, not to the other unit, not to a customer. It stays in its rack with a red tag until Quality releases it.
3. QC hold is counted in "on hand" but not in "free" (screen INV-STK-07).
4. Current holds (26 Oct 2026):
   - GSK-SIL-05L at Chakan, lot **QC-2610, 4,000 pieces**, hardness out of spec, lab retest due 29 Oct.
   - HDL-BKL-01 at Pune main stores, lot QC-2602, 1,500 pieces, flash on moulding.
5. Only Quality may release a hold. Stores and production may not ask for a "partial release" on the phone.
6. Food-contact parts (gaskets, lids) that fail a hardness or odour test are never reworked for food use without a fresh test.
