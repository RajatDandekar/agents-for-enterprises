What an ERP is, using Rasoi.

1

One database for the whole company.

ERP means enterprise resource planning. Every department reads and writes the same tables. When stores receives gaskets, purchasing sees the order close and finance sees what to pay. Nobody keeps their own spreadsheet of the truth.

2

Master data: what rarely changes.

Set up once, used by every transaction.

3

Transactions: what happens every day.

Each one is a document with a number and a status.

Statuses: DR draft · PA waiting for approval · AP approved · RL released · CL closed

7

How to drive a 1998 screen.

  • The menu is on the left. Each screen does one job and has a code, like PUR-PO-01.
  • F2 next to a field opens a lookup list. Fetch fills defaults. Save gives a number. F1 shows the screen's help.
  • The status bar at the bottom is the only place Bahi talks to you, and it speaks in codes like ERR-PO-0412.
  • Dates are DD-MM-YYYY. Bahi's date is 26-10-2026.
