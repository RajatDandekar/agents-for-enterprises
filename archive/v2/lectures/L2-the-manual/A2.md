In Lecture 2 we gave Kavita's agent job 4 of the form: explaining the rules, with the clause attached. Now do the
same for the module you picked in A1 (purchasing amendments, payables, leave, sales orders, warranty, transfers,
travel claims or contracts).

The rule from the lecture holds for your module too: **documents explain rules; the ERP holds facts.** Your
retrieval answers "may we", "who approves", "what does this error mean". It never answers "how many", "how much is
left" or "what is the status of".

## What to build

1. **A small corpus for your module** (in `modules/<your_module>/docs/`), every file with front-matter:
   `doc_id`, `title`, `version`, `effective`, `audience`, and `amends` on amendments. At least:
   - one policy with numbered clauses (10 or more clauses);
   - one section of a user manual for your module's screens, with at least three error codes;
   - **one amendment** that changes an earlier clause, with an effective date (either already in force or in the
     future; both make good tests);
   - **one restricted page** that ordinary users of your module must never see (for example HR's pay page, Finance's
     credit limits, Legal's negotiating fallback).
   Keep it consistent with the Bahi seed data and the story canon. Everything fictional.
2. **Retrieval** over that corpus: section chunks with the heading attached, hybrid search (BM25 + embeddings +
   reranker), permission filtering before ranking, freshness by effective date.
3. **One tool**: `search_<module>_policy(question)`, returning clauses with citations or a plain "not in the
   documents". Plug it into your agent next to your module's tools with `ToolBelt`.
4. **A 20-question test set** with gold answers and gold clause ids, graded by code:
   | Kind | How many | What it tests |
   |---|---|---|
   | rule | 10 | the answer and the right clause |
   | manual | 3 | an error code or a screen step |
   | freshness | 2 | the amended rule wins (or the future one is dated) |
   | permission | 2 | a user without clearance gets nothing from the restricted page |
   | trap | 3 | a live fact that lives in the ERP; the answer must be NOT IN DOCUMENTS |
5. **A run**: answer all 20 with your RAG and report correct, gold clause cited, invented citations, leaks, and
   tokens per answer. If you also try the Wiki, report both.

## Two tracks

**Guided track (configuration).** Write the documents and `questions.py`. Use the library unchanged:

```python
from bahi.agent import retrieval as R
docs = R.load_corpus("modules/hr_leave/docs")
ret  = R.Retriever("KJOSHI", docs=docs)                 # or clearance={"all-staff", "hr"} for an HR user
tool = R.PolicySearch("KJOSHI", retriever=ret)
tools = R.ToolBelt(my_module_tools, tool)               # schemas() / call(name, args)
```

If your restricted page uses a new audience, add it in your notebook: `R.AUDIENCE["finance only"] = "finance"`
and give the right role the clearance in `R.ROLE_CLEARANCE`.

**Builder track (Python).** Do everything in the guided track, then change at least one part of the pipeline and
measure the change on your 20 questions: a different chunker, a different embedding model, a reranker threshold, a
query rewrite step, or your own wiki compile. Report before and after.
