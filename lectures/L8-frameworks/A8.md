# A8 · A framework decision for your module

**Lecture 8 · Agent Frameworks in Industry** · due before Lecture 9 (Sat 28 Nov 2026, 7 AM IST)

In A5 you put a harness around your module's toolbox. Your module's CTO now asks the question Anand asked in class: *should this run on a framework, which one, and what do we lose?* In A8 you answer with your own numbers.

## What to build

1. **Serve your toolbox over MCP.** `FolderToolbox("<your folder>/tools", ERP(modules=["<your module>"]), "<speaker>")` from `bahi.course.l5.kit`. Every port in `bahi.course.frameworks` accepts it and serves it with `serve_toolbox(..., toolbox=tb)`, so the frameworks see your tools, not Bahi's.
2. **Port your agent to two frameworks** from: OpenAI Agents SDK (`AgentsSDKPort`), Google ADK (`ADKPort`), LangGraph's prebuilt agent (`LangGraphPort`). Same model, same system prompt (your A5 prompt), same step cap. Copy the `# --- port ---` section of the framework's file into your notebook and adapt it; do not import ours blindly.
3. **Put the meter in front of every model call.** `Meter(cassette="A8_<you>").start()`; give `meter.base_url(tag)` to each framework, and use `harness_port(tb, cassette="A8_<you>_ours")` for your own harness. One way of counting for all three.
4. **Run your module's scenarios** (your A5 S1–S3 and the kit's four, as `Task`/`Beat` scripts) on your harness and both ports, **twice each** (use a second cassette name for run 2). Pass and never-events are checked in code on your ERP tables, never on the reply.
5. **Move one rule of your module into a framework guardrail** (a tool guardrail on the Agents SDK, a `before_tool_callback` on ADK, or a graph node on LangGraph). Show one scenario where it blocks, and run it again with the rule in your harness, so both versions are tested on the same case.
6. **Write the memo.** One page, for your module's CTO.

## The memo (one page, `A8_memo.md`)
- **The recommendation** in two sentences: which framework (or none) for which part of the job.
- **The table:** for each of the three implementations, runs 1 and 2: passed, never-event scenarios, model calls, input tokens, ₹ billed, model seconds, lines of code (`experiments/count_loc.py` shows how we counted).
- **What stays your code** whichever framework you pick: the rules, the pause for an approver, the read-back.
- **One surprise** you measured (ours: ADK sent about 1.7× the input tokens; our verify guardrail fired on previews).
- **What you did not measure** and would need before going live.

Every number in the memo must be in one of your logs. A number you could not measure is written as "not measured".

## What to submit
- `A8_<module>.ipynb`: the ports, the runs and the table. It runs top to bottom in replay with no key: record once with your OpenRouter key, then commit the `A8_*.jsonl` cassettes.
- `A8_memo.md`
- `STACK.md` started for demo day: the framework you chose, its pinned version, and your 150-word reason (rubric line 7).
