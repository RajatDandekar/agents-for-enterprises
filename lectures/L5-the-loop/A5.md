# A5 · The harness for your module

**Lecture 5 · The Loop: What Makes It an Agent** · due before Lecture 6 (Sun 15 Nov 2026, 7 AM IST)

In A4 you built your module's toolbox: one folder per business activity, each a `tool.json` and a `tool.py`. In A5 you put a harness around it, so one sentence from your module's speaker gets the whole job done, across turns and, where someone has to approve, across a pause.

## What to build

1. **Load your toolbox.** `FolderToolbox("<your folder>", ERP(modules=["<your module>"]), "<speaker>")` from `bahi.course.l5.kit`. It behaves like the Bahi toolbox, so every agent from NB5 runs on it unchanged.
2. **Write your system prompt** (the harness's first part): who the speaker is, today's date, their limits, and the rules of *your* module's job. Plain sentences. Look at `SYSTEM_FULL` in `bahi/course/l5/harness.py` for the shape.
3. **Run your module's job with the harness**: `Harness(tb, LLM(MODEL, cassette="A5_<you>"), system=YOUR_PROMPT)`.
4. **Three scenarios**, each a short script of messages (copy the `Task`/`Beat` shape from `bahi/course/l5/tasks.py`, or write plain Python):
   - **S1, the happy path**: the module's "after" sentence (for example, *"I need Thursday and Friday off for my sister's wedding."*), done to the end.
   - **S2, the silent outcome**: your module's trap (the document that saves but sits stuck). Pass = the agent never calls it done, and it either fixes it or tells the truth.
   - **S3, a pause**: a step that waits for a person (an approver, a second department). Save the harness with `save()`, load it into a new object, deliver the person's decision as a message, and show the harness checks your ERP before it resumes.
5. **A pass/fail table**: for each scenario × {bare loop `parts=()`, full harness}, record pass/fail (checked in code against the ERP tables, not the reply), model calls, tokens, and which failure modes you saw (loop, false "done", skipped step, duplicate request). At least one row should show a harness part preventing something the bare loop did.

## What to submit
- `A5_<module>.ipynb` (runs top to bottom in replay mode: record once with your key, then commit the `A5_<you>.jsonl` cassette).
- The pass/fail table (in the notebook, printed as a DataFrame).
- Three sentences: which harness part mattered most for your module, and why.
