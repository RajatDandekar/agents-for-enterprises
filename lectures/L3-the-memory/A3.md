have not read.

## What to build

1. **Load and chunk** your kit's documents with `R.load_corpus(path)` and `R.chunk_by_section`. Print the chunk count
   and one chunk's `render()`. If a document does not chunk cleanly (a clause swallowed by its neighbour), fix the
   heading or numbering in a copy, not the kit.
2. **Who may read what.** Your kit's audiences ("accounts payable, purchase", "service desk, dealers", ...) are not in
   purchasing's `AUDIENCE` map, so today they all come out as `restricted`. Write `audience → clearance tag` for your
   documents and `role → clearances` for your kit's users, and build the index with `permissions=True` and your
   clearance. Show one user who can and one who cannot retrieve a restricted clause.
3. **Freshness.** Find your kit's stale or amended document. Show, with `as_of` dates, which clause answers before and
   after the change.
4. **Search.** Hybrid search (BM25 + embeddings, RRF) and the reranker, through `l3.retriever(...)` or
   `R.Retriever(...)`. Keep the citation id (`DOC-ID §n.n`).
5. **One tool**, `search_<kit>_policy(question)`: returns clauses with citations, or `answer_in_documents: false`.
   Its description must say what it does **not** know (live facts live in Bahi, not in documents).
6. **Twelve questions graded by code**, in the format of `bahi/course/l3/questions.py`:
   6 rule questions, 2 that need the error-code or screen part of the manual, 1 freshness, 1 permission,
   2 traps (a live fact such as a balance or a status that no document holds). Each with `gold` clause ids and `must`
   / `must_not` regexes. No LLM judge.
7. **Measure** recall@1 and recall@5 of the gold clause for BM25, embeddings, hybrid and hybrid + rerank, and answer
   accuracy for RAG against the model alone. Report tokens in per answer and ₹ for the twelve.

Start from NB3 §11, which already builds a retriever over `fin_payables`.

## Two tracks

- **Guided (configuration).** Use `bahi.agent.retrieval` unchanged. Your work is the audience map, the twelve questions
  and the measurements.
- **Builder (Python).** Do the guided track, then change one part of the pipeline and measure before and after on your
  twelve: the chunker, the embedding model, the reranker cut-off `TAU`, query rewriting with your module's one line of
  context (like Rasoi's "many of the parts it buys touch food"), or an LLM wiki for your kit.
