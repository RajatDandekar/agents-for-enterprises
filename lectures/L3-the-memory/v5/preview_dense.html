RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Search first, then let the model read only what the search found. Three words, three steps.

Retrieval

Search the documents for the few passages that answer the question.

Emma, on D: the 5 of D’s chunks closest to her question.

Augmented

Put those passages into the prompt, next to the question, each with where it came from.

Her question, then those 5 chunks: tokens.

Generation

The model writes the answer from those passages and cites them, so a person can check.

From chunk #5 to 384 numbers

bge-small-en-v1.5 (rag.EMBED_MODELS["bge-small"]), run on Modal. Every value below is this chunk’s.

  1. WordPiece pieces for chunk #5, from [CLS] to [SEP]. The model never sees letters, only these.
  2. Common words are one piece. Rarer ones split, and ## joins a piece to the one before: ledger ##line, quota ##tion, onboard ##ed.
  3. Codes break into fragments: pol - pu ##r - 001, un 38 . 3. ERR-PO-0412 becomes er ##r - po - 04 ##12. Remember this for the failure cases.
  1. Two arrows and the angle between them. Cosine of the angle: 1 when they point the same way, 0 at a right angle.
  2. The angles drawn are the real ones between Emma’s question and three chunks of D: θ = arccos of their 384-number cosine.
  3. They are close: 47.7° to 62.6°. In this model even unrelated text sits near 60°. What matters is the order, not the size.
