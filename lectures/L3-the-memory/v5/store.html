HNSW: Hierarchical Navigable Small World

Exact search compares the question with all 28,941 vectors. Fine today; slow at ten times the size. HNSW follows links instead.

Small world. Each point links to a few near neighbours, plus a few long links. Any point is then a few hops from any other.

Navigable. A greedy walk: step to whichever neighbour is closer to the question. Stop when none is.

Hierarchical. Sparse upper layers for long jumps, a dense bottom layer for the fine search: motorways, then local roads.

A toy graph of 42 points, searched by the real greedy rule. Chroma’s graph for the dump has layers; our Qdrant collection links each point to others.

One search, hop by hop, on ten points

A toy, not our data: points A to J and the question (the diamond). Distance here is straight-line on the picture; a real store uses cosine distance on 384 numbers.

Two settings you will meet

m: links per point. More links, a better graph, more RAM. Qdrant’s default, on our collection: .

ef: how many candidates the walk keeps as it searches. Larger: closer to exact, and slower.

It can miss: the walk stops where no neighbour is closer, even if a closer vector sits elsewhere.

3The indexes: made before the upload

A payload index on every field you filter on (confidential, superseded, folder…). The HR filter then cost nothing measurable: ms without it, ms with it.

Your capstone: six steps, in this order

Everything so far, as the checklist for retrieval over your own documents.

1Questions before documentsCollect 20 to 40 real questions. Write each true answer and where it lives: your golden set. Set targets, latency included.
2Parse by document typeA structured parser (Docling) for PDFs, OCR or a vision model for scans, one record per spreadsheet row. Never one reader for all.
3Chunk by structure, smallAbout 200 tokens, split at headings, the document’s title and version on every chunk.
4Start with BM25 and a rerankerOur winner: recall@5 0.893, search p95 48 ms. Add dense search if people paraphrase; keep BM25 wherever there are codes.
5Store it where it runsChroma on a laptop; Qdrant or pgvector in production, in the app’s region (93.9 → 14.0 ms). Filter permissions and old versions before ranking.
6Measure every changerecall@5, answer correct, citations, p95, cost, on the golden set, every time. Try a wiki only when answers need many documents and they change slowly.
