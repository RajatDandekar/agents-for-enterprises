# A1 · Your Before, your After

**AI Agents for Enterprises · Lecture 1 · Due before Lecture 3 (Sat 7 Nov 2026, 7 AM IST)**

In Lecture 1 you played Rasoi's procure-to-pay twice: by hand on Bahi ERP 7.2's legacy screens, and in one sentence each with Ask Rasoi. In this assignment you do two things: record your own Before and After for purchasing, and choose the module you will make AI-native over the rest of the course. Then map that module's Before, and write down the After you want.

Everything is fictional: Rasoi, Bahi, the people and the data. Do not bring in anything from a real employer.

## Part 3 · Map your module's Before

Write `my_module/BEFORE.md` with:

1. **The job:** who does it, how often, and what document it creates or changes.
2. **The route:** every screen in order (code and title), and who sits at each one. Count the screens, fields typed, codes typed and sign-ins. For your counts, use NB1 exercise 6 (the kit's `happy_path`), or the Desk driver.
3. **The traps:** every trap in the kit's `TRAPS`, with the exact words Bahi shows (error code or status bar text) and what the person has to know to avoid it.
4. **The pain points:** sort each trap and each step into Lecture 1's six kinds:
   - (1) codes;
   - (2) the route;
   - (3) silent outcomes;
   - (4) errors that do not help;
   - (5) hand-offs between desks;
   - (6) rules that live in heads.

   A table is fine.
5. **The silent outcome:** your kit has one, a document that looks saved but is stuck. Describe how the person would find out, and how late.

## Part 4 · Write the After you want

Write `my_module/AFTER.md` with:

1. **Five sentences** people in your module would say to an assistant, the first one being the kit's own sentence. At least one must be a question that no screen answers, like the Shakti Seals one.
2. **For each sentence:** what the assistant must check first, what it must show before saving (the preview), who must say yes, and what proof it gives back (the receipt).
3. **Three never-events:** things that must never happen however fast it gets (for example "a leave saved as unpaid without telling Kavita").
4. **One-tool-per-activity sketch:** list the tools your module needs, with a name and one line each (`apply_leave`: …). No code yet; Lecture 4 builds them.
